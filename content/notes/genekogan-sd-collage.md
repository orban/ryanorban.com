## Summary

Gene Kogan — artist, educator, and ML researcher — shares a work-in-progress collage tool built on Stable Diffusion. The demo shows a hybrid workflow: traditional collage composition (cutting and arranging image fragments) combined with Stable Diffusion for generating and blending the constituent pieces. The result bridges analog collage sensibility with AI-generated content.

Gene Kogan has been at the intersection of machine learning and creative practice for years — his educational work includes ml4a (machine learning for artists), which predates the Stable Diffusion era by several years. The WIP status is significant: in August 2022, Stable Diffusion had just been released and practitioners were immediately building creative tools on top of it. The speed from model release to application exploration was characteristic of the open-source ML ecosystem.

The collage tool concept is interesting because it preserves intentionality in composition — the artist chooses arrangement, while AI handles generation and blending. This is a form of human-AI collaboration in creative work: AI for what it's good at (generating plausible images), human judgment for composition decisions. A design philosophy that would be articulated more explicitly in later creative AI tools.
