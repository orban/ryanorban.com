## Summary

[My Younger Self](/notes/my-younger-self/) is a platform collecting short-form career advice stories from experienced professionals — the specific framing is what they wish they had known at an earlier stage. The interview format and letter to your younger self structure produces a distinct type of advice: more honest about failures, more focused on mindset shifts than tactical tips, and more personal than typical career guides.

The platform organizes content by career stage and domain, making it navigable for someone at a specific inflection point — early career, career change, starting a company, managing for the first time. The common themes that surface across interviews: the value of starting earlier (writing, investing, building in public), the importance of finding the right environment rather than optimizing within a bad one, and the underestimated role of luck and network effects in career outcomes.

The format is useful because advice is most actionable when it comes with the failure context that produced it. Generic career guides suffer from survivorship bias and abstract framing; first-person what I wish I knew stories include the specific mistake and what it cost, which makes the lesson concrete.

## Key points

- Interview-format career advice platform: what experienced professionals wish they'd known earlier.
- Organized by career stage and domain — navigable for specific inflection points.
- Format advantage: failure context makes advice concrete, counters survivorship bias of typical success stories.
- Common themes: start earlier, find the right environment, network effects matter more than optimization.
- Structured mentorship-at-scale — broader coverage than personal mentors but more personal than career books.
