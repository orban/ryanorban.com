## Summary

[Fontjoy](/notes/fontjoy/) is a browser tool that uses deep learning to generate and evaluate font pairings — combinations of fonts for headings, body text, and accents that look cohesive. The interface is a one-click generator: press a button, get a pairing; press again for another. Users can lock specific fonts and regenerate to find combinations around a preference.

The underlying model learns aesthetic coherence in typography from training data: which combinations of Google Fonts tend to look good together. This approximates the taste of a designer who can assess contrast, similarity, and hierarchy in font combinations. The output is a practical shortcut for non-designers who need functional typography without hiring someone.

[Fontjoy](/notes/fontjoy/) represents a class of generative AI tools that operate in aesthetic judgment domains — design, typography, color palettes — where the right answer is subjective but learnable from examples. Before Stable Diffusion and LLM-powered design tools, focused tools like [Fontjoy](/notes/fontjoy/) and [Brandmark](/notes/brandmark/) demonstrated that specific aesthetic judgment tasks could be automated with targeted deep learning models.

## Key points

- Generates font pairings using deep learning over Google Fonts combinations.
- One-click interface: regenerate pairings, lock individual fonts, explore variations.
- Approximates designer aesthetic judgment for typography — useful for non-designers.
- Works across heading + body + accent font slots — the standard typography hierarchy.
- Aesthetic judgment AI applied to a specific, bounded domain.
- Predates diffusion-model design tools; an early example of focused aesthetic ML.
