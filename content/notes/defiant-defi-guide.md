## Summary

The Defiant is one of the most respected DeFi-focused media publications, and this guide serves as their comprehensive introduction to decentralized finance. It covers the core primitive categories: DEXes (Uniswap, Curve), lending protocols (Aave, Compound, MakerDAO), yield aggregators (Yearn Finance), stablecoins (algorithmic vs. collateralized), and liquidity mining / yield farming.

What distinguishes this from generic crypto explainers is the interplay focus — DeFi's power comes from composability, where protocols can be stacked on top of each other (deposit to Aave, borrow against it to farm yield elsewhere, use that yield to buy more collateral). The guide explains this "money legos" pattern with concrete examples and discusses the risks: liquidation risk, smart contract risk, oracle manipulation, and the complexity of tracking positions across multiple protocols.

By 2022, DeFi TVL had reached hundreds of billions of dollars at peak, and the need for accessible explainer content was real — most protocol documentation assumed significant prior knowledge. The Defiant's guide sat at the right level of technical depth for someone serious about participating, not just speculating.

## Key points

- AMM mechanics: how Uniswap's constant product formula creates a DEX without an order book.
- Lending protocols: over-collateralized borrowing, liquidations, and why this is useful for leveraged positions.
- Yield farming: earning token incentives by providing liquidity — the risk/reward analysis.
- Composability: DeFi protocols as money legos — stacking protocols creates both opportunity and systemic risk.
- Stablecoins: USDC / USDT (centralized), DAI (over-collateralized), algorithmic stablecoins — and their failure modes.
