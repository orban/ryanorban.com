## Summary

A retweeted image from @GRDecter captioned RIP my portfolio, saved in December 2021 during a significant mid-cycle crypto market correction. After hitting all-time highs in November 2021 (Bitcoin ~$69k, Ethereum ~$4.8k), markets pulled back sharply through December as macro concerns and profit-taking triggered a 30-40% drawdown from peak prices.

This is a timestamp — a bookmark capturing market sentiment at a specific moment. December 2021 was ambiguous: was this a normal bull-market correction before another leg up, or the beginning of the 2022 bear market? Most of the crypto community assumed the former; the latter turned out to be correct as the market continued declining through 2022, eventually losing 70-80% from peak with the Terra/Luna collapse (May 2022) and FTX collapse (November 2022) as the defining catastrophes.

The cultural artifact value: crypto Twitter humor about portfolio losses was a persistent genre throughout the bull run — self-deprecating acknowledgment that volatility was the cost of entry. The same accounts posting we're all gonna make it (WAGMI) in October were posting portfolio loss memes in December.

## Key points

- Saved December 19, 2021: Bitcoin was ~$46k (down from $69k peak); Ethereum ~$3.7k
- What looked like a mid-cycle correction turned out to be the start of the 2022 bear market
- Terra/Luna collapse (May 2022) and FTX collapse (November 2022) were the defining events of the downturn
- Portfolio loss humor was a consistent crypto Twitter cultural genre — coping mechanism for volatility
- Compare with the GMI/WAGMI cultural vocabulary from two months earlier — same accounts, very different tone
