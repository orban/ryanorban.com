## Summary

Interviews.AI is a GitHub-hosted book by BoltzmannEntropy, explicitly written for "aspiring data scientists with a quantitative background" facing the modern ML interview gauntlet. The interview process for machine learning engineering and data science roles at top companies became increasingly rigorous through 2020-2022, requiring candidates to demonstrate both theoretical depth (statistics, probability, linear algebra, ML fundamentals) and practical coding ability.

The book targets the specific problem of quantitative candidates — physicists, mathematicians, engineers — who have the underlying knowledge but haven't framed it in the interview vocabulary that FAANG and top ML companies expect. Interview questions at these companies test Bayesian inference, hypothesis testing, probability distributions, gradient descent variants, regularization, cross-validation, and system design for ML systems, plus Python and SQL coding challenges.

The GitHub repository format makes it freely accessible and community-updatable, which was an advantage over paid platforms like Leetcode Premium or Interview Kickstart. The BoltzmannEntropy naming is a physics reference (the Boltzmann entropy formula S = k ln Ω) — appropriate for a book targeting physics/math backgrounds transitioning into ML.

## Key points

- Targets quantitative backgrounds (physics, math, engineering) preparing for ML/DS interviews at competitive companies
- Covers theoretical foundations: Bayesian inference, probability, statistics, linear algebra — the quantitative layer of data science interviews
- Complements coding prep (LeetCode) with ML-specific theory questions common at Google, Meta, Amazon, Microsoft
- GitHub-hosted = free, forkable, community-improvable
- Part of the 2020-2022 wave of ML interview resources responding to massively increased competition for ML roles
