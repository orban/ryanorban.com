## Summary

KTool is a web service for sending arbitrary web content — articles, blog posts, newsletters — to a Kindle or Kindle app. Amazon's native Send to Kindle functionality exists but requires specific workflows (email forwarding, browser extensions with limited compatibility). KTool provides a cleaner pipeline: browser extension or email forwarding that reliably converts and delivers web content in a format optimized for the Kindle's e-ink display.

The use case is for people who prefer reading long-form content on a Kindle rather than a phone or computer screen. E-ink displays have different reading characteristics — no blue light, lower eye strain for extended reading, battery life measured in weeks, no notifications. For research articles, long essays, newsletters, and documentation, the Kindle reading experience can be substantially better than browser reading.

KTool occupies the same space as Instapaper, Pocket, and Readwise Reader but with a different output: rather than a read-later app or a unified reading interface, KTool delivers directly to the Kindle hardware. This makes it a complement to those services rather than a replacement — the workflow is: save for later via Instapaper, send to Kindle via KTool for deep reading sessions. The service is simple but fills a gap that Amazon hasn't prioritized.

## Key points

- Sends web articles, blog posts, and newsletters to Kindle devices and apps.
- Cleaner pipeline than Amazon's native Send to Kindle — browser extension + email forwarding.
- Optimized formatting for Kindle's e-ink display; better reading experience for long-form content.
- Complements read-later apps (Instapaper, Pocket) by delivering to Kindle hardware.
- Small, single-purpose tool addressing a real gap that Amazon hasn't filled well.
