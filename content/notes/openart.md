## Summary

[OpenArt](/notes/openart/) is a community platform for discovering and sharing AI-generated images, launched in 2022 during the early explosion of text-to-image tools. It functions as both a gallery and a prompt database — each image on the platform shows the prompt that generated it, making it a practical learning resource for prompt engineering.

The platform initially focused on DALL-E 2 images, when DALL-E 2 was still invite-only and prompts were being treated as valuable intellectual property. Sites like [OpenArt](/notes/openart/), [Lexica](/notes/lexica/), and PromptBase emerged to commoditize and share this knowledge, effectively crowd-sourcing the discipline of prompt engineering for image generation.

As Stable Diffusion was released open-source shortly after [OpenArt](/notes/openart/)'s launch, the platform expanded to cover both. The gallery became a reference point for understanding what prompts produce what aesthetic outcomes — a form of empirical knowledge sharing that filled the gap before formal prompt engineering resources existed.

## Key points

- Community gallery showing AI-generated images alongside the prompts that produced them.
- Initially focused on DALL-E 2; expanded to Stable Diffusion after its open-source release.
- One of several platforms ([Lexica](/notes/lexica/), PromptBase) that emerged to share prompt knowledge during invite-only access period.
- Positioned as a prompt engineering learning resource through example-based discovery.
- Launched while DALL-E 2 was invite-only, giving it a first-mover advantage in gallery curation.
- Reflects the early view of prompts as tradeable/shareable artifacts with practical value.
