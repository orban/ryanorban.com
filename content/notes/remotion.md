## Summary

[Remotion](/notes/remotion/) was a virtual coworking platform designed to give distributed teams the always-on presence of a shared office. The core product insight: the most valuable thing about working in the same physical space isn't meetings — it's the ambient awareness of colleagues and the serendipitous collisions that lead to spontaneous conversation, quick questions, and social bonding. Video meeting tools solve scheduled collaboration but miss this ambient layer.

[Remotion](/notes/remotion/) approached this with persistent, low-friction video presence — a lightweight window showing colleagues available for spontaneous conversation, without the formality of joining a Zoom call. The design targeted the hallway conversation pattern that distributed teams consistently report losing. Competitors in this space at the time included Gather.town, Teamflow, and Tandem.

The company later rebranded to Multi, pivoting toward remote pair programming and engineering collaboration specifically — a more focused use case than general virtual coworking. This pivot reflects a common pattern in the remote work tool wave of 2020-2022: broad virtual office products narrowing to specific workflows where the pain was sharpest and willingness to pay was highest.

## Key points

- Virtual coworking platform providing persistent, ambient video presence for distributed teams.
- Targets "hallway conversation" serendipity — the thing video meeting tools don't replicate.
- Low-friction design: lightweight window for spontaneous conversation, not formal meetings.
- Competed with Gather.town, Teamflow, Tandem in the 2022 virtual office space.
- Later rebranded to Multi, pivoting toward remote pair programming and engineering collaboration.
- Reflects the broader remote work tool wave of 2020-2022 and the narrowing to specific use cases.
