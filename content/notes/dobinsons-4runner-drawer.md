## Summary

Dobinsons is an Australian suspension and accessories brand with a strong reputation in the overlanding and 4x4 community. Their rear dual roller drawer system for the Toyota 4Runner 5th gen (2010-2018) is a more substantial build than budget options: 1.6mm zinc-plated steel construction, 12mm heavy-duty plywood side panels with marine carpet, 8 stainless steel tie-down points, and a built-in heavy-duty fridge slide.

The fridge slide is the key differentiator — it lets you store a portable fridge (like ARB or Engel) in the cargo area and pull it out independently without moving the main drawers. For extended trips where cold food and drinks matter, this transforms the system from storage-only to full camp kitchen support. The outer wing trim kit provides a factory-fit appearance.

The installation caveat is significant: requires drilling through the floor to bolt the unit down. This is more structurally sound than systems using existing mounting points only, but it's a permanent modification. Compare with [DFG Offroad 4Runner drawer](/notes/dfg-offroad-4runner-drawer/) (bolts in without floor drilling) and [DIY 4Runner drawer plans](/notes/diy-4runner-drawer-plans/) for a custom build.

## Key points

- 1.6mm zinc-plated steel + marine carpet plywood: heavier duty construction than many competitors
- Built-in fridge slide: independent access to portable fridge without disturbing main drawers
- 8 stainless tie-down points: secure load attachment for off-road use
- Requires floor drilling: permanent installation, more secure but non-reversible modification
- Dobinsons suspension brand trust transfers to accessories; strong reputation in serious overlanding community
