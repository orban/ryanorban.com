## Summary

This Reforge post introduces the AI Survival Curve — a framework for product teams navigating AI disruption. The core question: given that LLM capabilities are rapidly commoditizing, which product categories are disrupted versus which can build defensible AI-native advantages? The framework maps products by how much their value comes from proprietary data, workflow integration, and the cost of user behavior change.

The argument: AI threatens products whose primary value is information retrieval or text generation — if an LLM can do it without your product, you don't have a moat. Products that survive are those that combine AI capabilities with proprietary data (your users' history, your company's specialized corpus), deep workflow integration (the cost of switching is behavioral, not technical), or network effects that AI doesn't eliminate.

The Reforge frame is product strategy, not engineering. The practical advice: don't bolt AI onto your existing product to seem relevant; identify the workflows where your proprietary data or deep integration creates outcomes that a general-purpose AI can't replicate. Find where AI makes your strengths stronger, not where it commoditizes your core value.

## Key points

- "AI Survival Curve": products threatened by commoditized AI vs. those that build on it.
- Threatened: products whose primary value is information retrieval or generic text generation.
- Defensible: proprietary data + workflow integration + behavioral switching costs.
- AI should amplify your existing differentiation, not replace it — don't bolt on features.
- From Reforge (product strategy) — strategic framing, not technical implementation guidance.
