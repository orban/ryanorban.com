## Summary

[TouchDesigner](/notes/touchdesigner/) is a node-based programming environment made by Derivative for creating real-time interactive media, visualizations, and installations. Unlike traditional programming, [TouchDesigner](/notes/touchdesigner/) uses a dataflow model: you wire together nodes (operators) that represent data sources, processing steps, and outputs. Data flows through the graph in real-time, making it natural for live and interactive work where parameters change continuously.

The tool is the industry standard for several creative disciplines: architectural projection mapping (mapping visuals onto building surfaces), live music visualization (synchronizing graphics to audio in real time), interactive installations (where sensor or audience input drives the visual output), and generative art. It handles video, audio, 3D geometry, data streams, and MIDI/OSC simultaneously in a unified graph.

[TouchDesigner](/notes/touchdesigner/) has a unique position straddling art and engineering. It's used by artists and designers who want real-time processing without writing traditional code, but it also exposes full Python scripting for complex logic and custom operators. Many of the most technically ambitious interactive art projects — Meow Wolf installations, stadium visuals for major concerts, museum interactive exhibits — are built in [TouchDesigner](/notes/touchdesigner/).

## Key points

- Node-based dataflow programming model: wire operators together rather than write procedural code.
- Handles video, audio, 3D, data streams, MIDI/OSC simultaneously in real time.
- Industry standard for projection mapping, live music visuals, interactive installations.
- Exposes Python scripting for custom operators and complex logic.
- Community is a mix of artists, designers, and technical programmers — accessible to non-coders but powerful enough for engineers.
- Non-commercial license is free; commercial license starts at ~$600/year.
