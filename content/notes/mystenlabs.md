## Summary

Mysten Labs was founded in 2021 by Evan Cheng, Sam Blackshear, Adeniyi Abiodun, George Danezis, and Kostas Chalkias — all researchers and engineers who had worked on Meta's Diem (formerly Libra) blockchain project before it was abandoned following regulatory pressure. After Meta killed Diem in early 2022, this team took the intellectual work and built Sui, a new Layer 1 blockchain with a novel architecture.

The URL saved here points specifically to the team page (`#Team`), which was significant in December 2021: the founding team's pedigree from Diem and Meta research was the primary reason to pay attention. This was pre-launch — Sui mainnet didn't launch until May 2023 — so in December 2021 Mysten Labs was a well-credentialed team with a research program and a white paper, not yet a deployed network.

Sui's key technical differentiators: object-centric state model (assets are objects owned by addresses, not entries in a global ledger), Move programming language (memory-safe, resource-oriented, designed for assets), and parallel transaction execution for non-conflicting transactions. This last point has throughput implications — most Ethereum-style blockchains execute transactions sequentially; Sui can execute independent transactions simultaneously.

## Key points

- Founded by ex-Meta Diem researchers: deep cryptography and distributed systems credentials
- Sui: object-centric Layer 1 blockchain; assets as typed objects with ownership, not account balance entries
- Move language: designed for asset programming — memory safe, prevents double-spend at the type system level
- Parallel execution: non-conflicting transactions execute simultaneously — throughput advantage for simple transfers
- Saved December 2021 while Diem was still winding down — early recognition of the team's likely next project
