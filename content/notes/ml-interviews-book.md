## Summary

Chip Huyen's ML interviews book is a free, openly available resource for people preparing for machine learning engineering and research roles. Unlike generic interview prep, it's specifically calibrated to what ML hiring loops actually test: mathematical foundations (linear algebra, probability, statistics), coding skills (algorithms, data structures, but also NumPy/pandas proficiency), and ML-specific knowledge (model architectures, training dynamics, evaluation metrics, deployment considerations). Chip draws on her experience recruiting at Snorkel AI and advising at ML startups.

The book is organized into two parts. The first covers the landscape of ML roles and how interviews differ across company types: research labs (DeepMind, OpenAI) test research depth and paper reproduction; product ML roles test applied ML and system design; MLE (ML engineer) roles emphasize production ML systems and data infrastructure. Understanding which type of role you're interviewing for shapes which parts of the book to prioritize. The types of ML roles taxonomy is genuinely useful even for practitioners already working in ML who want to understand what adjacent roles require.

The technical sections cover ground that other interview books skip: information theory (entropy, KL divergence — tested at research-heavy companies), Bayesian statistics, numerical issues in ML (gradient vanishing/exploding, numerical stability), and system design for ML (feature stores, training pipelines, model serving). The ML system design chapter is particularly practical — questions like design a recommendation system or "design spam detection at Gmail scale" are common at senior levels and require reasoning about the full stack.

## Key points

- Free, openly available at huyenchip.com — written by Chip Huyen (Stanford ML, Snorkel AI).
- Covers role types: research vs. applied ML vs. MLE — different loops test different things.
- Math foundations: linear algebra, probability, statistics, information theory — with ML-specific framing.
- ML system design chapter: recommendation systems, fraud detection, content ranking at scale — senior-level questions.
- Practical section on navigating offers, compensation, and company evaluation — rare in technical prep books.
- Complements coding prep (LeetCode) with the ML-specific layer most generic interview books lack.
