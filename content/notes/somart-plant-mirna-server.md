## Summary

SoMART (SOft-criteria based MiRNA and tasiRNA Research Tool) is a web server for computational analysis of plant small RNA — specifically microRNA (miRNA) and trans-acting siRNA (tasiRNA) — and their target genes. Published in The Plant Journal in 2012, the tool addressed a gap in plant RNA research: the need for an integrated platform that could predict small RNA targets using relaxed (soft) matching criteria appropriate for plant biology.

In plants, miRNA-target pairing is more tolerant of mismatches than in animals — plant miRNAs typically bind targets in coding sequences with near-perfect complementarity, but the biology allows some flexibility. SoMART implemented soft criteria for target prediction, allowing users to adjust mismatch tolerances rather than applying rigid rules. The server integrated with public databases of known plant miRNAs and allowed analysis of user-submitted sequences.
