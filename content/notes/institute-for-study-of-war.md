## Summary

The Institute for the Study of War (ISW) is a Washington, D.C.-based nonpartisan research organization focused on military analysis, conflict studies, and geopolitics. Founded in 2007 by Kimberly Kagan, ISW produces rigorous ground-truth analysis of ongoing conflicts, tracking military positions, tactics, and operational developments in detail.

ISW became one of the most cited sources for tracking the Russia-Ukraine war starting in February 2022. Their daily and campaign assessment reports — with annotated maps showing control of territory, reported military engagements, and operational analysis — filled a gap that traditional media couldn't: real-time, detailed military analysis of a complex, fast-moving conventional conflict. Their Russian Offensive Campaign Assessments quickly became required reading for anyone tracking the war's military dimension.

What distinguishes ISW from media coverage is the operational specificity: rather than reporting fighting continues near Kyiv, they would assess which specific towns had changed hands, what the tactical significance was, and what it implied for the broader campaign. Their maps combined reported Ukrainian and Russian sources with satellite imagery analysis and OSINT (open-source intelligence).

The organization was bookmarked during the first weeks of the Russia-Ukraine war, when Russia's rapid advance toward Kyiv was underway and assessment of whether Kyiv would fall was the central analytical question.

## Key points

- ISW Russian Offensive Campaign Assessments became the standard reference for daily military tracking of the Russia-Ukraine war.
- Produces annotated control maps combining multiple intelligence sources — distinguishes claimed vs. confirmed control.
- Nonpartisan and US-focused — explicitly not a Ukrainian or Russian information operation.
- Founded 2007; significant pre-2022 work on Iraq, Afghanistan, and Middle East conflicts.
- OSINT integration: combines satellite imagery, social media geotagging, official statements to build ground-truth maps.
