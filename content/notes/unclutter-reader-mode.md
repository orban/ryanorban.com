## Summary

Unclutter is a browser extension that strips articles to readable format, removes ads and popups, and adds reading productivity features: highlight saving, annotation, and a built-in read-it-later list. It overlaps with native browser reader modes but goes further with persistence features — your highlights and reading list stay within the extension rather than requiring an external app.

The tool is in a category that includes Readwise Reader, Pocket, Instapaper, and native browser reader modes. The differentiation versus native reader mode is the annotation and read-later list management; versus dedicated read-later apps like Pocket, it stays entirely in the browser without requiring a separate account sync.

The read-it-later use case is chronically underserved — most people have Pocket or Safari Reading List queues they never actually read, because saving something and returning to it later requires a context switch. Inline browser extensions that make reading comfortable at the moment of discovery have a potentially better workflow fit than apps that require a dedicated reading session in a separate app.

## Key points

- Browser extension reader mode with ad blocking, highlight saving, and read-later list management.
- Available for both Chrome and Firefox; free.
- Annotation and highlights stored locally without requiring an external service.
- Competes with Pocket, Instapaper, Readwise Reader, and native browser reader modes.
- Reduces friction by keeping the reading workflow in-browser rather than requiring context-switch to a separate app.
