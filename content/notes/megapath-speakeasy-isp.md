## Summary

A Pinboard bookmark from March 2009 of MegaPath's network coverage page, saved with the note: "Speakeasy is having nationwide connectivity problems, I know this first hand in NorCal, seems to be fixed." Speakeasy was an ISP popular with technically-minded users in the mid-2000s; MegaPath acquired Speakeasy in 2010.

This is an ephemeral troubleshooting bookmark — the kind saved in the heat of an internet outage. Speakeasy was known for offering DSL and T1 services with business-grade reliability; a nationwide outage would have been notable enough to document.

## Key points

- Speakeasy ISP nationwide connectivity problems, NorCal especially affected, March 2009.
- MegaPath later acquired Speakeasy in 2010; both offered business DSL/T1 services.
- Ephemeral troubleshooting bookmark; coverage page no longer accessible.
