## Summary

This Quora answer by Ricky Kwok is a first-person account of attending Zipfian Academy (later rebranded under Galvanize), one of the leading data science bootcamps of its era. Student testimonials on Quora were a significant part of how prospective students evaluated bootcamps in 2015, when there was little independent data on outcomes and programs varied enormously in quality.

Zipfian Academy was founded in San Francisco and was distinctive for its focus on the full data science stack — not just statistics or just coding, but the combination of SQL, Python, machine learning, and data storytelling that practitioners actually used. It later became part of Galvanize, which operated coworking spaces alongside educational programs.

The save likely reflects Ryan Orban's interest in how students and alumni were describing the program publicly — as a curriculum lead, external perception would have mattered.

## Key points

- First-person student experience at Zipfian Academy / Galvanize data science program.
- Quora testimonials were a primary information source for prospective bootcamp students in 2015.
- Zipfian covered the full data science stack: SQL, Python, statistics, ML, storytelling.
- Later became Galvanize Data Science; Galvanize itself was acquired by Stride in 2021.
- Saved by Ryan Orban, who was teaching the curriculum being described.
