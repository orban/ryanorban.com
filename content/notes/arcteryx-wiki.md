## Summary

Arc'teryx is a Canadian outdoor apparel brand known for technical outerwear — Gore-Tex shells, fleece, and hardshell gear at premium prices. The brand has a notoriously complex product lineup with multiple series (Alpha, Beta, Gamma, Zeta, Atom) each targeting different use cases and activity levels, making it difficult to navigate without a reference.

This community-compiled master sheet aggregates prices, ratings, and specs across the Arc'teryx product line, serving as a buying guide and comparison tool. The complexity that creates demand for such a reference: a jacket called Alpha SV sounds similar to Beta SV without knowing that Alpha is for technical alpine climbing and Beta is for general mountaineering, and that "SV means severe weather while AR means all-round and LT" means lightweight.

Saved as a gear research reference — likely in the process of evaluating a specific purchase or comparing options across the product line.

## Key points

- Arc'teryx has a complex product taxonomy: Alpha/Beta/Gamma/Zeta/Atom series × SV/AR/LT/FL suffixes.
- Alpha = technical alpine, Beta = general mountaineering/hiking, Atom = synthetic insulation.
- SV (severe), AR (all-round), LT (lightweight), FL (fast and light) — suffixes indicate intended use intensity.
- Gore-Tex Pro vs Gore-Tex vs Gore-Tex Paclite: different waterproofing membranes at different weights and price points.
- Community reference sheet for navigating purchases in a deliberately complex lineup.
