## Summary

The Nomad Overland kitchen is a complete camp kitchen integrated into a slide-out drawer unit designed for the Toyota 4Runner 5th generation. The system includes a propane stove, sink, cutting board, drying rack, and storage in a single unit that slides out from the rear cargo area. It's the vehicle-integrated equivalent of a truck-bed camp kitchen setup.

The appeal of a dedicated slide-out kitchen vs. loose camp kitchen gear is setup time and ergonomics: unfold a drawer instead of unpacking a bag, cook at cargo-height rather than on the ground, and pack up by closing the drawer. For overlanding trips where you're camping multiple nights and cooking real meals, the difference between a 30-second kitchen deploy and a 10-minute gear-unpacking session accumulates quickly.

This was saved as part of the same December 25, 2021 research session that included [DFG Offroad 4Runner drawer](/notes/dfg-offroad-4runner-drawer/), Dobinsons 4Runner drawer system, and [DIY 4Runner drawer plans](/notes/diy-4runner-drawer-plans/) — a comprehensive survey of 4Runner cargo organization options. The Nomad Kitchen represents the most expedition-oriented end of that spectrum: purpose-built for cooking, not general storage.

The trade-off is cargo space: a dedicated kitchen unit takes up a significant portion of the 4Runner's cargo volume. It pairs best with a roof rack or Front Runner slimline rack for gear that doesn't need to be accessed quickly.
