## Summary

Nat is a personal CRM designed around the premise that relationships matter more than pipelines — it explicitly positions itself against the sales-funnel model of traditional CRM software. The pitch: instead of tracking deal stages and next actions, Nat tracks when you last interacted with people in your network and surfaces who you're drifting from. The core insight is that professional relationships decay passively — without intentional maintenance, valuable connections fade, and by the time you realize it, the relationship has become too cold to easily revive.

The feature set reflects this philosophy: contact recency tracking, interaction logging (when you met, what you discussed), a reconnect queue that surfaces people you haven't talked to recently, and light reminder functionality. This is closer to a relationship memory system than a pipeline tool — the goal is to capture context so interactions are warm ("I remember you were working on X, how did that go?") rather than transactional. Integration with email and calendar to automatically log interactions is the key automation layer that makes this passive to maintain.

The distinction from HubSpot or Salesforce for personal use is significant: those tools are designed for repeatable sales processes with teams and stages and close rates. For managing a personal professional network of 200-500 people — investors, potential co-founders, advisors, mentors, friends in adjacent industries — you don't need pipeline management, you need relationship memory and gentle prompting. Dex, Clay, and Folk are in the same category, with different emphasis on automation and enrichment.

## Key points

- Relationship recency as the core primitive: surfaces who you haven't talked to recently rather than tracking deal stages.
- Interaction logging: captures context from past conversations to make future interactions warm.
- Personal CRM category sits between full CRM (Salesforce, HubSpot) and no system (address book) — for managing 200-500 high-value contacts.
- Passive maintenance model: email/calendar integration auto-logs interactions without manual entry.
- Competes with Dex, Clay, Monica CRM (open-source), Notion personal CRM templates.
- Anti-pattern it addresses: the social debt of valuable relationships that atrophy because there's no system to prompt reconnection.
