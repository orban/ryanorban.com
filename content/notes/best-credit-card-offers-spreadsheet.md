## Summary

MonkeyMiles maintains a regularly-updated spreadsheet of the best current credit card signup offers and points bonuses. The spreadsheet covers cards across major issuers (Chase, Amex, Citi, Capital One) with current welcome bonuses, annual fees, and spending requirements — the key variables for evaluating whether a card's offer is worth pursuing.

The travel hacking community uses resources like this to track when bonuses are at historical highs (cards periodically run elevated offers) and to plan application timing around spending requirements. The MonkeyMiles spreadsheet is one of several community-maintained resources alongside Doctor of Credit, The Points Guy, and FlyerTalk wiki pages.

The value over official card websites: the spreadsheet aggregates and normalizes across cards, making comparison easier, and community members flag elevated offers when they appear — useful because some offers are available only through specific referral links or for limited periods.

## Key points

- Spreadsheet of current credit card signup bonuses — updated as offers change.
- Covers Chase, Amex, Citi, Capital One, and other major issuers.
- Key columns: welcome bonus, annual fee, spending requirement, bonus expiration.
- Community resource in the travel hacking / points optimization space.
- Complements Doctor of Credit and The Points Guy for offer tracking.
