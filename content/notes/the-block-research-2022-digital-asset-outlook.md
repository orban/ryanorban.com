## Summary

The Block Research's annual digital asset outlook for 2022 surveys the full crypto ecosystem at peak bull market — written in late 2021 and published just before the 2022 crash that would wipe out ~$2T in market capitalization. Reading it now is instructive: the bullish projections, sector analysis, and infrastructure investment theses reveal what the most sophisticated institutional observers believed to be directionally true about the space. Some predictions aged well; others mark the high-water mark of peak-cycle thinking.

The report covers DeFi, NFTs, Layer 2 scaling, institutional adoption, stablecoin infrastructure, DAO governance, and gaming/metaverse. The DeFi section analyzes TVL trends and protocol revenue, projecting continued growth in lending, derivatives, and DEX volumes. The Layer 2 coverage comes right as Arbitrum and Optimism were launching, with ZK-rollups seen as the longer-term solution — a call that proved prescient. The NFT section captures the moment when institutional collectors and brands were just beginning to enter the space.

The Block occupies a distinct position in crypto research: more institutional and data-driven than Messari (see `inbox/2022-01-19-messari-crypto-theses-2022.md` for comparison), with a heavier emphasis on on-chain metrics and market structure analysis. The 2022 outlook reads alongside Messari's Crypto Theses as complementary documents — Messari more opinionated and sector-specific, The Block more systematic and data-grounded. Both were written at the same market moment and capture the institutional crypto consensus of late 2021.

## Key points

- Comprehensive scan of DeFi, NFTs, Layer 2, stablecoins, DAOs, and institutional crypto at late-2021 peak
- Layer 2 section correctly identifies ZK-rollups as the longer-term scaling solution over optimistic rollups
- DeFi TVL and protocol revenue projections reflect the assumption of continued bull market conditions — useful baseline for understanding the 2022 crash's magnitude
- Institutional adoption section tracks the early wave of TradFi interest — exactly when Bitcoin ETF applications, bank custody, and hedge fund exposure were accelerating
- The Block Research methodology is data-first: on-chain metrics, developer activity, and revenue rather than narrative/thesis-driven analysis
- Pairs with Messari Crypto Theses for 2022 as a complementary institutional consensus document from the same moment
