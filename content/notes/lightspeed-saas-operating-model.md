## Summary

Natalie Luu at Lightspeed Venture Partners published this framework for how SaaS companies should think about operating metrics at different growth stages. The operating model framing is deliberate — this is about the full financial and operational picture, not just top-line metrics.

The framework is organized around the metrics that matter most at each stage: early-stage SaaS should focus on net revenue retention (NRR) and early customer cohort behavior before worrying about sales efficiency; growth-stage companies should track CAC payback period, magic number, and Rule of 40 as guides for balancing growth and profitability; mature companies need to demonstrate free cash flow conversion.

The core insight is that different metrics tell different stories at different stages, and optimizing for the wrong metric can lead companies astray. A company with 200% NRR but terrible CAC payback is making a different bet than one with 110% NRR and 12-month payback — neither is wrong, but each requires different capital planning. Lightspeed's framework helps founders know which dial to watch at which point.

## Key points

- Stage-specific SaaS metrics: early focus on NRR and cohort behavior; growth focus on CAC payback and efficiency.
- Rule of 40: growth rate + FCF margin ≥ 40% as a balance indicator at growth stage.
- Magic number: net new ARR / S&M spend — measures sales efficiency.
- Net revenue retention (NRR) is the single most important early-stage metric.
- Framework designed for Series A through growth stage — not early-seed metrics.
- From Natalie Luu at Lightspeed Venture Partners.
