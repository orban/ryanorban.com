## Summary

The [State of AI Report](/notes/state-of-ai-report/) is an annual publication by Nathan Benaich (Air Street Capital) and Ian Hogarth (later appointed head of the UK's Frontier AI Taskforce). Published each October, it synthesizes the year's most significant developments across AI research, industry, politics, and safety into a single dense slide deck.

The report covers: new model capabilities and benchmarks, the compute and hardware landscape (Nvidia dominance, chip alternatives), major research breakthroughs and their implications, the commercial AI landscape (funding, M&A, revenue), geopolitics (US-China competition, export controls), and AI safety developments. It reads as an opinionated summary rather than neutral aggregation — the authors have views and they share them.

The 2022 edition was published at the peak of the first generative AI wave — Stable Diffusion's open release, DALL-E 2, Midjourney, and GitHub Copilot going to GA. It documented the moment before ChatGPT changed public perception of the field. Earlier editions (2018 onwards) provide useful historical context for how AI expectations and capabilities evolved.

## Key points

- Annual report covering AI research, industry, hardware, policy, and safety.
- Co-authored by Nathan Benaich (Air Street Capital) and Ian Hogarth.
- Published each October — the 2022 edition captured the pre-ChatGPT generative AI moment.
- Covers compute/hardware, major research milestones, commercial landscape, and geopolitics.
- One of the most-cited annual AI summaries, used by researchers, investors, and policymakers.
- Opinionated — includes the authors' views, not just aggregated data.
