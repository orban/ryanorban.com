## Summary

Elad Gil wrote this analysis in October 2022 — before ChatGPT's public launch — asking where value accrues in the AI wave: to startups building applications, or to incumbents (cloud providers, large tech companies) controlling infrastructure. His framework has held up well.

The core argument: AI infrastructure (model training, compute, API access) tends to consolidate around incumbents with capital and distribution advantages — Google, Microsoft, Amazon, Nvidia. Startups building on top of foundation models face the thin layer problem: if OpenAI or Google releases a model that does your product's core task natively, your differentiation disappears overnight. The value that persists in startups tends to come from proprietary data, deep workflow integration, brand trust in specific verticals, or genuine UI/UX differentiation that foundation models don't commoditize.

The analysis was written when GPT-3 was the frontier model and Stability AI had just released Stable Diffusion. Gil was skeptical of AI wrapper startups that provided little more than a prompt and a web interface around an existing model. The skepticism proved well-founded — many such companies struggled as base models improved and incumbents added similar features natively. The companies that succeeded built defensibility beyond the AI capability itself: HubSpot and Salesforce integrating AI into existing trusted workflows, GitHub Copilot capturing developer habit, Notion AI embedded in productivity tools.

## Key points

- AI infrastructure (compute, models) consolidates to incumbents; startups must build beyond thin layers.
- Elad Gil's framework: proprietary data, workflow integration, vertical trust, and UX differentiation are defensible.
- AI wrapper startups (just a prompt + model API) face commoditization as base models improve.
- Written pre-ChatGPT, October 2022 — prescient about consolidation dynamics that played out in 2023-24.
- Value accrues where models don't substitute directly: data flywheels, distribution, and deep integrations.
- Pairs with a16z's navigating the AI wave and Benedict Evans' similar analyses.
