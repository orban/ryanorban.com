## Summary

Zipfian Academy co-hosted a happy hour with Speakeasy Beer during PyData SV 2014, bringing together conference attendees and the local San Francisco data science community. Events like this were central to how the data science community formed in 2014 — the PyData conference drew practitioners from around the country, and local organizations like Zipfian Academy used it as an opportunity to host meetups, build relationships, and establish community presence.

## Key points

- Community mixer during PyData SV 2014 — Zipfian Academy as a community hub for SF data science.
- Co-hosted with Speakeasy Beer — local SF brewery, characteristic of the startup/tech event culture.
- Organized by Ryan Orban and Zipfian Academy staff.
