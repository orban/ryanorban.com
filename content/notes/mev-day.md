## Summary

[MEV Day](/notes/mev-day/) was a conference organized by Flashbots as a focused gathering for the MEV (Maximal Extractable Value) research and practitioner community. It brought together searchers, block builders, protocol designers, and academics to share findings on how MEV is extracted, measured, and potentially mitigated or redistributed.

By 2022, MEV had evolved from a fringe research curiosity (kicked off by Flash Boys 2.0 in 2019) into a professional industry with dedicated firms, billions in annual extraction, and significant implications for Ethereum's security and user experience. Flashbots had created infrastructure to channel MEV more transparently, but this raised new questions: who captures the value, how does proposer-builder separation affect decentralization, and what does healthy MEV look like in a post-Merge Proof of Stake world?

[MEV Day](/notes/mev-day/) served as the yearly coordination point for this community — sharing empirical data on extraction volumes, new strategies (beyond sandwich and arbitrage into JIT liquidity, NFT sniping, cross-domain MEV), and next-generation infrastructure like SUAVE. For the vault, this is primarily a reference to the event and its context rather than a single deep idea.

## Key points

- Flashbots-organized conference at the center of the MEV research and practitioner community
- Covered: new MEV strategies, proposer-builder separation implications, cross-domain MEV, SUAVE roadmap
- 2022 was a pivotal year: pre-Merge work on MEV-Boost, post-Merge implications for validator economics
- JIT liquidity and NFT sniping emerged as new MEV vectors beyond the classic arbitrage/sandwich pair
- Cross-domain MEV: extraction opportunities spanning Ethereum L1 and multiple L2 rollups simultaneously
