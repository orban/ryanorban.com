## Summary

Colourco.de is a web-based color scheme generator that applies color theory rules to produce harmonious palettes interactively. You pick a base color and choose a harmony rule — complementary colors (opposite on the color wheel), analogous colors (adjacent hues), triadic, split-complementary, or tetradic — and the tool generates a coordinated palette in real time.

The value is in applying rules from color theory algorithmically. Rather than picking colors by gut feel, the tool grounds choices in relationships that tend to produce visually pleasing results: complementary palettes create high contrast, analogous palettes feel cohesive, triadic palettes balance vibrancy with variety. These rules come from the Itten color wheel and Josef Albers' work on color interaction.

Tools like Colourco.de emerged alongside the design tools wave of 2012-2014, including Adobe Kuler (now Adobe Color) and later Coolors.co. They democratized color design for developers and non-designers building web products who needed palette guidance without hiring a visual designer.

## Key points

- Color harmony rules: complementary (180°), analogous (30° apart), triadic (120° apart), split-complementary, tetradic
- Real-time interactive palette generation based on HSL color space relationships
- Complementary colors: maximum contrast — good for accent colors and call-to-action elements
- Analogous colors: low contrast, high cohesion — good for backgrounds and content areas
- Part of the 2012-2014 wave of color tool web apps; competes with Adobe Kuler and Coolors.co
- Color theory application: Itten's contrast of hue, simultaneous contrast, and the color wheel as design grammar
