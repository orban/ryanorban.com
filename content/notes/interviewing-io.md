## Summary

interviewing.io is a platform for practicing technical interviews through anonymous mock interviews with engineers from top tech companies. The anonymity is bidirectional: neither party knows who the other is until after the interview, which reduces social anxiety and makes the practice more realistic. Interviewers are volunteer engineers from companies like Google, Facebook, Amazon, and Airbnb; interviewees are candidates preparing for actual hiring loops.

The platform addresses a real gap in technical interview preparation: LeetCode and reading books develops knowledge, but the actual interview experience — thinking out loud under pressure, handling hints, asking clarifying questions, explaining your approach — requires practice in realistic conditions. Most people's first realistic interview experience is their actual interview, which wastes a significant opportunity to learn from low-stakes feedback. interviewing.io provides that practice environment.

The feedback mechanism is the key feature: after each session, both parties leave detailed feedback, and the platform provides playback of the session with interviewer annotations. This closes the loop between practice and improvement in a way that solo LeetCode grinding cannot. The platform also tracks which companies' engineers the mock interviewers work at, so candidates can practice with engineers from their target companies and get calibrated on that company's interview style.

## Key points

- Anonymous mock interviews with engineers from top tech companies — realistic pressure without the high stakes.
- Bidirectional anonymity: neither party knows who the other is, reducing social dynamics that distort the practice.
- Session playback with annotations: interviewer can timestamp and comment on specific moments — structured feedback loop.
- Fills the gap between knowledge preparation (LeetCode) and actual interview performance.
- Paid tiers for unlimited access; free interviews available on a limited basis.
- Complements resources like the [ML interviews book](/notes/ml-interviews-book/) (Chip Huyen) and [coding interview patterns](/notes/coding-interview-patterns/) guides.
