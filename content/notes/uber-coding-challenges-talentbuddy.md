## Summary

Talentbuddy was an early technical hiring platform (2012–2015 era) that partnered with companies to publish branded coding challenge sets — a precursor to HackerRank and LeetCode becoming dominant. This save is the Uber-branded challenge set, bookmarked by Ryan in September 2013 during the early weeks of Zipfian Academy.

The timing makes the context clear: Zipfian Academy students were simultaneously learning data science skills and preparing for job searches. Uber was a prominent SF tech company in 2013 (Series C, $258M raised, rapid geographic expansion underway) and a logical target for data science / data engineering roles.

Talentbuddy challenges were typically algorithmic — graph traversal, dynamic programming, string manipulation — the standard coding interview curriculum. For data science roles in 2013, candidates typically faced both algorithmic coding rounds and statistics/ML concept questions.

## Key points

- Talentbuddy: early technical hiring platform with company-branded coding challenge sets — precursor to HackerRank/LeetCode.
- Saved during Zipfian Academy cohort as interview practice material; Uber was a top target for SF tech roles in 2013.
- Uber was in rapid expansion in 2013: Series C, just beginning international growth, hiring aggressively in technical roles.
- Algorithmic challenge sets were (and remain) standard for technical hiring regardless of role type.
