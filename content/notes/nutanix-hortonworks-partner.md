## Summary

Nutanix joined the Hortonworks Certified Technology Partner Program in June 2013, a business development milestone that gave enterprise customers formal validation that HDP (Hortonworks Data Platform) workloads would run on Nutanix hyper-converged infrastructure. Ryan's bookmark note (BOOM!) captures the enthusiasm inside Nutanix — this was an important step in the company's strategy to expand beyond pure VMware virtualization into Hadoop analytics workloads.

Hortonworks was one of the two major Hadoop distributions in 2013 (alongside Cloudera) and the one most closely aligned with the Apache Software Foundation's governance model. Being a certified partner meant Nutanix engineering had done the work to ensure HDFS compatibility, YARN resource management integration, and joint support agreements that enterprise buyers required before deploying a new platform combination.

The certification was also competitive positioning against dedicated Hadoop hardware — pure-play commodity servers from Dell or HP. Nutanix's pitch: run your Hadoop analytics on the same infrastructure as your virtualization workloads, with certified compatibility and single-vendor support.

## Key points

- HDP certification: Hortonworks validated that Nutanix hardware/software passed the compatibility test suite for running full Hadoop workloads.
- Nutanix Hadoop strategy: position NDFS as an HDFS alternative and Nutanix appliances as certified Hadoop infrastructure — competing with dedicated commodity server clusters.
- Hortonworks vs Cloudera: two competing Hadoop distributions in 2013; Nutanix pursuing certifications with both to cover the enterprise market.
