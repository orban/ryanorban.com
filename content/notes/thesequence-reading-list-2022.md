## Summary

TheSequence is an AI/ML newsletter by Jesus Rodriguez that covers research and applied ML with a practitioner focus. Their 2022 reading list is a curated guide to books and papers worth reading for someone trying to build serious ML depth going into the year — a snapshot of what the community considered essential reference material at the end of 2021.

The reading list format serves a specific function in a fast-moving field: there's too much to read, so curation by credible practitioners has genuine value. TheSequence focuses on the applied end — production ML, MLOps, and emerging capabilities — rather than pure theory, so their picks tend toward books like Chip Huyen's work, practical deep learning resources, and applied NLP references alongside foundational papers.

The end-of-2021 context: GPT-3 was 18 months old and had spawned enormous research activity; DALL-E had just dropped; AlphaFold2 had solved protein folding; transformer-based architectures had expanded from NLP into vision, audio, and code. A 2022 reading list from this moment would reflect both catching up on this wave and anticipating what comes next — foundation models, multimodal learning, and the early hints of what would become the generative AI explosion.

## Key points

- TheSequence by Jesus Rodriguez: practitioner-focused ML/AI newsletter, covers applied and research ML
- A 2022 reading list captures the field at a pivotal moment: GPT-3, DALL-E, AlphaFold2 all recent milestones
- Likely includes: Hands-On ML (Géron), Deep Learning (Goodfellow), Designing ML Systems (Huyen), foundational papers
- Reading list as curation artifact: the choices reveal what practitioners considered essential vs. hype in late 2021
- Foundation models and transformer architectures were the dominant theme heading into 2022
