## Summary

ryanburgess/engineer-manager is a GitHub repository by Ryan Burgess (engineering manager at Netflix) that curates resources for engineering managers. It covers the full lifecycle of EM concerns: transitioning from individual contributor to management, hiring and team building, 1:1 meetings, technical leadership, and engineering culture.

The resource types include articles, books, podcasts, and conference talks. The book section typically includes recognized titles in the space: The Manager's Path by Camille Fournier, High Output Management by Andy Grove, An Elegant Puzzle by Will Larson, and more recent additions as the field matures. The article collection tends to reflect what's resonated in engineering leadership circles — often posts from blogs like Lara Hogan's or engineering blogs at large tech companies.

What makes this kind of curated list valuable is that engineering management is a relatively young discipline compared to general management. The skills needed — running technical roadmaps, managing engineers' career development, hiring for technical roles, debugging organizational dysfunction — have their own distinct literature that's not well-served by generic business leadership books. This repo aggregates that specific literature.

## Key points

- Curated by Ryan Burgess (Netflix EM), active since the early engineering management blogging era.
- Covers: IC→manager transition, hiring, 1:1 structure, technical debt decisions, engineering culture.
- Canonical books in this list: The Manager's Path, High Output Management, An Elegant Puzzle.
- Useful starting point for new engineering managers overwhelmed by the category.
