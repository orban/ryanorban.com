## Summary

Resoume is a resume building platform that added an AI assistant for generating and improving resume content — one of the early consumer applications of AI writing assistance before ChatGPT made AI text generation mainstream in late 2022. The tool helps users write bullet points for job experience sections, tailor resumes to specific job descriptions, and optimize for ATS (Applicant Tracking System) keyword matching.

The resume optimization space was an obvious early AI application: resumes are highly structured (clear sections, predictable formats), the task is well-defined (describe your experience compellingly), and the user pain is real (most people struggle to write about themselves). Tools like Resoume, Teal, and Kickresume all attacked this market in the 2020-2022 window with AI features before the foundation model revolution made custom-built solutions feel redundant.

In retrospect, this class of tool represents the pre-ChatGPT AI product era: purpose-built applications with fine-tuned or prompted language models for specific tasks. Post-ChatGPT, many users simply use a general-purpose LLM to do the same task — which commoditized the value proposition of specialized tools and forced them to differentiate on workflow, integrations, and ATS optimization data rather than raw text generation.

## Key points

- AI-assisted resume writing and ATS optimization — an early consumer AI application
- Helps with bullet point generation, tailoring to job descriptions, keyword optimization
- Pre-ChatGPT category: specialized AI writing tools before general-purpose LLMs made them feel redundant
- ATS optimization still a meaningful differentiator: data on what keywords specific systems parse
- Competes with Teal, Kickresume, Enhancv — all converging on similar AI feature sets
- The underlying pain (people are bad at writing about themselves) is real; the moat is now in workflow and data
