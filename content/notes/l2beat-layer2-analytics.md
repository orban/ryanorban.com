## Summary

L2BEAT is the canonical analytics and research platform for the Ethereum Layer 2 ecosystem. Beyond tracking TVL (total value locked) across Arbitrum, Optimism, zkSync, StarkNet, Polygon, and others, L2BEAT provides what other TVL dashboards don't: a security risk framework that categorizes each L2 by how much trust users are actually required to place in the system.

The risk framework categorizes L2s on dimensions like: is there a fraud proof system? Can the sequencer be censored? Is the upgrade key timelocked? Can the operator steal funds? This analysis revealed that many systems marketed as L2s in 2022 had significant trust assumptions that weren't prominently disclosed — some were closer to multi-sigs than trustless rollups. L2BEAT's rigorous classification created accountability pressure on L2 teams to actually deliver on decentralization roadmaps.

The platform emerged from Ethereum researcher Bartek Kiepuszewski's work and became indispensable for anyone making deployment or investment decisions in the L2 ecosystem. For DeFi protocols deciding where to deploy, and for users deciding where to bridge assets, L2BEAT's security ratings are the reference. It distinguishes between Optimistic rollups, ZK rollups, Validiums, and Plasma chains with honest characterizations of each.

## Key points

- TVL tracking across all Ethereum L2s with historical charts — the reference for ecosystem size.
- Security risk matrix: fraud proofs, upgrade keys, sequencer censorship resistance, exit window analysis.
- Classifies L2s honestly: Arbitrum, Optimism, zkSync Era vs. systems with meaningful trust assumptions.
- Forces accountability: L2 teams know their security properties are publicly graded.
- Essential reference for DeFi protocol teams choosing deployment targets.
