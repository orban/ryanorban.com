## Summary

Protos is an independent cryptocurrency news publication that positions itself with the tagline informed crypto news — implying more rigor, less hype, and more willingness to cover failures, scams, and structural problems than promotional crypto media. The publication covers blockchain news, DeFi events, exchange activity, NFT market developments, and policy, with a tone that skews skeptical of industry claims while remaining substantively engaged with the technology.

The media landscape for crypto in 2022 was notably bad: most dedicated publications were funded by crypto companies or dependent on advertising from exchanges and projects, creating obvious conflicts of interest. Press releases were reprinted as news, token launches were covered without critical analysis, and negative stories about sponsors were under-covered. Protos occupied a niche alongside Web3 is Going Just Great (Molly White's project), Dirty Bubble Media, and Crypto Critics' Corner — independent voices willing to write about crypto problems directly.

The timing of saving this (April 2022) was during peak crypto prices, just before the Terra/Luna collapse (May 2022), the broader crypto winter of 2022, and the FTX collapse (November 2022). Critical crypto coverage that was marginal in April 2022 became mainstream by end of year. Publications like Protos were early in treating skepticism as compatible with serious crypto journalism rather than as disqualifying bias.

## Key points

- Independent crypto news with "informed" positioning — more skeptical than sponsor-dependent industry publications.
- Covers blockchain, DeFi, exchanges, NFTs, and crypto policy without the promotional framing typical of the space.
- 2022 context: saved at peak prices, before Terra/Luna, FTX, and broader crypto winter validated critical coverage.
- Part of a small tier of independent critical crypto journalism alongside Molly White / Web3 is Going Just Great.
- Media independence matters in crypto: most publications have structural conflicts via ad revenue from the industry they cover.
