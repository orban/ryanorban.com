## Summary

Bay Area Motorcycle Training is one of the primary providers of the MSF Basic RiderCourse (Motorcycle Safety Foundation) in the San Francisco Bay Area. Bookmarked as a personal reference — likely for motorcycle license preparation.

The MSF Basic RiderCourse is the standard path to a motorcycle license in California: a weekend course that combines classroom instruction with range exercises on provided low-displacement motorcycles. Completion waives the DMV riding skills test. The San Francisco location was convenient for city residents, though range courses are typically held at parking lots in less dense areas.

California requires either passing the DMV motorcycle skills test or completing an approved course like the MSF BRC to obtain a Class M1 motorcycle license endorsement.

## Key points

- MSF Basic RiderCourse: weekend format (classroom + range), DVM test waiver upon completion, motorcycles provided
- California Class M1 license: required for all motorcycles; M2 for mopeds/motorized scooters
- Bay Area Motorcycle Training operated multiple locations across the Bay Area — the SF calendar was for their San Francisco-area range sessions
- The course is designed for true beginners — no prior experience required, small motorcycles used on the range
