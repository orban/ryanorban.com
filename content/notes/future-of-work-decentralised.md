## Summary

Florian Strauf argues in this 2022 Medium essay that DAOs represent a fundamental shift in how knowledge work is organized — away from the hierarchical employer-employee relationship toward fluid, permissionless contributor networks where work is rewarded with tokens and governance rights. The piece reflects the optimism of early 2022 when DAO treasury sizes were hitting historic highs and "leaving your job to work in DAOs" was a genuine narrative arc for many people in the crypto space.

The core argument: traditional employment bundles too many things — access to work, income, identity, community, and governance rights — in a single relationship with a single entity. DAOs unbundle these. You can contribute to multiple DAOs simultaneously, earn tokens proportional to contribution, hold governance rights in projects you care about, and exit freely without institutional permission. The model is closer to freelancing or open-source contribution than employment, but with economic upside tied to protocol value.

The future of work angle also touches on the remote-work wave: the pandemic had already demonstrated that physical co-location wasn't necessary for many knowledge work roles, and that asynchronous coordination could work at scale. DAOs extend this logic — if geography doesn't matter, why should legal jurisdiction? If teams can coordinate asynchronously, why not with participants who hold tokens rather than employment contracts?

## Key points

- DAO contribution model unbundles employment: separate work access, income, governance rights, community membership
- Permissionless participation: anyone can contribute without HR approval; token rewards calibrate pay to contribution
- Contrasts with platform labor (Uber, Fiverr): DAO workers own governance tokens, not just gig earnings
- Challenges: coordination overhead, lack of benefits/security, contributor burnout common in DAO work patterns
- The 2022 DAO employment wave largely reversed during the bear market — contributor retention proved difficult
