## Summary

The Complete FAANG Preparation is a GitHub repository by Akash Singh that aggregates interview preparation resources for large tech companies (Facebook/Meta, Amazon, Apple, Netflix, Google, and equivalents). The repo covers the two main tracks in these interviews: Data Structures and Algorithms (DSA) and computer science fundamentals (OS, DBMS, SQL, networking, OOP).

The DSA section follows a structured curriculum including the "Love Babbar 450" — a popular 450-question DSA sheet that was widely followed in India's competitive tech job market in 2021–2022 as preparation for software engineering roles. The questions range from arrays and linked lists through graphs, dynamic programming, and system design. The CS fundamentals sections include theory-level coverage of operating systems, database internals, and network protocols.

This kind of aggregator repo reflects the high coordination in the technical interview pipeline: once it became clear that large tech companies test a specific set of algorithmic patterns, a whole ecosystem of preparation resources (LeetCode, GeeksForGeeks, curated question lists) emerged. The FAANG interview pattern is now broadly emulated even by companies that don't have the same resources as FAANG.

## Key points

- DSA coverage: arrays through graphs, DP, trees — aligned with patterns tested at large tech companies.
- CS fundamentals: OS, DBMS, SQL, networking, OOP — all as theory + questions.
- Follows popular Love Babbar 450 DSA problem set, widely used in Indian tech job preparation.
- Reflects the standardization of technical interviews — the pattern is now emulated across the industry.
