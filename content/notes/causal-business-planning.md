## Summary

Causal is a financial and business modeling tool positioned as a better alternative to Excel for planning work. Instead of spreadsheet grids with opaque cell references, Causal uses named variables and natural-language formulas — Revenue = Customers × ARPU rather than =B3*C3. It connects directly to data sources (accounting software, CRM, databases) and produces shareable, embeddable dashboards.

The product targets the workflow that lives at the intersection of financial modeling and business intelligence: scenario planning, headcount modeling, unit economics, and board reporting. Excel handles this technically but creates problems at scale — deeply nested formulas that no one understands six months later, no version history, no collaboration model, and no live data connections. Causal tries to solve each of these.

The key differentiator over tools like Google Sheets is the formula language: Causal formulas are readable expressions with units, named ranges as first-class objects, and automatic scenario handling. You can define a base case, bull case, and bear case on any variable and the model automatically propagates through. The visualization layer is built-in — models produce charts and dashboards without exporting to separate tools.

## Key points

- Named variable formulas ("Revenue = Customers × ARPU") instead of opaque cell references — readable, maintainable models.
- Live data connections to accounting software, CRM, databases — models update automatically.
- Built-in scenario modeling: define base/bull/bear cases on any variable, automatic propagation.
- Targets financial modeling, headcount planning, unit economics, board reporting.
- Competes with Excel, Google Sheets, Mosaic, Runway in the FP&A tooling space.
