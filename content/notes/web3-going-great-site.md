## Summary

Web3 Is Going Just Great is Molly White's running timeline of failures, scams, hacks, rug pulls, and general chaos in the cryptocurrency, NFT, and broader web3 space. This bookmark appears to be from the site's early days — it launched in January 2022 (this save is December 2021, suggesting either an early preview or a near-launch save).

The site's format is a reverse-chronological timeline with individual incident entries, each with a brief summary, a link to source coverage, and a running counter of total funds lost across all documented incidents. The running total has grown into the billions. Molly White is a software engineer and Wikipedia editor who brought the same documentation mindset to tracking crypto harms as Wikipedia applies to encyclopedia accuracy.

What made the site distinctive and widely cited: it didn't editorialize heavily. Each entry was factual — "[Protocol X] was exploited for $[amount]" — with links to primary sources. The accumulation of matter-of-fact incidents was more damning than any polemic. By early 2022 it was being cited in Congressional testimony, mainstream financial press, and academic papers on blockchain externalities.

Note: a specific incident entry from January 2022 is bookmarked separately at Web3 Is Going Just Great — Jan 15, 2022 Entry.

## Key points

- Launched December 2021 / January 2022 by Molly White — software engineer and Wikipedia editor
- Format: reverse-chronological incident timeline, each with source links and brief factual summary
- Running total of documented losses — billions across hundreds of incidents through 2022-2023
- No-editorial approach: factual documentation is more effective than polemic; cited in Congressional testimony
- Became the canonical reference for web3 critique literature and was linked from academic papers, press, and policy discussions
