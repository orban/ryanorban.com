## Summary

olimpiocrypto (Olimpio) is a Web3 researcher and content creator who was covering Lens Protocol around its June 2022 launch. Lens Protocol is a decentralized social graph built on Polygon, developed by the Aave team (specifically Stani Kulechov). The core idea: your social graph — followers, follows, posts, comments — is represented as NFTs and owned by you, not by a platform like Twitter or Instagram.

In traditional social media, your followers are the platform's asset. Lens Protocol inverts this: your profile is an NFT you own, each post is an NFT, followers are represented by on-chain relationships. If Twitter disappears, you lose your followers. If Lens' front-end disappears, your social graph persists on-chain and any other client can render it. This is the "protocol, not platform" thesis applied to social media.

The bookmark in June 2022 captures the moment of genuine developer excitement around Lens: it had just launched its first wave of profiles (invite-only), dozens of apps were building on the protocol, and the question of whether decentralized social could ever achieve mainstream adoption felt live. The answer three years later was "not yet at scale, but the developer ecosystem is real."

## Key points

- Lens Protocol: decentralized social graph on Polygon — profiles, posts, and follows as NFTs
- Built by Aave team (Stani Kulechov) — connected the DeFi infrastructure community to social media
- User owns their social graph: platform-agnostic, portable, not subject to deplatforming
- Launch window (June 2022): invite-only, significant developer interest, multiple apps building on protocol
- Protocol, not platform thesis: any client can render the same social graph — competition on UI not on data lock-in
- Related: Farcaster (alternative decentralized social protocol), Bluesky (AT Protocol)
