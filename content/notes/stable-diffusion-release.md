## Summary

This post by @pess_r documents the initial announcement of Stable Diffusion model checkpoints being made available — "text-to-image checkpoints are now available for research purposes upon request." This was the first public release of the Stable Diffusion weights before the full open-source launch via the CompVis GitHub repository, in August 2022.

The tweet notes two key pending developments: "a more permissive release" (foreshadowing the CreativeML-OpenRAIL-M license) and inpainting checkpoints (filling in masked regions of existing images). It also mentions integration with Runway ML for text-to-video-editing — Runway's subsequent focus area that led to Gen-1 and Gen-2 video models.

This captures the announcement moment of what became a watershed event. Before this release, state-of-the-art text-to-image generation was accessible only through closed APIs with content policies (DALL-E 2, Midjourney). Stable Diffusion's open weights changed the landscape permanently — enabling local inference, custom fine-tuning, and community modification at a scale impossible with closed systems.

## Key points

- Announcement tweet for Stable Diffusion's initial research checkpoint release.
- Initial release "for research purposes upon request" — pre-dated the full permissive open-source release.
- Foreshadowed: more permissive license + inpainting checkpoints + Runway ML integration for video.
- August 2022: the beginning of the open-source generative AI image era.
- Direct predecessor to the CompVis GitHub repository and full community release.
- Runway ML mentioned as early integration partner — later pivoted to Gen-1/Gen-2 video generation.
