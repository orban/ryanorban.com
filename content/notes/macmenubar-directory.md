## Summary

MacMenuBar.com is a curated directory of macOS menu bar applications — small utilities that live in the Mac's top-right status bar rather than in the Dock or as full-window apps. The site catalogs 700+ apps organized by category: productivity, system monitoring, clipboard management, window management, networking, time tracking, and more.

The macOS menu bar is a uniquely Mac interface concept: a persistent strip at the top of the screen that apps can occupy with icons providing quick access to settings or status. Third-party apps have colonized this space extensively — Bartender (manage which icons show), Stats (system monitoring), Rectangle (window management), CleanMyMac (system maintenance), 1Password (password manager mini), Clockify (time tracking) all have menu bar presences. The space has its own crowded ecosystem that doesn't neatly overlap with the broader Mac app ecosystem.

For developers and power users setting up a new Mac, a directory like MacMenuBar.com serves a discovery function: it's hard to know what menu bar utilities exist without systematically browsing. The categories help filter by need. The site complements resources like [awesome-macos-command-line](/notes/awesome-macos-command-line/) (terminal tools) and Setapp (subscription app service) for the full picture of Mac software worth knowing about.
