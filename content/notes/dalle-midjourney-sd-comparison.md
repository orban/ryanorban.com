## Summary

This August 2022 Twitter thread by fabians.eth is a systematic side-by-side comparison of DALL-E 2, Midjourney, and Stable Diffusion across photography, illustration, painting styles, and abstract genres. August 2022 is the precise moment when all three were simultaneously accessible: DALL-E 2 was in limited access via OpenAI, Midjourney was in public beta via Discord, and Stable Diffusion had just been released open-source by CompVis.

The "image synths are like instruments" framing is apt for the period: each model had a distinctive aesthetic signature. DALL-E 2 produced photorealistic results with a distinctive clean quality. Midjourney leaned toward cinematic, painterly aesthetics that quickly became the recognizable AI art look. Stable Diffusion was the most flexible but required more prompt engineering to achieve consistent quality. The comparison thread format became a common community genre for evaluating model releases.

From a research perspective, this kind of comparative human evaluation — same prompts, different models, systematic coverage of styles — is qualitative but captures what benchmark metrics miss. The aesthetic signatures of these three models shaped what the broader public came to expect from generative AI image generation, and the community forum (Twitter, Reddit) was where these evaluations happened before formal research caught up.

## Key points

- Side-by-side comparison of DALL-E 2, Midjourney, and Stable Diffusion at their simultaneous emergence.
