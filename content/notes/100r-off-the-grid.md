## Summary

100R is the creative studio of Rekka Bellum and Devine Lu Linvega, who live and work aboard sailing vessel Pino. Their off-grid documentation is a detailed accounting of how they manage water, power, cooking, heating, sanitation, and internet connectivity under the severe constraints of ocean sailing. It's distinct from most off-grid living content because it's driven by necessity, not lifestyle signaling.

Their water system uses a 170L main tank plus 50L secondary and 100L in portable bins, with consumption at ~2.5L per person per day through discipline (manual pump faucets rather than pressurized tap). Power is solar-only, and rather than scaling up battery capacity, they've systematically reduced power requirements. The key principle they articulate: "halving requirements is the same as doubling capacity" — constraint as design tool, not just budget limitation.

The internet section is particularly relevant for remote workers: offline website mirrors, satellite phones for weather data (not general internet), and strategic timing around marina connectivity. They've designed their tools (Uxn, Orca, custom systems) to work offline-first. The off-grid documentation is adjacent to their broader philosophy of maintainable, simple systems that can be repaired at sea, far from supply chains.

## Key points

- "Halving requirements is the same as doubling capacity" — the central design principle applied to water, power, food, and computing.
- Solar-only power with reduced consumption rather than larger battery bank — constraint drives elegance.
- Water: 170L+ storage, ~2.5L/person/day with manual pump faucets and deliberate conservation.
- Tools designed to be offline-first and repairable: no proprietary electronics, no dependencies on connectivity.
- Internet: offline mirrors + satellite for weather only — demonstrates viable deep-work setup without reliable connectivity.
- Connects to broader philosophy of small computing and self-sufficient digital tools (Uxn, Orca).
