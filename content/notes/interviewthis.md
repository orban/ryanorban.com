## Summary

[InterviewThis](/notes/interviewthis/) is a community-maintained GitHub repository collecting questions that developers should ask when interviewing with prospective employers. The framing is important: most interview question lists focus on what employers ask candidates; this reverses the relationship — candidates evaluating companies.

The questions probe topics that are difficult to assess from job descriptions: engineering culture (how are on-call rotations structured?), technical practices (what's the test coverage culture? how does code review work?), team dynamics (how are technical decisions made?), growth and learning (is there a training budget?), and company health (what happened to the last person in this role?). These surface information that standard interviews are designed to obscure.

The community-sourced format means the list grows with collective wisdom about red flags and green flags in engineering organizations. The GitHub issues and PRs show ongoing debate about which questions are most informative — an implicit signal about what the developer community has found most predictive of good vs. bad places to work. Useful as a pre-offer checklist.

## Key points

- Reverse-interview questions: developers evaluating employers, not the other way around.
- Covers: engineering culture, technical practices, team dynamics, growth, company health.
- Community-maintained GitHub repo — collective wisdom about what signals good/bad employers.
- Useful complement to Glassdoor and LinkedIn research — direct questions rather than filtered reviews.
- Surfaces questions that catch employers off guard — useful for probing culture alignment.
- Opens conversations about how engineering teams actually function, not just what's on the careers page.
