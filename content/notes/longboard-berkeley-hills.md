## Summary

A longboarding video captured on one of UC Berkeley's notoriously steep residential hills — saved in 2009 when this kind of spontaneous urban downhill footage was early YouTube-era viral content. Berkeley sits at the base of the East Bay Hills, and many streets drop sharply from campus toward downtown, making them a natural draw for downhill skateboarding and longboarding.

The note is mostly ephemeral — a college-era bookmark — but it captures the geography and culture of UC Berkeley in 2009. Downhill longboarding on those hills was a real phenomenon: Panoramic Way, Dwight Way at their steepest grades, and several streets near Strawberry Canyon were known spots. The combination of smooth asphalt, steep grade, and light traffic made them attractive and genuinely dangerous.
