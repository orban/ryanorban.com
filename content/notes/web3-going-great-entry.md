## Summary

Web3 Is Going Just Great is Molly White's running timeline of failures, scams, and disasters in the cryptocurrency, NFT, and broader web3 space, covering incidents from early 2021 onward. Each entry documents a specific incident — exchange hacks, rug pulls, protocol exploits, and the general chaos that the speculative frenzy of 2021-2022 produced in abundance.

This particular bookmark points to a specific entry from January 15, 2022. The site's value isn't just as a list of failures — it's as a counterweight to the promotional framing that dominated crypto media at the time. While outlets like Bankless and CoinDesk covered upside, Molly White's site provided a systematic accounting of what the space was actually producing for ordinary participants: losses, fraud, and broken promises.

The site emerged as one of the most-cited references for web3 skepticism — not from a position of ignorance but from someone who understood the technology and chose to document its actual track record rather than its theoretical potential.

## Key points

- Molly White launched Web3 Is Going Just Great in January 2022 as the NFT and DeFi frenzy was at its peak
- Tracks specific incidents with links to sources — rug pulls, hacks, failed decentralized exchanges, Ponzi schemes disguised as yield farming
- The format (timeline + incident summaries) makes the scale of failures legible in a way that individual articles don't
- Complements technical critiques by Moxie Marlinspike and economic critiques of proof of work energy use
- Often cited alongside web3 critique literature alongside arguments that the technology solves coordination problems that don't exist
