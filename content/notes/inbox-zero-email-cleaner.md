## Summary

Inbox Zero is a web application targeting the email overload problem with AI automation. The core features are a newsletter cleaner (bulk unsubscribe from mailing lists), a cold email blocker (detect and filter unsolicited sales outreach), and email analytics (understand your inbox composition). The aim is reaching inbox zero without manually processing hundreds of unwanted messages.

The newsletter cleaner pattern is well-established — Unroll.me pioneered this before being acquired. The differentiation Inbox Zero claims is AI-powered categorization that's more accurate than rule-based filtering, plus the combination of newsletter management with cold email blocking in one tool. Cold email filtering is a harder problem: it requires distinguishing legitimate business correspondence from unsolicited sales outreach, a judgment call that benefits from ML.

The email analytics layer reveals inbox composition: what percentage of emails are newsletters, cold outreach, actual correspondence. This is useful for diagnosing the problem before applying solutions. For professionals managing multiple domains or high-volume inboxes, understanding the composition is a necessary first step before cleanup. The tool connects to Gmail and likely uses OAuth access to read and manage messages.

## Key points

- Bulk newsletter unsubscriber + cold email blocker + inbox analytics in one tool.
- AI categorization to distinguish newsletters, cold outreach, and real correspondence.
- Analytics reveal inbox composition before cleanup — diagnose, then act.
- Connects to Gmail via OAuth; read/manage permissions required.
- In the same space as Unroll.me (unsubscribe), SaneBox (filtering), HEY (email client redesign).
