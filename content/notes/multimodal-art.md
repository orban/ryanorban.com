## Summary

multimodal.art is a gallery and community platform for AI-generated artwork, specifically focused on outputs from multimodal models — systems that understand and generate across text, images, and other modalities. When this was saved in April 2022, the landscape was: DALL-E 2 had just been announced (April 6, 2022) but was invite-only; Midjourney was in early Discord alpha; Stable Diffusion wouldn't be released for another four months. The community was working primarily with CLIP-guided diffusion, VQGAN+CLIP, Disco Diffusion, and similar techniques that required significant technical SKILL and compute.

The site positioned itself as a curated space for AI art that took the medium seriously — not as a novelty but as an emerging artistic practice. This was a genuine question in early 2022: was AI art a tool for artists (like Photoshop), a new medium with its own aesthetics (like photography in the 1800s), or a threat to illustrators and concept artists? The community exploring these tools were often technically sophisticated — ML researchers, creative coders, and artists who had come from Processing and generative art traditions.

The historical context matters: this was the moment just before AI image generation became mainstream. The techniques required prompt engineering that was closer to parameter tuning than writing, running Google Colab notebooks, and waiting hours for outputs. Four months later, Stable Diffusion's open-source release would collapse this into a consumer product. The community captured at multimodal.art represents the pre-democratization phase, when the tools were genuinely experimental.

## Key points

- Gallery for AI-generated art from multimodal models — saved April 2022, four months before Stable Diffusion open release.
- Technical context: pre-Stable Diffusion toolchain was VQGAN+CLIP, Disco Diffusion, CLIP-guided synthesis — required real technical effort.
- Represents the pre-democratization moment in AI art — before consumer tools collapsed the barrier to entry.
- The aesthetic vocabulary: CLIP-guided images had a distinctive dreamlike, painterly quality distinct from later diffusion models.
- Community overlap with generative art and creative coding traditions — Processing, openFrameworks practitioners.
- Historical artifact: DALL-E 2 was announced the same week this was saved; Midjourney in Discord alpha.
