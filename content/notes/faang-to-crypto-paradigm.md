## Summary

Paradigm is one of the most influential crypto-native venture firms (Matt Huang and Fred Ehrsam, ex-Coinbase co-founder). This September 2021 essay makes a direct argument to engineers at FAANG companies: the career opportunity in crypto / web3 is better than it looks from the outside, and the right time to make the move is now (meaning Q3 2021, near the bull market peak).

The community garden metaphor frames the argument: FAANG companies are fully cultivated gardens where incremental work produces marginal improvements to mature systems, while crypto is an earlier, wilder space where foundational infrastructure hasn't been built yet. The work matters more, the ownership stake is larger (tokens vs. RSUs from a company already worth hundreds of billions), and the mission is more compelling for people who care about financial access and decentralized systems.

The essay likely covers specific arguments: the technological primitives (smart contracts, zero-knowledge proofs, layer 2 scaling) are still being invented; technical talent is the binding constraint so senior engineers have unusually high leverage; token compensation provides upside that stock options in a mature public company can't match; and the culture rewards first-principles thinking over incremental optimization.

From December 2021 hindsight: the timing was near the top. Engineers who left FAANG in late 2021 entered an industry that contracted sharply in 2022. The case wasn't wrong about the technology, but the timing advice aged poorly.

## Key points

- Paradigm's thesis: crypto is where senior engineers have most leverage — foundational primitives still being built
- Token compensation upside vs. RSUs in already-mature FAANG companies
- Community garden metaphor: mature FAANG = cultivated garden; crypto = still being planted
- Published September 2021, saved December 2021 — near the bull market peak; 2022 contraction followed
- Zero-knowledge proofs and Layer 2 scaling as examples of unsolved hard technical problems in crypto
