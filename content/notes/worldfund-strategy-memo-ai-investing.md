## Summary

WorldFund is a research lab and investment fund founded around the thesis that artificial intelligence will eventually automate investing end-to-end — sourcing, underwriting, and securitizing — and that whoever builds the right systems early will have a compounding structural advantage as AI performance scales with data. The November 2021 strategy memo outlines a $5M seed raise to hire a research team and build production modules for alpha-finding in venture capital, digital asset markets, and public markets, which ensemble into an autonomously learning neural network.

The fund is designed as a multi-strategy platform. At the research layer, it builds ML systems for each market vertical. At the fund layer, it deploys capital using those systems. Capital partners get equity in the lab plus exclusive early access to research — positioned as a call option on the disruption of institutional investing. The memo is explicit that today's technology is only good enough to "begin" — the long-horizon vision is a fully autonomous fund with no humans in the loop, which they view as inevitable.

The structure is unusual: it conflates a research lab with a fund to create alignment between capital returns and research quality. If the models work, the fund benefits; if the fund attracts elite LPs, they provide data and domain expertise that improves the models. This is analogous to the Renaissance Technologies approach of using market performance as the ground truth for research validity, but made explicit and extended to private markets and digital assets.

## Key points

- WorldFund argues that fully autonomous investing is inevitable over long time horizons and positions itself as an early mover capturing the data-advantage flywheel before incumbents adapt.
- The lab/fund structure creates alignment: research quality is tested in real markets, and LP relationships provide proprietary data and domain knowledge that feed back into model improvement.
- Three market verticals — venture capital, digital assets, and public markets — are targeted with separate alpha-generation modules that ensemble into a single learning system.
- Capital partners receive lab equity plus waived fees, positioning the investment as a call option on AI-driven disruption of institutional finance rather than a traditional fund return.
- Written in November 2021 — before ChatGPT, before the LLM explosion — the memo's assumptions about AI capability timelines and the pace of autonomous decision-making are worth reading with the hindsight of what came next.
