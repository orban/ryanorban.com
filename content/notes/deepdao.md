## Summary

[DeepDAO](/notes/deepdao/) is a data platform and analytics dashboard that tracks the DAO ecosystem across multiple blockchains. Where individual DAO tools like Snapshot or Aragon show you data for a single organization, [DeepDAO](/notes/deepdao/) provides cross-DAO visibility: how many DAOs exist, how much capital they hold in treasury, how many token holders participate in governance, what percentage actually vote, and how proposal activity trends over time.

The dashboard emerged from a real information gap: as the DAO space grew rapidly through 2021, there was no authoritative source for aggregate metrics. [DeepDAO](/notes/deepdao/) indexed organizations across Ethereum, Polygon, and other chains, tracking both on-chain governance (direct execution) and off-chain signaling via Snapshot. By early 2022 it was tracking billions in cumulative DAO treasury holdings.

Key metrics [DeepDAO](/notes/deepdao/) surfaces: total assets under governance (AUG), number of active voters, voter participation rates (almost universally low — most DAOs see single-digit percentage participation), proposal pass rates, and contributor activity. The low participation finding became a recurring critique of token voting — token-weighted governance in practice means a handful of large holders determine outcomes while the long tail of token holders never engage.

## Key points

- Cross-DAO analytics: treasury size, proposal counts, voter participation — the aggregate picture of the DAO ecosystem
- Voter participation is consistently low (often <5%): reveals plutocracy dynamics in token voting governance
- By early 2022 tracking hundreds of DAOs with combined treasuries in the billions — MakerDAO, Uniswap, Compound, Aave
- Data useful for due diligence: a DAO with 10,000 token holders but only 50 voters has a centralization problem
- Comparable to Dune Analytics for protocol-level data, but DAO-organization-focused rather than transaction-focused
