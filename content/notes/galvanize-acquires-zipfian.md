## Summary

In November 2014, Galvanize acquired Zipfian Academy, merging the data science bootcamp into Galvanize's broader tech education and coworking platform. The WSJ covered this in their VC/startup section, framing it as a signal of growing demand for data science training and the consolidation happening in the bootcamp industry.

Zipfian Academy had been founded in 2012 in San Francisco by Ryan Orban, Jonathan Dinu, and Adam Ozimek as one of the first immersive data science bootcamps. It ran 12-week cohort programs teaching Python, SQL, machine learning, and data visualization. By 2014 it had placed graduates at companies including Twitter, Yelp, LinkedIn, and various startups.

Galvanize was a tech education and workspace company that also ran coding bootcamps (full-stack web development) and co-working spaces across major US cities. Acquiring Zipfian let them add a data science track to their education portfolio and expand across their existing physical locations. The combined entity became Galvanize Data Science.

## Key points

- November 2014: Galvanize acquires Zipfian Academy — one of the first data science bootcamp acquisitions.
- Zipfian Academy founded 2012 by Ryan Orban, Jonathan Dinu, Adam Ozimek — first major data science immersive.
- Combined into Galvanize Data Science — expanded to Galvanize's multi-city network.
- Reflects mid-2014 bootcamp industry: strong demand but fragmented supply → consolidation beginning.
- Galvanize was later acquired by Stride in 2021, with the data science curriculum eventually wound down.
- WSJ's VC/startup desk covering bootcamp M&A signals the segment had reached mainstream investor attention.
