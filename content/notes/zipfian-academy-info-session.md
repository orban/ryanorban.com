## Summary

Zipfian Academy ran regular info sessions for prospective students — typically Monday evening events where alumni and staff fielded questions about the program. Info sessions were a core part of the data science bootcamp recruiting pipeline: prospective students who weren't sure whether to commit to a 12-week intensive program (and $16,000 in tuition in 2014) needed to meet people who had done it.

The alumni presence was key. At info sessions, current cohort members and graduates could speak to what the curriculum was actually like, what jobs they got after, and whether the data science skills stuck. Zipfian Academy's placement record — most graduates were getting jobs at companies like Airbnb, Twitter, and Yelp — was the most compelling selling point.

## Key points

- Weekly info sessions were Zipfian Academy's primary prospective student pipeline.
- Alumni testimonials at info sessions carried more weight than marketing copy.
- 2014 cohort placement record was strong: graduates going to Airbnb, Twitter, Yelp, and similar companies.
- Zipfian Academy co-founded by Ryan Orban and Jonathan Dinu.
