## Summary

[StoryGraph](/notes/storygraph/) is a book tracking and recommendation platform built by Nadia Odunayo as a deliberate alternative to Goodreads. The core differentiator: recommendations based on reading mood (tense, emotional, funny, dark, adventurous) and pacing (fast-paced, slow-burn) rather than just star ratings and genre categories. The result is recommendations that are genuinely better matched to what you're in the mood for at a given time.

The practical improvements over Goodreads: the interface is cleaner; there are no reviews cluttered with spoilers above the fold; reading stats are detailed (pages per day, reading pace trends, genre distribution over time); and mood tagging gives a richer signal for recommendations than genre alone. The platform doesn't have the social features Goodreads has (friends' shelves, book clubs), but for personal tracking and discovery it's more functional.

[StoryGraph](/notes/storygraph/) also benefits from not being owned by Amazon — Goodreads was acquired by Amazon in 2013 and has been largely neglected since, while the reading community wanted something better. StoryGraph emerged from that frustration and found a sizeable audience of readers who wanted a tracking tool without the abandoned-product feeling. By mid-2022 it had several hundred thousand users and had raised a small seed round.

## Key points

- Mood-based and pace-based book recommendations — tense, emotional, slow-burn as recommendation axes
- Detailed reading stats: pages per day, genre breakdown over time, yearly reading pace
- No ads, no Amazon data integration — explicit positioning as a Goodreads alternative
- Built by Nadia Odunayo — a notable example of a solo founder bootstrapping a consumer product to meaningful scale
- Import from Goodreads is supported — low friction to switch for existing users
