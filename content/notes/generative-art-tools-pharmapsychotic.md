## Summary

Pharmapsychotic's tools page is a comprehensive catalog of AI-powered generative art tools, compiled during the explosive growth of the Stable Diffusion ecosystem in 2022. It became one of the most-shared resource lists in the AI art community — a curated map of a fast-moving, fragmented landscape.

The catalog covers the full generative art workflow: text-to-image models (Stable Diffusion, DALL-E 2, Midjourney), image-to-image transformations, inpainting and outpainting, upscaling, depth estimation, NeRF-based 3D generation, and video/animation tools. Each entry has a brief description and link, organized by task type rather than just listing everything in a flat list.

The timing (September 2022) captures the moment when the field was democratizing: Stable Diffusion's open-source release had just happened, Colab notebooks and local tools were proliferating, and the barrier to using state-of-the-art image generation had dropped from thousands of dollars of compute to a free Google Colab session. This list helped people navigate that explosion.

## Key points

- Comprehensive catalog of AI art tools organized by task: generation, editing, upscaling, animation.
- Covers Stable Diffusion, DALL-E 2, Midjourney, and dozens of tools built on top of them.
- Published September 2022 — captures the post-open-source-release explosion.
- By pharmapsychotic, a widely-followed AI art community figure.
- One of the most-bookmarked resources during the early generative AI art wave.
- Complementary to CLIP Interrogator (also by pharmapsychotic) for prompt reverse-engineering.
