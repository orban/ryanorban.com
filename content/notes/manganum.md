## Summary

[Manganum](/notes/manganum/) is a Chrome extension that replaces the default new tab page with a productivity-oriented workspace: a sidebar with widgets for tasks, notes, calendar, weather, and links, alongside an inspirable background. The pitch is that the browser new tab is prime real estate — opened dozens of times daily, usually with nothing useful — and [Manganum](/notes/manganum/) converts it into a lightweight dashboard.

The design philosophy matches a broader trend in 2022: productivity tools competing to capture the zero-intent browser moment. When you open a new tab without a specific URL in mind, you're in a malleable state — products like [Manganum](/notes/manganum/), Momentum, and Tabliss try to fill that moment productively. The sidebar approach is a differentiation: rather than a full-page replacement, [Manganum](/notes/manganum/) adds a persistent side panel that stays accessible without hiding the browsing experience.

For the workflow it's targeting — knowledge workers who live in the browser — the value is consolidation. Instead of switching between a task manager tab, a notes tab, and a calendar tab, core widgets are always a click away in the sidebar. The competing products (Notion Web Clipper, Roam Research browser extensions, Todoist extension) all attack the same browser as work environment pattern from different angles.

## Key points

- [Manganum](/notes/manganum/): Chrome new tab extension with sidebar productivity widgets (tasks, notes, calendar, bookmarks)
- Competes for the "browser as work environment" position against Momentum, Tabliss, Notion
- New tab real estate: opened dozens of times daily, usually wasted — [Manganum](/notes/manganum/) tries to make it useful
- Sidebar-first design: persistent access without replacing the browsing experience entirely
- 2022 context: this class of tool was proliferating as remote work made browser-native workflows dominant
- Low switching cost but also low stickiness: users often cycle through new tab extensions before settling
