## Summary

moebio.com is the portfolio and creative work of Santiago Ortiz, a Colombian data visualization artist and researcher known for creating some of the most conceptually rich interactive visualization work on the web. Mind is one of his projects — a JavaScript-based interactive piece that is rendered entirely in the browser, which is why a simple content fetch returns only the title text.

Santiago Ortiz's work sits at the intersection of data visualization, generative art, and information theory. His projects often explore complex systems, network structures, and biological or cognitive phenomena through interactive graphics that reward exploration. Previous well-known work includes visualizations of literary structures, network graphs of ideas, and simulations of complex systems.

The "mind" project is consistent with his broader interest in consciousness, cognition, and information — topics that recur across his portfolio. Like much of his work, it likely uses d3.js or similar canvas/SVG tools to create something that is as much visual poetry as analysis. The value is in the interactive experience rather than a static description — these are pieces meant to be explored, not read about.
