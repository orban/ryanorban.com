## Summary

Gradient Flow (Ben Lorica and Roger Chen) published this analysis cataloging AI-focused companies that had reached $100M ARR by 2022. The list is useful as a pre-ChatGPT baseline — it shows which AI businesses achieved meaningful commercial scale before the generative AI wave reshaped the landscape.

The 2022 $100M club is dominated by companies applying ML to specific enterprise verticals: cybersecurity (Darktrace, SentinelOne, Vectra), computer vision (UiPath, Cognex), NLP for enterprise (Veeva, Medallia), and MLOps infrastructure (DataRobot, H2O.ai). Notably absent: pure LLM companies, which hadn't yet achieved this scale. OpenAI reached $100M ARR around this time but via API access, not traditional SaaS.

The pattern in the list reflects what worked before foundation model commoditization: narrow AI solving well-defined enterprise problems with clear ROI, sold through enterprise sales cycles. DataRobot automated model building; Darktrace did network anomaly detection; Scale AI did data labeling. These businesses are defensible because they're embedded in workflows, not because the underlying AI is proprietary.

The list is a useful historical artifact — many of these companies' competitive positions eroded post-2023 as foundation models encroached on their core capabilities.

## Key points

- 2022 catalog of AI companies at $100M+ ARR — pre-ChatGPT commercial AI landscape.
- Dominated by narrow vertical AI: cybersecurity, computer vision, enterprise NLP.
- MLOps and data infrastructure (DataRobot, Scale AI) well-represented.
- Pure LLM companies hadn't yet achieved this scale in 2022.
- Defensibility came from workflow integration, not proprietary models.
- From Gradient Flow — useful as a historical baseline for post-2023 disruption analysis.
