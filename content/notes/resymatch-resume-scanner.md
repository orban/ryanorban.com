## Summary

ResyMatch by Cultivated Culture is a free resume scanner that compares your resume against a job description and scores it based on ATS (Applicant Tracking System) compatibility. The core premise: most large employers screen resumes through ATS software before a human sees them, and resumes that don't match the right keywords or formatting standards get filtered out automatically. ResyMatch simulates this screening to give candidates feedback before submission.

The analysis covers keyword matching (does your resume contain the key terms from the job description?), keyword density (are the important terms appearing enough times?), hard skills matching (specific technical skills listed in the JD), and formatting signals that ATS systems may parse poorly (tables, graphics, non-standard section headers, unusual fonts). The score gives a rough indication of whether the resume would pass initial ATS filtering.

The practical context: the advice to "tailor your resume to each job posting" is grounded in this ATS reality. A generic resume with the same language regardless of job description will score poorly on keyword matching for most postings. ResyMatch operationalizes the tailoring process by showing you exactly which terms are missing. The tool is particularly useful for career changers (where resume language may not naturally match the target industry's terminology) and for any role where the job description uses specific technical vocabulary.

## Key points

- ATS optimization: large-company applicant tracking systems filter resumes before human review — keyword matching is the primary mechanism.
- Keyword gap analysis: shows which terms from the job description are missing or underrepresented in the resume.
- Formatting scan: identifies ATS-hostile formatting (tables, graphics, headers) that may fail parsing.
- Free tool from Cultivated Culture, a career advice brand with content targeting white-collar job seekers.
- Limitation: ATS scoring is a floor, not a ceiling — a perfectly ATS-optimized resume still needs to impress a human reader.
- Complementary tools: JobScan (paid, more detailed ATS simulation), Resume Worded (AI feedback on writing quality).
