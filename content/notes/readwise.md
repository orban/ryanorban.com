## Summary

[Readwise](/notes/readwise/) is a knowledge management tool that solves a specific problem: you read something, highlight it, and then never see it again. The classic highlight graveyard. Readwise imports highlights from Kindle, Instapaper, Pocket, web articles, and physical books (via OCR), then resurfaces them via daily email review. The review mechanism uses spaced repetition — highlights you engage with get shown less frequently, harder ones more often.

The product has two modes. The basic resurfacing mode just shows you past highlights on a schedule. The more powerful feature is Readwise Reader, launched later as a separate product, which is a full read-it-later app built around highlighting and annotation from the start — so the highlight pipeline starts at capture, not after.

[Readwise](/notes/readwise/) became prominent in the personal knowledge management (PKM) space because it integrates with Obsidian, Roam Research, Notion, and other note-taking tools. Highlights flow automatically into your notes graph, allowing you to build connections between ideas across everything you read. This positions it as infrastructure for a Zettelkasten-style or second brain workflow.

## Key points

- Imports highlights from Kindle, Instapaper, Pocket, and physical books (OCR).
- Spaced repetition-driven review: resurfaces highlights at algorithmically optimal intervals.
- Integrates with Obsidian, Roam Research, Notion — makes it a sync layer for a PKM graph.
- Readwise Reader (later addition) is a full read-later app with native highlighting, eliminating the import step.
- Founded by Tristan Homsi and Daniel Doyon; grew significantly during the 2020–2022 PKM tool boom.
