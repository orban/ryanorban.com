## Summary

Weights & Biases (W&B) runs a publication called Fully Connected that covers machine learning research, tutorials, and MLOps practices. Unlike most corporate ML blogs that are product-adjacent, Fully Connected publishes genuine technical content — deep dives into papers, practitioner tutorials, and interactive reports using W&B's own experiment tracking platform. The reports are particularly distinctive: they embed live charts from actual training runs, so you see real data rather than cherry-picked screenshots.

W&B positioned itself as the experiment tracking layer for the ML workflow, and the blog reinforces this by publishing content that demonstrates how to think about training runs, hyperparameter sweeps, and model evaluation. By 2022, the tool had significant adoption at research labs and companies building serious ML systems — the blog reflected that audience by assuming hands-on practitioners rather than ML novices.

The MLOps angle matters: 2022 was when the term was crystallizing around a real set of problems. Getting models to production required reproducibility (tracking what you trained), collaboration (sharing results), and observability (understanding why a run succeeded or failed). W&B's platform addressed all three, and the blog documents the reasoning and patterns behind these practices. As a resource hub it's worth returning to for tutorials on specific techniques.
