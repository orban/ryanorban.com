## Summary

This is the original 2020 bookmark of Eugene Yan's eugeneyan/applied-ml repository on GitHub. The repository curates papers and engineering blog posts from companies sharing how they apply machine learning in production — not research papers optimizing for benchmarks, but production engineering posts from Google, Netflix, Airbnb, LinkedIn, Pinterest, and others describing what they actually deployed.

The vault has a more complete note on this resource from a 2021 bookmark at applied-ml 2021 note — this earlier bookmark is the original discovery. The repository was started in mid-2020 and grew substantially through the year as Eugene Yan actively curated it while working at Amazon. At time of this 2020 save, the repository was newer and smaller, but already organized around the key categories that would define its value: recommendation systems, search ranking, natural language processing, data quality, feature engineering, and experimentation.

The core insight that makes this list valuable: there's a large gap between what gets published in ML research and what actually works in production. Academic papers optimize for novelty and benchmark performance; production engineering posts optimize for reliability, latency, cost, and maintainability under real traffic. This repository sits firmly on the production side.

## Key points

- Original 2020 bookmark of Eugene Yan's applied ML curation on GitHub.
- Categories: recommendations, search, NLP, embeddings, data quality, feature stores, training, serving, monitoring.
- Focus on **production ML**, not academic benchmarks — what companies actually shipped.
- See 2021 vault note for more complete coverage; this bookmark predates some of the repository's growth.
- Eugene Yan was at Amazon during this period; later posts in the repo reflect that industry grounding.
