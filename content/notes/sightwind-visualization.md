An interactive wind visualization website, saved with the note "this is gorgeous." Part of the 2012-2014 wave of beautiful data visualization projects inspired by Fernanda Viégas and Martin Wattenberg's wind map on hint.fm.

These animated weather maps demonstrated that data visualization could be aesthetic objects, not just charts — influencing how the field thought about the relationship between beauty and information clarity.
