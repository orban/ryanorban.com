## Summary

Trail 4Runner published detailed DIY plans for building a dual drawer storage system for the Toyota 4Runner 5th generation. Rather than paying $600-1200 for commercial options like Dobinsons 4Runner drawer system or [DFG Offroad 4Runner drawer](/notes/dfg-offroad-4runner-drawer/), this guide shows how to cut plywood, add drawer slides, and build a functional system yourself for significantly less (typically $150-300 in materials).

The DIY approach offers advantages beyond cost: full dimensional customization to fit specific gear, choice of drawer configuration (deep vs shallow, single vs dual), finish and material choices, and the ability to integrate custom features like portable fridge mounting or specific tool organization. The Toyota 4Runner 5th gen cargo floor is wide and flat, making it well-suited to a shop-built system with standard drawer slides.

The plans provide cut lists, dimensions, and installation sequence. Standard tools (circular saw, drill, jigsaw) are sufficient — no specialized woodworking equipment needed. Baltic birch plywood is the typical recommendation for structural drawer boxes: strong, lightweight, and finishes well with paint or carpet. Ball-bearing drawer slides in 100-lb capacity are the standard hardware choice.

## Key points

- Materials cost ~$150-300 vs $600-1200 for commercial equivalents like Dobinsons 4Runner drawer system
- Baltic birch plywood + 100lb ball-bearing drawer slides: standard DIY material spec
- Custom dimensions: tailor drawer depth and configuration to your specific gear inventory
- Trail 4Runner is a community site with multiple 4Runner-specific build guides and reviews
- Part of the broader overlanding DIY culture: vehicle modifications as craft, not just purchases
