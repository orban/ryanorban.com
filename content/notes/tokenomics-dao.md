## Summary

[Tokenomics DAO](/notes/tokenomics-dao/) is a community organization focused on researching and discussing tokenomics — the economic design of cryptocurrency and Web3 protocols. Tokenomics covers how a protocol's token supply is structured (emission schedules, max supply, burn mechanisms), how tokens are initially distributed (fair launch, VC allocation, airdrops), what utility or governance rights tokens confer, and how these design choices create incentive alignment or misalignment between protocol developers, validators, users, and speculators.

The DAO structure itself is thematically appropriate: the community is studying the same organizational form it uses. By 2022, tokenomics had emerged as a recognized subdiscipline distinct from general crypto analysis — protocols were failing or succeeding partly due to their token designs, and the Terra/Luna collapse (which happened in May 2022) would become a high-profile case study in how flawed tokenomics can destroy a $40B ecosystem. The [Tokenomics DAO](/notes/tokenomics-dao/) positioned itself as a place to develop rigorous frameworks before bad designs get deployed.

The field intersects mechanism design (the branch of economics that asks how to design rules of a game to produce desired outcomes), agent-based modeling (simulating how rational and irrational actors respond to incentives), and cryptography (since the enforcement mechanisms rely on smart contracts). The serious academic thread — called token engineering — draws from control systems engineering and complex systems theory, treating protocol design as an engineering discipline.

## Key points

- Community researching tokenomics — token supply, distribution, utility, governance design for Web3 protocols.
- Sits at the intersection of mechanism design, game theory, and smart contract engineering.
- Token engineering as a discipline: treats protocol design as an engineering problem with formal analysis methods.
- 2022 context: Terra/Luna collapse (May 2022) is the canonical bad-tokenomics case study — unsustainable algorithmic stablecoin design.
- DAO form used for organizing the community studying DAO design — meta layer.
- Related: agent-based modeling for protocol simulation, Gauntlet Network for risk analysis.
