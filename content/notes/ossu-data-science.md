## Summary

Open Source Society University (OSSU) Data Science is a structured, free curriculum for self-taught data science, organized to approximate the depth of a university degree without the cost. It sequences coursework from mathematical prerequisites (linear algebra, calculus, statistics, probability) through programming foundations (Python, SQL) to core machine learning and domain specializations. Each section links to specific courses — primarily from Coursera, edX, and free university course sites.

The design principle is sequencing: rather than a flat list of resources, OSSU tells you what order to study topics in and what mastery looks like before moving on. This addresses the most common failure mode of self-taught ML: skipping foundational math and then hitting walls when reading papers. The curriculum includes Andrew Ng's Machine Learning Specialization on Coursera, various Johns Hopkins data science courses, and MIT OpenCourseWare mathematics.

Compared to other learning paths, OSSU is more rigorous and slower than [fast.ai](/notes/fastai/)'s top-down approach. It's the right choice for someone who wants genuine depth — able to read and implement papers — rather than quick practical results. The GitHub repo is actively maintained with community discussion on completions, alternatives, and pacing.

## Key points

- Free, self-paced curriculum that sequences from math prerequisites through advanced machine learning — designed as a complete data science education.
- Starts with linear algebra, calculus, probability, and statistics before touching ML code.
- Includes Andrew Ng's courses plus MIT, Johns Hopkins, and Stanford offerings.
- Complement to [fast.ai](/notes/fastai/): OSSU is bottom-up (foundations first), [fast.ai](/notes/fastai/) is top-down (results first).
- Community-driven on GitHub with discussion threads on pacing, alternatives, and completions.
