## Summary

A personal photo from a wearables gathering at Chartio — a business intelligence startup based in San Francisco — in late June 2013. The event brought together people interested in early consumer wearable devices: Fitbit, Jawbone UP, Nike FuelBand, and similar devices were the conversation topics about a year before Apple Watch changed the category.

Chartio (founded by Dave Fowler) was a BI tool focused on making SQL accessible to non-engineers. Their San Francisco office was a common venue for tech community events in this era. The cross-section of wearables and analytics people at a BI company's office was natural — quantified self data from wearables needed somewhere to go, and analytics tools were the obvious destination.

This is a personal artifact: a snapshot of Ryan's social and professional world in San Francisco's tech community in mid-2013.

## Key points

- Chartio: San Francisco BI startup that made SQL-based analytics accessible without requiring a data engineering team — later acquired by Atlassian (2021).
- 2013 wearables moment: about 12 months before Apple Watch announcement; the ecosystem was fragmented across Fitbit, Jawbone, Nike, and Basis.
- Quantified self community: people actively tracking sleep, steps, and activity data — looking for ways to analyze it beyond vendor apps.
