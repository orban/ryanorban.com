## Summary

Derek Sivers maintains a public collection of notes from every book he's read — over 250 books at the time of saving, spanning business, psychology, philosophy, science, and music. Each entry is a concise summary of the book's key ideas filtered through Sivers' perspective, not a neutral recap. He includes his personal rating, his takeaway sentences, and often his own commentary on where he agrees or disagrees.

Sivers is the founder of CD Baby (sold in 2008), author of *Anything You Want* and *How to Live*, and one of the more thoughtful public writers about business, learning, and life design. His book notes are valuable not just as summaries but as a window into how a specific type of curious, entrepreneurial thinker processes information. The curation is opinionated — some books get three sentences, others get pages of extracted insight.

The collection functions as a reading list and as a filter: if Sivers found something important enough to note, it's likely worth considering. If his notes reveal that a book's key idea can be captured in two sentences, that tells you something about whether reading the whole book would be worth your time. The format (public personal knowledge management) was ahead of the Obsidian / second brain wave.

## Key points

- 250+ book notes covering business, psychology, philosophy, science, and music — filtered through Sivers' lens.
- Personal takeaways, not neutral summaries — includes his ratings and where he agrees/disagrees.
- From Derek Sivers: CD Baby founder, author of *Anything You Want* and *How to Live*.
- Useful as a reading list filter: Sivers' curation signals which books deliver concentrated ideas.
- An early public personal knowledge management system before the Obsidian / Roam era.
