## Summary

Alethea AI is an AI company that in 2022 was building what it called iNFTs — Intelligent NFTs — by embedding AI personalities into NFT characters. The concept: an NFT is typically a static digital asset, but an iNFT includes an embedded AI model that gives it a conversational personality, the ability to respond to questions, and a backstory that can evolve based on interactions. The character is both a collectible and an interactive agent.

The Messari report covers Alethea's technical and business architecture. The AI layer is separate from the blockchain ownership layer: NFT holders own a token (on-chain, transferable) that gates access to the associated AI personality (off-chain, hosted by Alethea). This split creates both capability (you can update the AI independently) and dependency (the experience requires Alethea to remain operational). The underlying AI was using GPT-3 for the conversational layer.

In 2022 context, Alethea was an early experiment at the intersection of generative AI and NFTs — an intersection that got more interesting as text and image generation capabilities improved rapidly. The idea of AI characters tied to blockchain ownership anticipated later discussions around AI agents with persistent identities, digital twins, and AI companions. The execution was limited by 2022-era AI capabilities and the market dynamics of the NFT bubble, but the design space the project explored became more relevant as AI improved.

## Key points

- Alethea AI's iNFTs: NFT ownership token gates access to an embedded AI personality — static asset + dynamic agent
- Architecture split: on-chain ownership (transferable, provenance) + off-chain AI layer (updatable, hosted)
- 2022 AI layer was GPT-3-based — limited capability vs. later models
- The concept anticipated AI agents with persistent identities, digital companions, and character NFTs
- Platform risk: the AI experience depends on Alethea remaining operational — unlike pure on-chain assets
- Historical context: peak NFT era (2021-2022) combined with early accessible generative AI — the ideas matured faster than the market did
