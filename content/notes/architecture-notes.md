## Summary

[Architecture Notes](/notes/architecture-notes/) is a technical Substack newsletter by Mahdi Yusuf covering system design and software architecture — deep dives on distributed systems, database internals, engineering leadership, and large-scale system patterns. The newsletter publishes weekly and has 29,000+ subscribers.

The format targets software engineers who want to understand architecture at a level beyond tutorials — not just here's how to use Kafka but "here's why Kafka makes the trade-offs it does, and when those trade-offs are wrong for your situation." Mahdi Yusuf has a distributed systems background and writes from an engineering practice perspective, not purely academic.

Good system design education was relatively scarce when [Architecture Notes](/notes/architecture-notes/) launched. Technical interview prep resources (System Design Interview by Alex Xu, Designing Data-Intensive Applications by Martin Kleppmann) were popular but structured around interviews rather than architectural reasoning. [Architecture Notes](/notes/architecture-notes/) fills the practitioner education gap — the kind of content experienced engineers discuss in design review meetings, made accessible to mid-level engineers building their mental models.

## Key points

- Weekly Substack on system design and software architecture by Mahdi Yusuf.
- Focus: distributed systems, database internals, engineering leadership — practitioner-level depth.
- 29,000+ subscribers; recognized as a go-to source for architectural deep dives.
- Sits between interview prep (System Design Interview) and academic papers — practical engineering reasoning.
- Useful complement to Designing Data-Intensive Applications (DDIA) by Martin Kleppmann.
- Covers the why behind architectural choices, not just the what.
