## Summary

Published by Variant Fund, this is a case study in how [Magic Eden](/notes/magic-eden/) became the dominant NFT marketplace on Solana, reaching unicorn valuation in 2022. The framing is around three factors: transparency in how the platform operates and makes decisions, creativity in their launch mechanics and collection curation, and community — treating creators and collectors as participants rather than just users.

[Magic Eden](/notes/magic-eden/) launched in September 2021 and grew to capture over 90% of Solana NFT trading volume within months. The competitive dynamic was interesting: OpenSea was dominant on Ethereum but largely ignored Solana, creating a greenfield opportunity. [Magic Eden](/notes/magic-eden/) filled it by being faster (exploiting Solana's lower fees and transaction speeds), more responsive to creators, and quicker to ship features the community wanted. They launched a launchpad program for new collections that became the default distribution mechanism for Solana projects.

The article reflects the peak optimism of mid-2022 — written by investors at Variant at a moment when NFT market caps were declining but before the FTX collapse and broader crypto winter fully set in. The analysis of how Magic Eden won is reasonable as a marketplace playbook, but the context matters: Solana NFT volume later collapsed significantly, and Magic Eden had to pivot toward multi-chain support as their core market shrank. A useful artifact of the bull market thesis, worth reading against subsequent events.

## Key points

- [Magic Eden](/notes/magic-eden/) captured 90%+ of Solana NFT volume through speed, fees, and creator focus
- Three pillars: transparency (clear royalty rules), creativity (launchpad program), community (creator relationships)
- Exploited OpenSea's neglect of Solana — first-mover on a fast-growing chain
- Launchpad mechanic became the default distribution for new Solana projects
- Variant Fund thesis: community-owned platforms have defensible network effects
- Written at peak: later had to pivot to multi-chain as Solana NFT market contracted sharply
