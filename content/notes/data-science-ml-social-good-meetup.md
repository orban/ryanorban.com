## Summary

This SF Data Science meetup on "Data Science and Machine Learning for Social Good" was organized with Zipfian Academy involvement in March 2014. It reflects a genuine thread running through the San Francisco data science community at the time: many practitioners had strong social motivations alongside technical interests, and wanted to see machine learning applied to problems beyond consumer optimization.

The meetup overlaps with the launch period of Bayes Impact and [DataDonors](/notes/datadonors/) — all part of a cluster of "data for good" activity in early 2014. Zipfian Academy students who came from non-traditional backgrounds (NGO workers, public health researchers, educators) were often the most interested in these connections between technical skills and social impact.

## Key points

- Zipfian Academy participated as organizer and community bridge for this social good–focused meetup.
- Contemporaneous with Bayes Impact and [DataDonors](/notes/datadonors/) launches — a coordinated moment in data-for-good.
- SF Data Science meetup group was a key community venue throughout the 2013-2016 era.
