## Summary

This community-produced guide appeared in mid-2022 as DALL-E 2 access was expanding beyond the initial waitlist. At the time, prompt engineering for image generation was a genuine craft SKILL — small phrasing changes could produce dramatically different outputs, and the community was rapidly discovering which artist names, style descriptors, and compositional modifiers reliably shaped results.

The prompt book is essentially a systematic exploration of the DALL-E 2 latent space from the prompt side. It catalogs: artistic styles by named artists (Greg Rutkowski, Artstation aesthetic), photographic terms (lens types, lighting setups, film stocks), historical art movements (impressionism, art nouveau, brutalism), and rendering styles (octane render, unreal engine, ray tracing). These combinations became the vocabulary of early AI image generation — a jargon that spread across Midjourney, Stable Diffusion, and every text-to-image model that followed.

Looking back from 2026, this kind of prompt book is a historical artifact. The era of needing specific incantations to get quality outputs is largely over — modern models understand intent more directly. But in July 2022, the gap between a mediocre and a stunning DALL-E output could be a single well-chosen modifier. The book captured that knowledge when it was still being actively discovered.

## Key points

- Systematic catalog of style terms, artist references, and compositional modifiers for DALL-E 2
- Greg Rutkowski and Artstation aesthetic became dominant style references in the community during this period
- Rendering terms (octane render, unreal engine 5, ray tracing) reliably improved photorealism in early models
- The vocabulary in this book spread to Midjourney and Stable Diffusion as those launched later in 2022
- Historical context: model capability has since improved such that explicit style incantations are less necessary
