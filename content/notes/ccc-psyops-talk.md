## Summary

Trevor Paglen (artist and researcher known for surveillance and intelligence work) delivered this talk at 37C3 (the 37th Chaos Communication Congress) exploring the historical continuity between Cold War psychological operations and contemporary algorithmic manipulation. The title is provocative by design — the talk argues that modern audiences are subjected to military-grade cognitive influence techniques at scale, normalized through advertising and social media.

The historical thread runs from declassified CIA programs involving mind control, UFOs, and remote influence (Project MKUltra and related programs) to the present-day landscape of algorithmic targeting, generative AI synthetic media, and social media manipulation. Paglen introduces the frame of "PSYOP Capitalism" — the idea that the same techniques developed to manipulate enemy populations have been productized and deployed commercially, with individual psychological profiles as the targeting layer.

The talk doubles as a critique of generative AI's role in this ecosystem: synthetic media makes it dramatically cheaper to create convincing false content, and algorithmic distribution systems are optimized for engagement rather than truth — a combination that accelerates manipulation capability. The interactive CYCLOPS game running through the conference (framed as a fictional CIA cognitive warfare program) makes the audience complicit in exploring these dynamics. Paglen's work connects to broader debates about disinformation, information warfare, and the ethics of AI development.

## Key points

- PSYOP Capitalism = Cold War psychological operations techniques commercialized via algorithmic targeting.
- Historical arc from MKUltra and classified mind-control programs to modern behavioral targeting.
- Generative AI + distribution algorithms = accelerated manufacturing of false content at scale.
- CYCLOPS interactive game threads through 37C3 — audience participates in the argument.
- Trevor Paglen: artist/researcher known for CIA surveillance and intelligence documentation.
