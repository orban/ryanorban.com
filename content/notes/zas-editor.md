## Summary

Zas (marketed as Zeditor at this URL) is a minimal macOS writing and markdown editor targeting writers who want a focused, distraction-free environment. It sits in the crowded space between Bear (feature-rich, iOS/macOS, tag-based), iA Writer (strict minimalism, typography focus), and Obsidian (graph-based PKM) — positioned as clean and capable without the complexity of full PKM tools.

The differentiator in this category is typically a combination of typography, editing experience, and export quality. Minimal markdown editors compete on how good the writing feels rather than feature count — iA Writer's success with its custom monospace fonts established that writers will pay for a superior typing experience. The challenge is that VS Code with a minimal theme covers most technical writing needs for free, and Obsidian covers the PKM use case. Purpose-built writing apps like Zas/Zeditor need a strong UX reason to exist in that gap.

The app appears to target the same audience as Typora (WYSIWYG markdown) and Craft (structured documents for the Apple ecosystem) — people who want native macOS feel, good typography, and markdown output without the heavyweight feature sets of document editors.

## Key points

- Minimal markdown editor for macOS — distraction-free writing without PKM overhead.
- Competes with iA Writer, Bear, Typora, Craft in the writing-focused macOS app category.
- The writing app market is crowded; differentiation requires excellent typography and editing feel.
- Markdown-based: plain text storage, portable, no vendor lock-in on the file format.
- Target: writers who want a dedicated tool rather than a general-purpose editor with a minimal theme.
