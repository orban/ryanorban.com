## Summary

Goldilocks Zone Finder by Luke Champine is a tool for finding locations with comfortable year-round temperatures — named after the astrophysics concept of a habitable zone where conditions are not too hot and not too cold. It displays monthly temperature data (min, max, average) in both Fahrenheit and Celsius for different locations, letting you compare climates across a full calendar year at a glance.

The practical use case: digital nomads, remote workers, and people considering relocation often want to know not just is it warm? but "what does January look like? Is August bearable?" Standard weather sites give you today's temperature or seasonal averages, but rarely a clean comparative view of the full temperature range across all 12 months side by side. The Goldilocks framing is apt — most people don't want extremes in either direction.

This fits the broader category of personal data tools for life decisions — small, single-purpose web tools that make a specific lookup significantly easier than the standard approach (Googling each city separately, mentally stitching together data from multiple sources). Luke Champine is known for thoughtful, minimalist software (he's also the author of siad and the Sia blockchain).

## Key points

- Monthly temperature grid: min/max/average by month, full year visible at once.
- Supports both °F and °C — useful for international comparisons.
- Purpose-built for the find a livable climate use case vs. general weather apps.
- Minimalist design — fast to use, no account, no friction.
- Luke Champine also known for Sia (decentralized storage) and Go ecosystem contributions.
