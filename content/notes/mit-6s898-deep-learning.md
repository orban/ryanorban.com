## Summary

MIT 6.S898 is a graduate deep learning course taught by Phillip Isola, a professor at MIT CSAIL known for research on image translation (pix2pix), self-supervised learning, and visual representations. The Fall 2022 edition has publicly available course materials — lectures, slides, and assignments.

The course covers the core deep learning curriculum: fundamentals (gradient descent, backpropagation, neural network architectures), then modern components (convolutional networks, transformers, attention mechanisms), then generative models (GANs, VAEs, diffusion models). The curriculum was updated to reflect the 2022 state of the field — meaning diffusion models were covered as first-class content, not footnotes.

Isola's research background in visual representations shows in the course's emphasis on understanding what neural networks actually learn, not just how to train them. This is graduate-level material assuming linear algebra, probability, and basic ML familiarity — not an entry point for beginners.

## Key points

- MIT graduate deep learning course, Fall 2022, taught by Phillip Isola.
- Covers fundamentals through modern architectures: CNNs, Transformers, diffusion models, GANs.
- Public course materials — lectures, slides, and problem sets available online.
- Emphasis on understanding what networks learn, influenced by Isola's research on representations.
- Graduate-level: assumes linear algebra, probability theory, and basic ML.
- Phillip Isola is known for pix2pix and self-supervised visual learning research.
