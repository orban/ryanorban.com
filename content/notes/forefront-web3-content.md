## Summary

Forefront is a Web3-native media platform and community focused on social tokens, DAOs, and the broader tokenized community ecosystem. The FF token is both a governance mechanism and a membership credential — token holders get access to deeper content, community channels, and governance votes. This structure makes Forefront itself an example of the thing it covers: a token-gated community organized as a DAO.

The editorial focus in 2021-2022 was the intersection of social tokens and online communities — the thesis that tokens would transform how creators monetize relationships and how communities coordinate. Rally, Mirror, and Friends with Benefits (FWB) were the flagship examples: FWB in particular was widely cited as evidence that crypto communities could sustain real cultural production. Forefront covered these communities sympathetically but with more depth than crypto marketing materials — the writing engaged with governance failures, economic unsustainability, and the gap between community rhetoric and practical coordination.

The platform reflects a broader pattern in the 2022 Web3 media ecosystem: outlets funded by token sales or VC investment into Web3 media, covering a space they're also participating in. This creates obvious tension between editorial independence and community loyalty. Forefront was more self-aware about this than many peers. The subsequent crypto bear market (2022-2023) significantly reduced attention to social tokens as a category — many tokens lost 90%+ of their value, and the active communities shrank dramatically. Forefront's continued existence through the bear market would be a signal of genuine community health.

## Key points

- Token-gated community: FF token = access + governance + membership signal — Forefront practices what it covers.
- Editorial focus: social tokens, DAO governance, tokenized creator communities — the community primitives thesis.
- Friends with Benefits (FWB) and Rally were flagship examples covered — both experienced significant bear market decline.
- Represents crypto-native media funded and governed by its own token — embedded in the ecosystem it covers.
- Mirror.xyz (decentralized publishing platform) is a complementary tool Forefront writers use.
- The bear market test: outlets funded by token value face structural challenges when tokens decline — distinguishes mission-driven from speculation-driven media.
