## Summary

[Per Prompt](/notes/per-prompt/) is a weekly newsletter curating interesting news, tools, and developments in LLMs, GPT, and AI broadly. It appeared during the peak of the early 2023 AI news explosion, when the pace of releases (ChatGPT, GPT-4, LLaMA, Alpaca, AutoGPT) made it difficult to stay current without following dozens of sources.

Curation newsletters in fast-moving technical fields serve a real function: they do the signal-from-noise work that individual subscribers don't have time for, filtering from hundreds of announcements, papers, and HN threads down to the things actually worth knowing about. [Per Prompt](/notes/per-prompt/)'s weekly cadence is appropriate for the space — daily would create fatigue, monthly would miss important developments.

The format — weekly digest, AI/LLM focused — had significant competition in 2023: The Rundown AI, AI Breakfast, TLDR AI, The Neuron, and many others. The field was crowded because the audience was large and the barrier to entry was low. Whether any specific newsletter provides durable value depends on the quality of the curation and the curator's perspective.

## Key points

- Weekly AI/LLM newsletter for staying current without following the full firehose.
- Appeared during the 2023 AI news explosion — rapid release cadence drove newsletter demand.
- Competes with The Rundown AI, TLDR AI, AI Breakfast, and many other AI curation newsletters.
- The value of any curation newsletter is the quality of selection and editorial perspective.
