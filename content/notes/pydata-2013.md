PyData 2013 talk materials from Martin Laprise. PyData is the conference series run by NumFOCUS covering the Python data ecosystem — NumPy, Pandas, SciPy, scikit-learn, Jupyter, and related tools.

By 2013 the Python data science stack was mature enough that PyData had become the primary community venue for practitioners to share techniques and tools, competing with SciPy conference and PyCon tutorials.
