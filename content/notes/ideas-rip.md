## Summary

Ideas.rip is a platform that catalogs and curates startup ideas across sectors: AI/LLM tools, B2B SaaS, healthcare, e-commerce, construction, defense, and others. The model: a browseable, filterable database of problem spaces and concepts that potential founders can explore, with a submission form for community contribution.

The value proposition of a curated idea list is contested. Ideas themselves are cheap — execution is what matters — and a public list of good ideas immediately creates a coordination problem (everyone sees the same list). The site seems to position itself more as a discovery tool for problem spaces than a here's your startup generator: exploring categories can surface domains where you weren't aware of the pain points, which is useful early-stage research.

Examples like "LLM Analytics for Restaurant Chains or AI Ambient Documentation for Nursing Homes" show the level of specificity: these are niche vertical applications of AI rather than general concepts. That specificity is more useful than build an AI chatbot — it points at specific workflows with specific users.

## Key points

- Catalogues startup ideas across AI/LLM, B2B SaaS, healthcare, e-commerce, and specialized verticals.
- Filterable by category and updated with new submissions.
- Specificity is the value — niche vertical applications rather than generic concepts.
- Useful as a domain discovery tool rather than a here's your idea generator.
- Community submission model: anyone can suggest ideas.
