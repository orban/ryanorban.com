## Summary

Save All is an AI-augmented bookmarking tool that tries to solve the classic problem with read-it-later apps like Pocket and Instapaper: saved content that's never revisited. The AI layer summarizes saved pages, extracts key concepts, and surfaces relevant bookmarks when you're working on related topics.

The core insight: the failure mode of bookmark managers isn't saving — it's retrieval. Most people have hundreds of bookmarks they never return to because finding the right one requires remembering that you saved it and searching for it. AI summarization + semantic search addresses this by making the collection query-able without remembering exact titles or URLs.

Save All sits in a category that also includes Readwise Reader, Heyday (which auto-surfaced relevant bookmarks as you browsed), and Reeder 5. The 2022 wave of AI-augmented PKM tools all attacked this same retrieval problem from slightly different angles. The common theme: making knowledge capture useful requires surfacing it back at the right moment.

## Key points

- AI-powered bookmarking that summarizes and surfaces saved content.
- Addresses read-it-later failure mode: content saved but never revisited.
- Semantic search over your saved content — find bookmarks by topic, not just keywords.
- Part of the 2022 wave of AI-augmented PKM tools.
- Competes with Readwise Reader, Heyday, and Pocket.
- Core thesis: capture is easy, retrieval is the hard problem.
