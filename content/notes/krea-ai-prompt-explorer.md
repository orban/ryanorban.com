## Summary

Krea.ai launched in August 2022 as a gallery and search engine for Stable Diffusion-generated images with their prompts attached. The core functionality: search by style, subject, or keyword to browse community-generated images; click any image to see the full prompt that produced it; use that prompt as a starting point for your own generations. It addressed a real friction point in the early Stable Diffusion ecosystem — generating good images required knowing which keywords and modifiers worked, and that knowledge was scattered across Discord servers, Reddit threads, and personal experimentation.

Krea.ai aggregated community prompt knowledge into a searchable corpus. The site initially featured images generated with Stable Diffusion, but the format anticipated what became Lexica, PromptBase, and the broader prompt marketplace ecosystem. The discovery problem was real: the difference between a mediocre Stable Diffusion output and a striking one often came down to specific artist names, rendering terms, and quality keywords that weren't obvious from the model's documentation.

Over time, Krea.ai evolved toward real-time AI image generation — streaming generations as you type — becoming a more interactive tool than a gallery. The initial prompt gallery use case was eventually eclipsed by the proliferation of Midjourney (which has its own community gallery) and tools like Civitai that focused on model sharing and prompt discovery for the AUTOMATIC1111 ecosystem.

## Key points

- Gallery and search engine for Stable Diffusion images with prompts — browse community generations to discover working prompt patterns.
- Solved the prompt discovery problem: how do you learn which keywords produce which styles?
- Predecessor to Lexica, PromptBase, and the broader prompt engineering marketplace.
- Later pivoted toward real-time streaming image generation as the product evolved.
- Community-sourced prompt knowledge at scale — aggregated what Discord/Reddit had fragmented.
- Illustrates the information asymmetry in early generative AI: knowing the right prompts mattered enormously.
