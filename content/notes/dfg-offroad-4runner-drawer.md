## Summary

DFG Offroad makes a bolt-in rear drawer system for the Toyota 4Runner 5th generation (2010+). The system provides organized cargo storage by converting the flat cargo area into drawers that slide out, letting you access gear without climbing into the back or digging through a pile. All hardware is included for a direct install — no cutting or drilling required for the basic fit.

The target use case is overlanding and truck camping: gear that you want accessible in the field (recovery equipment, cooking gear, tools, first aid) lives in the drawers, while larger items like sleeping bags and tents can go on top. The drawer system also serves as a raised platform for sleeping inside the vehicle — a flat sleeping surface at the height of the cargo floor is a significant quality-of-life improvement over sleeping on a lumpy uneven floor.

This was saved alongside Dobinsons 4Runner drawer system, [DIY 4Runner drawer plans](/notes/diy-4runner-drawer-plans/), and the [Nomad Kitchen 4Runner](/notes/nomad-kitchen-4runner/) as part of a single research session on 4Runner cargo organization. The DFG Offroad unit is a simpler/less expensive commercial option compared to Dobinsons; the DIY option offers customization at lower cost.

## Key points

- Bolt-in installation: no cutting, all hardware included — simpler install than some competitors
- Creates organized drawer storage from flat cargo area; top surface usable for sleeping platform
- 5th gen 4Runner specific fit (2010-present) — different cargo dimensions from 3rd/4th gen
- Commercial alternative to [DIY 4Runner drawer plans](/notes/diy-4runner-drawer-plans/); compare with Dobinsons 4Runner drawer system for higher-spec option
- Overlanding use case: keeps frequently-accessed recovery and camp gear in separate drawers from packed gear
