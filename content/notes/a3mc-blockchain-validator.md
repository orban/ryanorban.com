## Summary

A3MC (art3mis.cloud) is an independent blockchain research laboratory and validator operation run by a decentralized international team. Their primary infrastructure focus is running validators across multiple proof-of-stake networks — most notably Solana, where they offer staking delegation at approximately 7.5% APY with 0% MEV commission and claimed 99.99% uptime backed by PCI DSS and SOC certifications.

Beyond Solana, the team participates in Avalanche, The Graph Protocol, MultiversX (formerly Elrond), Mina Protocol, and Casper Network — reflecting a multi-chain validator strategy rather than betting on a single ecosystem. They also develop validator analytics tooling and contribute to network research initiatives, positioning as infrastructure operators who build software rather than pure capital allocators.

The unusual detail about the organization: alongside blockchain infrastructure, they run a long-running charitable program feeding over 1,000 cats daily across multiple continents through a decentralized volunteer network. This combination of validator ops and animal welfare is either genuine mission diversity or an effective differentiation signal in a crowded validator market.

## Key points

- Solana validator with ~7.5% APY, 0% MEV commission, 99.99% uptime claim — enterprise-grade infrastructure setup.
- Multi-chain presence: Avalanche, The Graph, MultiversX, Mina Protocol, Casper Network.
- Builds validator analytics tooling alongside operating nodes.
- Independent, decentralized team — not a VC-backed infrastructure company.
- Related context: Proof of Stake validator economics, MEV (maximal extractable value), staking yield dynamics.
