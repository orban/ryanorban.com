## Summary

[Photofeeler](/notes/photofeeler/) is a platform that collects blind ratings on profile photos from real users, giving you data-backed feedback on how your photos are perceived before you use them on LinkedIn, dating apps, or social profiles. The core insight: photo selection is subjective, and your own judgment about which photo looks best is systematically biased — you're evaluating familiarity and self-recognition rather than first-impression perception. Photofeeler provides what looks like the first impression from strangers.

The rating dimensions vary by context. For business/LinkedIn photos, raters evaluate competence, likability, and influence — the professional signal a headshot sends. For social and dating contexts, the metrics shift toward attractiveness and approachability. This contextual calibration makes the feedback more actionable: knowing a photo scores high on competence but low on warmth tells you something specific about which use case it fits.

The mechanism is a reciprocal rating economy: you earn credits by rating other people's photos, which you then spend to get your own photos rated. This creates organic volume without requiring payment (though paid credits are available for faster results). The blind rating approach — raters see the photo without context about the submitter — reduces social pressure to be kind and produces more honest feedback. The practical application is pre-selecting the highest-performing photo before updating a professional profile rather than guessing.

## Key points

- Blind ratings from real users on first-impression qualities — competence, likability, influence (professional), attractiveness (social).
- Reciprocal credit system: rate others to earn credits; prevents freeloading, creates organic volume.
- Addresses systematic self-selection bias: you can't judge your own first impression; strangers can.
- Business vs. social modes: different rating dimensions calibrated to context of use.
- Practical use: A/B test profile photos before committing to one for LinkedIn, professional headshot selection.
- Similar tools: TestMyProfile, manual feedback from friends (less blind, hence less useful).
