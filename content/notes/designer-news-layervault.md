## Summary

Designer News was a Hacker News-style link aggregator and community forum specifically for the design industry, built by LayerVault. LayerVault was a version control and collaboration tool for design files — an attempt to give designers something like GitHub for Photoshop and Sketch files. Designer News launched in early 2013 and quickly became one of the primary community hubs for UI design and UX practitioners.

The site filled a gap: Hacker News was technically dominated, Reddit's design subreddits were broad and varied, and there was no equivalent community for practitioners who cared about interface design, typography, design process, and design tools. Designer News developed a distinctive culture — more thoughtful than Dribbble, more design-specific than Hacker News.

LayerVault itself had an interesting trajectory: it pioneered design version control before Abstract, Plant, and Kactus existed, and before Figma made real-time collaboration standard. The company shut down in 2015 when it couldn't reach the necessary scale; Designer News was acquired and continued independently for several years before eventually declining as Dribbble, Twitter, and other platforms absorbed the community.

## Key points

- LayerVault: version control for design files (PSD, Sketch); predated Abstract and Figma by years; closed 2015
- Designer News peaked 2013-2015 as the primary link-sharing forum for UI/UX designers
- Community culture: skewed toward practitioners over students; higher signal-to-noise than design subreddits
- Hacker News model: karma, upvoting, comment threads — same mechanism applied to design industry news
- After LayerVault closed, Designer News was sold; continued but eventually lost community to Twitter and Figma's community features
