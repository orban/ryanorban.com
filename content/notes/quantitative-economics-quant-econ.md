## Summary

quant-econ.net is a free online textbook and course by Thomas Sargent (Nobel laureate in economics, 2011) and John Stachurski teaching quantitative economics using Python. It covered dynamic programming, Markov chains, stochastic processes, linear state space models, and time series analysis — the mathematical toolkit of modern macroeconomics and econometrics — all implemented in Python code.

The course was significant on two levels. First, it brought economics into the Python scientific computing ecosystem at a time when most academic economists used Matlab or R. Second, it made graduate-level quantitative economics accessible for free, authored by one of the most prominent names in the field. Sargent's involvement signaled that Python was a legitimate tool for serious economic research, not just data wrangling.

The QuantEcon project (the organization behind quant-econ.net) later expanded into a broader community and added Julia versions alongside Python. The approach — starting from economic theory, implementing in code, building up from first principles — influenced how data scientists from economics backgrounds understood the relationship between their mathematical training and computational tools.

## Key points

- Thomas Sargent (Nobel Economics 2011) and John Stachurski authored this free Python-based quantitative economics curriculum.
- Covers dynamic programming, Markov chains, stochastic processes, and econometric models — graduate-level content, free and in Python.
- Helped legitimize Python as a tool for serious economic research, challenging Matlab's dominance in academic economics.
- The QuantEcon project is the foundation; later expanded to Julia and broader economic modeling tools.
