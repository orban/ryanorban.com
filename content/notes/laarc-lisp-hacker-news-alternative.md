## Summary

laarc was a Hacker News-style link aggregator and discussion community oriented toward Lisp, functional programming, and programming language theory. The name combines "Lisp" and Arc — Arc being Paul Graham's Lisp dialect (the same language Hacker News itself runs on). The community attracted programmers interested in language theory, SICP, Clojure, Racket, and adjacent topics.

The site appears to have shut down or pivoted significantly after 2020 — the domain now redirects to an unrelated service. At the time of saving, it was a niche aggregator serving a specific subset of the programming community that found HN's content too broad and wanted a higher-signal environment focused on PL theory and functional programming topics.

Link aggregators with topic focus (versus HN's general tech scope) have struggled to maintain critical mass — the network effects of a large community like HN are hard to replicate even with better topic alignment.

## Key points

- Built on Arc (Paul Graham's Lisp dialect) — a community eating its own dog food, appealing to the self-referential nature of Lisp culture.
- Intended audience: programming language researchers, Clojure and Racket practitioners, SICP readers, and adjacent communities.
- The site appears defunct as of 2020-2021 — a common fate for niche link aggregators that don't reach critical community mass.
- Reflects a broader pattern: Lobsters (programming-focused) and Tildes are examples of HN alternatives that have had more staying power through tighter community governance.
