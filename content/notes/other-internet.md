## Summary

[Other Internet](/notes/other-internet/) is a research collective and studio that studies emerging internet-native institutions — primarily DAOs, crypto protocols, and new forms of online coordination and community. The output is longform research and essays that engage seriously with both the technical mechanisms and the social/organizational implications. The tone is more academic than promotional, making it one of the more rigorous sources in a space full of marketing content.

Their work addresses questions that most crypto media ignores: how do online communities actually make decisions? What happens to DAO governance when financial incentives dominate? How does token distribution shape community culture? What does ownership mean in a web3-native context? They published influential pieces on DAOs as corporations, on the sociology of crypto Twitter, and on the gap between the rhetoric and practice of decentralization.

The studio model means they also take client work — research projects and consulting for protocols and organizations navigating these questions. The research function and the commercial function are related but distinct. Key figures associated with [Other Internet](/notes/other-internet/) include Toby Shorin and Laura Lotti, whose writing on post-brand societies and crypto-native organizations circulated widely in the DAO research community.

## Key points

- Research focus: social, cultural, and organizational dimensions of DAOs and internet-native institutions
- More rigorous and empirical than typical crypto commentary — engages with sociology and organizational theory
- Influential pieces on: DAO governance failures, token distribution and community culture, limits of decentralization rhetoric
- Studio model: research + consulting for protocols navigating organizational questions
- Associated thinkers: Toby Shorin, Laura Lotti — post-brand societies, crypto-native org theory
