## Summary

The altLINE equity simulator is an interactive calculator that models how equity dilution works across early-stage funding rounds. Enter your initial ownership percentages, add a funding round (investment amount, pre-money valuation), and it shows how each party's ownership percentage changes after the round closes. The tool makes the math behind cap table dilution transparent without requiring a spreadsheet.

The problem it solves: first-time founders often don't have an intuitive grasp of how raising money changes their ownership. A $1M investment on a $4M pre-money valuation means selling 20% — and that 20% comes from everyone's existing stake proportionally (new investors are added, everyone else dilutes). Running multiple rounds through the simulator makes it concrete: how does a seed round plus Series A affect the original founders? What happens to employee stock option pool (ESOP) dilution?

This isn't a sophisticated financial modeling tool — it doesn't handle convertible notes, SAFE agreements, pro-rata rights, or liquidation preferences. But for founders who haven't internalized the basic dilution math, it's a useful first step before engaging with lawyers and investors. The full picture requires tools like Carta or Capshare for actual cap table management, and understanding instruments like YC SAFEs and convertible notes is essential before any real funding conversation.

## Key points

- Models equity dilution across funding rounds: enter investment amount + pre-money valuation to see ownership changes.
- Makes cap table math transparent for first-time founders unfamiliar with how dilution works.
- Limitations: doesn't model convertible notes, SAFE agreements, pro-rata rights, or liquidation preferences.
- Entry-level tool — real cap table management requires Carta or Capshare.
- Useful starting point before engaging with term sheets and investor negotiations.
- Context: YC SAFE and convertible instruments are how most early-stage rounds work — this tool only models priced rounds.
