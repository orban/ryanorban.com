## Summary

Evan Conrad takes an honest look at which crypto use cases have genuine product-market fit versus which remain primarily speculative or circular. Written in mid-2022 — during a market downturn that was forcing the industry to reckon with what actually worked — the post distinguishes between use cases where blockchain provides a real improvement over alternatives and cases where it's mostly narrative.

The genuine use cases Conrad identifies tend to cluster around a few themes: financial access where traditional systems fail (remittances to countries with poor banking infrastructure, dollar-denominated savings in hyperinflationary economies), and DeFi primitives that provide new financial capabilities (permissionless lending via Aave, on-chain price discovery via AMMs). The argument is that stablecoins like USDC and USDT are arguably the biggest success story — providing dollar access to people who can't easily hold USD through traditional banking.

The weaker use cases are more familiar: NFTs as status symbols, DAOs for governance of on-chain protocols (works) versus real-world organizations (mostly doesn't), Web3 social networks, and most of the "insert traditional product but on a blockchain" category. The piece is useful because it was written at a moment when speculative excess was deflating, forcing clearer thinking about which use cases could sustain without the bull market tailwind.

## Key points

- Genuine PMF: remittances, stablecoins for dollar access in high-inflation economies, DeFi primitives (AMM, permissionless lending)
- Stablecoins (USDC, USDT) are possibly the most successful crypto product — dollarization without a bank account
- DeFi works for on-chain-native use cases; fails when trying to represent off-chain assets on-chain (the oracle problem)
- NFTs have cultural value but slim non-speculative use cases in 2022
- DAO governance works for on-chain protocols; struggles for off-chain coordination
- Honest framing: crypto doesn't need a killer app for every use case — a few genuine wins are enough
