## Summary

[Made With ML](/notes/made-with-ml/) (created by Goku Mohandas) is a free, open-source curriculum that covers machine learning from foundations through production deployment, with particular depth on MLOps. The curriculum emphasizes the full production pipeline rather than just model training — data versioning, experiment tracking, model serving, monitoring, and CI/CD for ML systems.

The distinctive approach: rather than teaching ML theory in isolation, the course builds a complete end-to-end system — a production-grade NLP tagging service — and teaches concepts as they arise in building it. This project-first structure means students encounter MLOps problems in context rather than as abstract topics. The stack covered includes PyTorch, MLflow, DVC, Airflow, FastAPI, Docker, and Kubernetes — a reasonable approximation of what a real ML engineering job requires.

[Made With ML](/notes/made-with-ml/) also runs a newsletter that curates recent ML papers and tools, which in 2022 was one of the better ways to track the field outside of reading arXiv directly. The combination of structured curriculum and ongoing curation made it unusual among ML learning resources. It's particularly targeted at software engineers transitioning into ML roles or ML researchers who want to understand the engineering side of putting models into production.

## Key points

- Free, project-based MLOps curriculum — builds a production NLP system as the teaching vehicle.
- Covers the full pipeline: data versioning (DVC), experiment tracking (MLflow), serving (FastAPI), and deployment (Docker, Kubernetes).
- Made by Goku Mohandas, previously at Google Brain and Apple; credible production ML background.
- Structured for software engineers learning ML or researchers learning engineering — bridges both directions.
- One of the better self-study resources for moving from trains models to ships production ML systems.
- Companion newsletter curated recent papers and tools — useful signal in a noisy research landscape.
