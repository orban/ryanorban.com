## Summary

1-toolbox.com is a curated collection of web tools, utilities, and resources hosted as a public Notion page. It's an artifact of the 2020-2022 era when public Notion pages became a popular format for sharing personal knowledge, tool stacks, and curated lists — a lightweight alternative to maintaining a personal website. The format (Notion database with categories, descriptions, and links) became a genre: tools I use, resources I love, productivity stacks publicly shared.

The value of such toolboxes is primarily as inspiration and discovery — a snapshot of what tools practitioners in a particular community found useful at a specific time. The No-Code and indie maker communities were particularly active in this format, sharing tool stacks around Notion, Airtable, Zapier, Webflow, and Bubble for building products without traditional engineering. These curated lists propagated through Product Hunt, Twitter/X, and IndieHackers communities.

The limitation of Notion-hosted public pages as a reference format: they age quickly (tools shut down, better alternatives emerge), don't show modification dates clearly, and depend on the curator continuing to maintain them. This one from nami-w dates to early 2022, and the tool landscape has shifted significantly since. Still useful as a historical snapshot of what was valued in the No-Code/productivity tooling space.

## Key points

- Curated web tool collection on a public Notion page — snapshot of the 2021-2022 No-Code/productivity tool ecosystem.
- Format popularized in the Notion public pages era: personal knowledge and tool stacks shared as Notion databases.
- Primary value: discovery of tools the curator found useful; historical snapshot of what was popular.
- Notion public pages as a format: low friction to publish, but ages quickly and lacks version history signals.
- The No-Code tool landscape it reflects: Airtable, Zapier, Webflow, Bubble, Notion itself as building blocks.
- Compare to more durable formats: GitHub repos (version controlled), dedicated sites (more discoverable).
