## Summary

A Zipfian Academy email newsletter from March 2014 promoting the program to potential referrals — targeting software engineers, business analysts, and scientists. The referral framing ("Do you have a friend who's a software engineer?") reflects the reality that data science bootcamps in 2014 had specific ideal student profiles: people with existing quantitative or programming skills who needed to cross-train rather than start from scratch.

12 weeks of intensive immersive learning was the format Zipfian Academy, Galvanize, and similar programs used. The intensity was a feature: candidates who could commit fully to a 12-week program often had the strongest post-program outcomes. Zipfian Academy specifically selected for people who already had a technical foundation — the curriculum assumed programming and statistical literacy as a baseline.

## Key points

- Zipfian Academy referral campaign targeting software engineers, scientists, and business analysts.
- Ideal student profile: existing technical skills (programming, statistics) + desire to move into data science.
- 12-week immersive format common across 2014 data science bootcamps.
- Referral-driven growth was a key student acquisition channel for bootcamps in this era.
