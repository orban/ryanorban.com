## Summary

[hirethePIVOT](/notes/hirethepivot/) is a job board specifically for developers who are on their second career — people who came to software engineering after working in finance, medicine, law, teaching, or any other field. The platform's tagline is "Find second career developers looking for freelance, contract, and full-time work."

The underlying argument is that career-changers bring something traditional computer science graduates often don't: deep domain expertise from their first career. A developer who spent 10 years as a nurse brings healthcare domain knowledge to health tech companies that is genuinely hard to hire for separately. A former accountant building fintech tools understands the actual problem space, not just the code. Their motivation to succeed (having made a deliberate, sometimes costly career switch) also tends to be higher.

This connects to broader thinking in technical hiring about signal quality — bootcamp graduates and self-taught developers are often evaluated unfairly compared to CS graduates, despite evidence that domain knowledge and work ethic predict job performance better than academic pedigree. [hirethePIVOT](/notes/hirethepivot/) is an attempt to create a market for this specific talent pool rather than making career-changers compete on the same playing field as traditional CS hires.

## Key points

- Job board for developers who pivoted from other careers — not bootcamp-specific, but career-change focused.
- Domain expertise argument: a developer with 10 years in their first field often knows the problem space better than a new CS grad.
- Career change into tech is a real signal — requires conviction and usually some sacrifice.
- Relevant to companies with strong domain requirements: healthtech, fintech, legaltech, edtech.
- Addresses a gap in technical hiring where non-traditional backgrounds are systematically undervalued.
