## Summary

Zipfian Academy's Machine Learning for Growth workshop was a short-format course targeting product managers and growth engineers who wanted to apply machine learning to product decisions. The curriculum covered churn prediction, A/B testing, cohort analysis, and related techniques — the toolkit for data-informed product optimization.

This workshop reflects the 2014 convergence of two trends: the growth hacking movement (popularized by Sean Ellis and Andrew Chen) and the mainstreaming of machine learning. Growth hacking was fundamentally about applying quantitative methods to user acquisition and retention; ML made those methods more powerful. Zipfian Academy was packaging this intersection for non-data-scientists who needed ML literacy without the full 12-week immersive program.

A/B testing and cohort analysis are the foundational techniques: controlled experiments to measure treatment effects, and time-bucketed user analysis to understand retention. Churn prediction is the classic supervised learning application in this space — train a classifier on early behavioral signals to identify users likely to leave, then intervene.

## Key points

- Applied machine learning workshop for product and growth roles: churn, A/B testing, cohort analysis.
- Reflects 2014 convergence of growth hacking culture with data science skills.
- Churn prediction: classic supervised learning problem — early behavior signals future retention.
- A/B testing: controlled experiments with statistical significance — the foundation of growth analytics.
- Zipfian Academy offered both 12-week immersive and shorter workshops to reach different audiences.
