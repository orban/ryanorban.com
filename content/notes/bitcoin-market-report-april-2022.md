## Summary

Bitcoin Magazine Pro's April 2022 monthly research report surveys on-chain analysis metrics, bitcoin derivatives markets, bitcoin mining economics, and the macro backdrop during a significant drawdown period. At the time, bitcoin was 43% below its all-time high while equity markets were also selling off amid a major credit deleveraging cycle. The report frames bitcoin's behavior relative to both its internal market cycle indicators and its correlation with risk assets.

The on-chain section covers core on-chain analysis tools: the MVRV ratio (Market Value to Realized Value), Realized Price to Liveliness Ratio, coin days destroyed, and exchange flow data. Key observation: while price had fallen sharply, long-term holders continued accumulating and exchange outflows set records — the second and third-largest outflow months in history. Hash rate hit new all-time highs during this period, and the Central African Republic adopted bitcoin as legal tender.

The macro section tracks US Treasury yields, Fed Funds Rate projections, BTC-VIX correlation, high yield credit spreads, and the BTC-DXY correlation. The core thesis is that bitcoin's monetization process continues independent of short-term price action, but in early adoption it trades as a high-beta risk asset tightly correlated with global liquidity.

## Key points

- MVRV ratio had not yet reached capitulation levels seen in prior cycles — suggested further downside risk but not yet a confirmed bottom
- Exchange bitcoin outflows set records in March-April 2022, a historically bullish on-chain analysis signal despite price weakness
- Bitcoin mining hash rate at all-time highs even as prices fell — miners were not capitulating
- Central African Republic adopted bitcoin as legal tender, a second sovereign adoption after El Salvador
- BTC traded as high-beta risk asset correlated with macro liquidity, making Fed policy a dominant near-term driver
