## Summary

Zipfian Academy was one of the first data science bootcamps, founded in San Francisco in 2012 by Jonathan Dinu and Katie Kent. This SF Data Science meetup celebrated a graduation cohort and featured Tim Eller and Nick Elprin speaking on startup data science.

Tim Eller (co-founder of Kite) was known for emphasizing the importance of "getting your hands dirty with data" — that no amount of algorithm knowledge substitutes for deep familiarity with a specific dataset. Nick Elprin co-founded Domino Data Lab, which would later become a platform for deploying and managing data science workflows.

The SF Data Science meetup was a key community institution in the early SF data science scene — before the current proliferation of events, these meetups were where practitioners met, shared techniques, and heard from people building data products at scale. The Zipfian graduation events served double duty: alumni networking and de facto recruiting events for SF tech companies.

## Key points

- Zipfian Academy: one of the first data science bootcamps, SF 2012. Competed with later programs like Galvanize, General Assembly data tracks.
- Speakers: Tim Eller on hands-on data practice; Nick Elprin who later founded Domino Data Lab.
- SF Data Science meetup: the community anchor for practitioners in the SF data science scene, 2012-2015.
- Graduation showcases served as recruiting pipelines — companies scouted Zipfian graduates directly.
- This was the moment data science bootcamp was proving itself as a career path, not just a marketing label.
