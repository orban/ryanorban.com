## Summary

Forbes' annual Next Billion-Dollar Startups list identifies 25 companies deemed likely to reach unicorn status ($1B+ valuation). The 2022 edition was published in August 2022 — at a specific inflection point: the 2021 venture capital bubble had peaked, interest rates were rising, and startup valuations were beginning to compress. Companies selected here were being evaluated at the tail end of peak ZIRP-era optimism.

The list is a joint project between Forbes and TCV (Technology Crossover Ventures), relying on VC nominations and editorial judgment. The selection criteria emphasize companies with strong growth metrics but not yet public or privately valued at $1B+. Historically, the list has a mixed track record — some companies do reach unicorn status, others struggle or pivot, and a few become cautionary tales.

As a snapshot of 2022 venture thesis priorities, the list captures what themes were attracting capital at the time: climate tech, fintech infrastructure, B2B SaaS, and early AI-adjacent tools. Reading it from 2024-2026 provides a useful lens on which 2022 bets aged well vs. which reflected bubble-era enthusiasm.

## Key points

- Annual Forbes list identifying 25 likely future unicorns — co-produced with TCV (Technology Crossover Ventures).
- 2022 edition: published at peak-to-turning-point of the venture capital cycle.
- Selection criteria: strong growth, not yet public, not yet valued at $1B+.
- Mixed historical track record — useful as a snapshot of investor thesis priorities, not a reliable prediction tool.
- 2022 themes: climate tech, fintech infrastructure, B2B SaaS, early AI-adjacent tools.
- Read retrospectively as a window into what capital was prioritizing before the 2022-2023 correction.
