## Summary

This design exploration by Prabros presents 10 interaction ideas for chat UIs specifically on mobile, pushing beyond the standard scrollable-bubble-list convention that dominates messaging apps. The piece was published just before ChatGPT's launch — making it accidentally relevant: within days, chat became the dominant interface pattern for AI interaction, and its conventional design was the thing everyone was building on.

The ideas in the piece include concepts like spatial chat layouts (messages positioned in 2D space rather than linear scroll), time-based organization, contextual message threading without the full Discord-style thread complexity, and more expressive input methods. The core challenge being addressed: the linear message bubble UI works for short conversational exchanges but becomes awkward for long AI responses, code blocks, mixed media, and branched conversations.

This connects to the broader [AI UX](/notes/ai-ux/) problem identified around the same time: the chat metaphor that ChatGPT popularized was intuitive for getting started but had clear limitations as the complexity of interactions grew. Claude Artifacts, ChatGPT Canvas, and custom UIs built on top of LLM APIs all represent attempts to move beyond pure chat for tasks that require structured output, editing, or multi-step workflows. The design constraints explored in this piece — how do you handle long responses, how do you navigate history, how do you show context — are the same ones AI product teams grappled with through 2023-2024.
