## Summary

A personal photo from Maker Faire 2012 at the San Mateo County Event Center, shared via Path (the private social network that capped connections at 150). Maker Faire 2012 coincided with the annular solar eclipse on May 20 — the weekend had an unusual energy with both the faire and the eclipse drawing people to San Mateo.

Maker Faire Bay Area 2012 was a high point: MakerBot had just launched the Replicator, Arduino was in its prime as the go-to prototyping platform, and the faire was large enough to feel significant but not yet corporate. Path was the social network for this milieu — it had a 150-person cap, which kept it intimate, and was popular among tech people in the Bay Area during 2011-2013 before declining when Facebook and Instagram consolidated.

## Key points

- Maker Faire Bay Area 2012 coincided with the annular solar eclipse on the same weekend.
- Path (social network, 150-connection cap) was the primary personal photo-sharing platform in this Bay Area tech circle.
- 2012 faire highlights: early MakerBot Replicator, Arduino projects everywhere, large robotic installations.
