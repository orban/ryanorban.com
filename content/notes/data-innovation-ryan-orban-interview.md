## Summary

The Center for Data Innovation runs a 5 Questions interview series with data science practitioners and educators. This July 2014 entry features Ryan Orban, co-founder of Zipfian Academy, discussing the data science education landscape, what the bootcamp model offers that traditional education doesn't, and what skills employers are actually looking for.

Zipfian Academy had been running for about two years at this point and had placed dozens of graduates at tech companies and startups. Ryan Orban's perspective would have been shaped by direct feedback from hiring managers about what skills bootcamp graduates lacked versus what university programs produced, and by the curriculum decisions Zipfian had made in iterating on its program.

The Center for Data Innovation is a think tank focused on data policy and how data is reshaping industries — their audience is policy makers and industry professionals, so this interview serves as a public articulation of the bootcamp model's value proposition at a time when immersive data science programs were new enough to require explanation.

## Key points

- Ryan Orban as public voice for Zipfian Academy — articulating the bootcamp model to a policy/industry audience.
- Interview covers: why bootcamps work, what skills data science roles actually require, hiring trends.
- July 2014: Zipfian Academy was ~2 years old, pre-acquisition by Galvanize (November 2014).
- Center for Data Innovation: DC-based think tank focused on data policy and industry impact.
- Primary source for Ryan Orban's public thinking about data science education.
- Historical document: captures the state of data science hiring before the field standardized on specific tools and credentials.
