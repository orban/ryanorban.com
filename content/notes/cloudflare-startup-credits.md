## Summary

Cloudflare revamped its startup program to offer up to $250,000 in credits for eligible startups, valid for one year. The credits cover the full Cloudflare Developer Platform stack: Cloudflare Workers, Durable Objects, KV storage, speed tools (Images, Argo Smart Routing), security (WAF, DDoS protection, Zero Trust), and up to three Enterprise-level domains with bundled security features.

Eligibility requires a software-based product founded between 2019-2024 with $50,000-$5,000,000 in funding. Bootstrapped startups under $50k can apply with promo code BOOTSTRAPPED. The predictable credit structure — clearly documented so founders can forecast costs after the promotional period — is the stated improvement over the prior program.

Beyond credits, the Workers Launchpad Program offers additional support: bootcamp sessions, architect office hours, VC introductions, and Demo Day opportunities. For startups already building on Cloudflare's edge platform (or evaluating it), this meaningfully reduces infrastructure cost during early growth. The $250k figure is enough to cover substantial edge computing workloads for a year without burning runway.

## Key points

- Up to $250k in credits, 1 year, for eligible startups.
- Covers: Cloudflare Workers, KV, Durable Objects, WAF, DDoS, Zero Trust, Enterprise domains.
- Eligibility: software product, founded 2019-2024, $50k-$5M funding.
- Bootstrapped path available with promo code.
- Workers Launchpad: bootcamp, architect office hours, VC intros, Demo Day.
- Predictable credit accounting — clear forecast of post-program costs.
