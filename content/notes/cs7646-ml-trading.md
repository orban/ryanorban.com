## Summary

CS 7646 is a graduate course from Georgia Tech's OMSCS (Online Master of Science in Computer Science) program that applies machine learning techniques to financial trading and portfolio management. The course videos are freely available, making it one of the most accessible formal treatments of quantitative trading from an ML perspective.

The curriculum covers technical indicators (moving averages, Bollinger Bands, momentum), time series analysis, supervised learning applied to stock price prediction, and notably reinforcement learning for portfolio optimization — Q-learning agents that learn to trade by maximizing cumulative return. The course also covers backtesting methodology and the practical pitfalls of overfitting to historical data.

The intersection of machine learning and finance is a crowded space, but this course takes a principled academic approach rather than the vague AI trading pitch common in retail finance. It grounds everything in the efficient market hypothesis — acknowledging what ML can and can't do — and emphasizes rigorous evaluation over cherry-picked backtests. The OMSCS context means it's part of an accredited degree program with proper exams and projects.

## Key points

- Georgia Tech OMSCS graduate course — free video access, structured curriculum.
- Covers technical indicators, supervised learning for price prediction, and reinforcement learning for portfolio management.
- Uses Q-learning as the RL approach for trading agents.
- Strong emphasis on backtesting methodology and overfitting risks.
- Grounded in efficient market hypothesis — honest about what ML can realistically do in finance.
