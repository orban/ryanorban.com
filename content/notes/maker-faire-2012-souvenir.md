## Summary

A personal photo shared on Twitter from Maker Faire 2012 — a souvenir or purchase from the faire. Maker Faire vendors ranged from commercial electronics suppliers to independent craftspeople selling one-off projects, kits, and maker-themed merchandise.

The faire's commercial side included Adafruit, SparkFun, and similar electronics vendors selling components and kits alongside the project showcases. This was part of the broader maker culture ecosystem where attending an event like Maker Faire often meant purchasing components or kits to fuel future projects.
