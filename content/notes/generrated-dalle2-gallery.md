## Summary

Generrated is a curated gallery of 7,040 images generated with DALL-E 2, each paired with the prompt that produced it. The site serves as both an aesthetic showcase and a practical prompt engineering reference — browsing the gallery reveals patterns in what prompt constructions reliably produce interesting results.

In late 2022, DALL-E 2 access was still gated behind a waitlist and credits system, making galleries like Generrated valuable as proxies for understanding the model's capabilities. People who didn't have access could browse examples to understand what the model could and couldn't do. People who did have access could study prompt structures.

The gallery sits alongside sites like Lexica (which became a larger Stable Diffusion prompt database) and PromptHero in the ecosystem of prompt discovery resources. Unlike those sites which aggregated community submissions, Generrated appears to be a single-author curation with consistent aesthetic sensibility.

## Key points

- 7,040 DALL-E 2 images with full prompts — browsable prompt engineering reference.
- Useful during DALL-E 2's limited-access period as a proxy for understanding capabilities.
- Each image paired with the prompt that generated it.
- Sits alongside Lexica and PromptHero in the prompt discovery ecosystem.
- Single-author curation vs. community aggregation — consistent aesthetic sensibility.
- Historical artifact of the 2022 text-to-image moment.
