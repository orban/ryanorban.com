## Summary

Red Blossom Tea Company is a San Francisco tea shop specializing in high-quality Taiwanese and Chinese teas. Their Formosa oolong collection focuses on high-mountain teas from Taiwan, where elevation (1000–2600m+) combined with cool temperatures and misty conditions produces leaves with concentrated flavor and distinctive character.

Formosa oolong refers to lightly oxidized oolong from Taiwan — the term "Formosa" is the old Portuguese name for Taiwan. These teas sit between green tea and fully oxidized black tea in processing, but the specific character comes from the high-altitude growing conditions: floral aromatics, creamy texture, vegetal sweetness, and a long finish.

Varieties in the collection include: **Da Yu Lin** — Taiwan's highest elevation tea (~2600m), complex and aromatic; **Lishan** — ~2000m, floral and sugarcane notes; **Alishan** — mid-elevation, smooth and vegetal; **Fu Shou Shan** — cream and mango notes; **Jin Xuan** — known for buttery sugarcane flavor (sometimes called milk oolong for its natural creaminess without any flavoring).

## Key points

- High-altitude growing conditions are the key quality driver for Taiwanese oolong — longer maturation at cooler temperatures concentrates flavor compounds.
- Jin Xuan (#17 cultivar) is the most widely available Taiwanese oolong variety — its natural creamy notes come from the cultivar's genetics, not flavoring.
- Da Yu Lin is one of the world's most highly regarded teas — production is extremely limited and prices reflect that.
- Light oxidation (10-30%) preserves floral and green character; heavier oxidation (30-70%) produces Dong Ding and similar styles with more roasted complexity.
- Red Blossom's sourcing model emphasizes direct relationships with specific farms — the specific garden of origin matters more than the regional designation.
