## Summary

Figment is one of the largest proof-of-stake validator and staking infrastructure companies, operating validators on 40+ blockchain networks including Ethereum (post-Merge), Solana, Cosmos, Polkadot, Near, Avalanche, and many others. Their $110M Series C in December 2021, branded as the WAGMI Round (We're All Gonna Make It — crypto slang), was a significant institutional bet on the staking infrastructure market.

The business model: institutions (hedge funds, exchanges, custodians, large token holders) want to earn staking rewards on their proof-of-stake assets without running their own validator infrastructure. Figment operates the infrastructure, takes a commission on staking rewards. As more blockchains moved to proof of stake (the Ethereum Merge was approaching, Solana and Cosmos were already PoS), the market for professional validator services was growing rapidly.

The "further down the Web 3 rabbit hole" framing in the URL signals the cultural positioning — this is institutional crypto capital using the community's own language. Figment sits in the infrastructure layer between individual stakers/validators and the protocol level. Their DataHub developer API product extended beyond validator services into developer access to node infrastructure.

The timing was peak bull market (December 2021); the subsequent year saw Terra/Luna collapse and FTX collapse, which rattled institutional confidence but didn't fundamentally change the proof-of-stake infrastructure thesis.

## Key points

- Figment: institutional proof-of-stake validator infrastructure across 40+ chains; commission on staking rewards
- $110M Series C December 2021 — institutional capital validating PoS infrastructure as a business
- WAGMI branding: institutional crypto firm using community slang — cultural signal of how integrated crypto native culture had become
- DataHub: Figment's developer API product for node access, separate from validator services
- Ethereum Merge was then upcoming; anticipation of ETH staking demand drove validator infrastructure investment
