## Summary

A personal photo bookmark from a typography and design evening in San Francisco, July 2013 — attended with a group of designers including Stephen Coles ([@stewf](https://twitter.com/stewf)), a typographer and editor who worked on fonts at Fonts In Use. The evening centered on type design, visual design, and the intersection with technology — a common SF gathering topic in this era when designers and technologists occupied overlapping social spaces.
