## Summary

Surf Office is a retreat-as-a-service company that handles the logistics of corporate off-sites — venue selection, accommodation, catering, activities, and transport — at destinations in Europe, the US, and beyond. The product targets remote and distributed teams for whom an annual or quarterly in-person gathering is a significant investment of planning time and organizational energy. By packaging the logistics, Surf Office lets the organizing company focus on the meeting agenda rather than sourcing venues and negotiating contracts.

The value proposition is most clear for remote-first companies where the team retreat is the primary mechanism for team cohesion, culture building, and strategic alignment. For these companies, a poorly organized retreat wastes both the travel investment and the team's limited in-person time. The destinations (Lisbon, Barcelona, Las Palmas, etc.) are chosen to be desirable enough to motivate attendance and enable genuine decompression from daily work, while remaining accessible from multiple international hubs.

The category has grown significantly with the rise of distributed teams post-2020. Competitors include Huckletree, The Swarm (workspace-focused), Wanderer Retreats, and a growing number of specialized retreat operators. The DIY alternative (using corporate travel tools to book venues directly) requires significant coordinator time. Surf Office's margin comes from volume relationships with venues and the operational infrastructure to run retreats repeatedly — similar to how conference venues extract margin from corporate event buyers.

## Key points

- Retreat logistics-as-a-service: venue + accommodation + activities + coordination packaged for corporate buyers.
- Core market: remote-first and distributed teams for whom in-person gatherings are high-value but logistically complex.
- Destinations designed for decompression and team bonding — not just convenient, but genuinely attractive.
- The product justification: planning a retreat is a significant time investment; outsourcing logistics focuses the team on actual objectives.
- Growing category post-2020 remote work shift; competes with DIY booking, other retreat operators, and coworking-adjacent products.
- Pricing model: per-person/per-day packages covering all logistics vs. DIY venue-plus-hotel combinations.
