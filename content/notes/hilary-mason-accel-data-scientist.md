## Summary

Hilary Mason — Chief Scientist at Bitly and one of the most visible data science practitioners in the 2013 community — left Bitly to become the first "Data Scientist in Residence" at Accel Partners, the prominent venture capital firm. TechCrunch covered this as a significant career move that signaled data science was becoming important enough for VCs to hire dedicated technical data talent.

Hilary Mason had built her profile at Bitly through her work on URL shortening data at scale — Bitly processed billions of link clicks and had unusual visibility into what content spread virally on the internet. She was known for her public speaking, her Python-focused approach to data analysis, and her work on machine learning for link classification and recommendation. She co-founded HackNY, a fellowship program connecting students to NYC tech startups.

The Data Scientist in Residence role at Accel was novel: the idea was that a senior data scientist could help portfolio companies with their analytics infrastructure and strategy, and help Accel evaluate potential investments that had data-intensive components. The role reflected the VC community's recognition that data science was becoming a core competency, not just a feature — and that they needed people who could assess it in founders, not just fund it.

## Key points

- Hilary Mason: one of the most prominent data science practitioners of the 2013 era — her moves were industry signals.
- Data Scientist in Residence: a new role category in 2013 — VC firms adding technical analytics talent to their partner tracks alongside the traditional finance/operator backgrounds.
- Bitly data science: working on URL shortening at scale gave unusual insight into viral content propagation — a research platform, not just a utility.
- Accel Partners strategic bet: treating data capability assessment as important enough to warrant a dedicated hire at the partner level.
- Career trajectory: Mason later founded Fast Forward Labs (a machine learning research consultancy), then joined Cloudera as Chief Data Scientist.
- Historical context: "data scientist in residence" roles at VCs proliferated after this — by 2016, most major VC firms had some equivalent.
