## Summary

A personal Path check-in at San Francisco International Airport (SFO) marking the end of a May 2012 San Francisco trip. The trip appears to have included TacoConf (May 12) and later Maker Faire 2012 (May 19-20) — a characteristic Bay Area tech weekend combining informal conferences and maker culture events.

SFO in 2012 was one of the main gateways for tech community travel to and from San Francisco, before BART to SFO became the standard option for anyone traveling light. The Path check-in format — location + brief note + private sharing to 150 connections — was the social layer for these personal moments.
