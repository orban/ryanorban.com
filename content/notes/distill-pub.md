## Summary

Distill (distill.pub) was a peer-reviewed online journal dedicated to the proposition that ML research could be explained with far more clarity than standard academic papers achieve. Founded in 2016, it published interactive articles that combined rigorous technical content with visualizations that actually moved — you could adjust parameters and watch the model respond, navigate embeddings, and see attention patterns animate. The bar for clarity was genuinely high: articles went through peer review specifically for explanation quality, not just technical correctness.

The most influential pieces include Feature Visualization (how to understand what neural networks look for), The Building Blocks of Interpretability (composing interpretability techniques), articles on attention and transformers, curve detectors, and circuits-style mechanistic interpretability. These became canonical references — the Feature Visualization work is cited in virtually every paper on [mechanistic interpretability](/notes/mechanistic-interpretability/), and the circuits work directly influenced the research program at Anthropic.

Distill went on hiatus in July 2021 after five years, citing the difficulty of sustaining the editorial workload. The archive is still online and still actively read. The style it established — interactive, exploratory, prioritizing intuition over formalism — influenced how ML practitioners think about communicating research. Tools like Observable and the later explosion of interactive ML notebooks owe something to the existence proof Distill provided.

## Key points

- Peer-reviewed ML journal with explicit focus on explanation quality and interactive visualization
- Key works: Feature Visualization, The Building Blocks of Interpretability, circuits mechanistic interpretability
- GitHub-based transparent peer review — review threads are public
- On hiatus since July 2021 — 5 years of active publication
- Directly influenced [mechanistic interpretability](/notes/mechanistic-interpretability/) research at Anthropic and elsewhere
- The gold standard for interactive ML pedagogy; archive at distill.pub remains essential reading
