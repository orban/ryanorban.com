## Summary

[OpenBB Terminal](/notes/openbb-terminal/) is an open-source, Python-based investment research platform positioning itself as an accessible alternative to expensive institutional tools like the Bloomberg Terminal. The project started as Gamestonk Terminal in 2021 (inspired by the GameStop short squeeze) and rebranded to OpenBB as it matured.

The platform aggregates financial data from free and paid sources: stock prices and fundamentals from Yahoo Finance and Alpha Vantage, options data, sentiment analysis from news and social media, cryptocurrency data, ETF analysis, macroeconomic indicators. All accessed through a command-line interface or optional web UI. The CLI is intentionally Bloomberg-like — muscle memory from expensive terminals transfers.

The open-source model lets developers extend it: custom data sources, new analysis modules, integrations with local and cloud LLMs for natural language querying. OpenBB subsequently launched a paid Hub layer for teams and enterprises, following the open-core business model.

For individual investors and researchers who can't justify a Bloomberg subscription, OpenBB fills a real gap — the Bloomberg Terminal costs ~$25,000/year, and most of what retail investors need is available from free data sources if you have something to aggregate them.

## Key points

- Open-source investment research terminal with Bloomberg-style CLI.
- Aggregates data from Yahoo Finance, Alpha Vantage, and other free/paid sources.
- Covers equities, options, crypto, ETFs, macro, and sentiment analysis.
- Python-based — extensible with custom data sources and analysis modules.
- LLM integration for natural language queries of financial data.
- Started as Gamestonk Terminal (2021) during the GameStop short squeeze.
