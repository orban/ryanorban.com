ThingConf was a conference organized by Rachel Binx for people who make and sell physical products online — Etsy sellers, independent hardware makers, and direct-to-consumer brands. The Kickstarter campaign was the launch announcement.

Sits at the intersection of the maker movement and e-commerce infrastructure — a niche that anticipated the broader creator economy conversation.
