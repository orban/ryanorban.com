## Summary

Pitch Interactive is a data visualization studio based in Oakland, California, founded by Wesley Grubbs. They became well-known in data visualization circles for projects that combined rigorous data journalism with high-quality interactive graphics — particularly their "Out of Sight, Out of Mind" drone strike visualization, which tracked covert US drone strikes in Pakistan and presented the data in a way that made the scale of civilian casualties legible.

The studio worked at the intersection of data journalism, information design, and interactive media — a space that was growing rapidly in 2012-2013 as news organizations were building out data teams and the web had become capable enough to deliver sophisticated interactive graphics. Their work was notable for taking difficult, politically charged subjects and letting the data make the argument through visual structure rather than editorial assertion.

In the 2013 data visualization landscape, a handful of studios defined the aesthetic and methodological direction: Stamen Design (civic data, mapping), Periscopic (data humanism), Moritz Stefaner (information design), and Pitch Interactive (data journalism). These studios influenced a generation of practitioners who went on to build the data teams at major media organizations.

## Key points

- Pitch Interactive / Wesley Grubbs: Oakland-based studio; one of the leading practices in data journalism visualization in the early 2010s
- "Out of Sight, Out of Mind": their most-shared work — a visualization of covert US drone strikes in Pakistan with civilian casualty data; widely circulated in 2013
- Their approach: data integrity first, then design — not decorative charts but visual encodings that reveal structure
- Context: this era of data visualization was pre-Tableau, pre-Power BI mainstream; studios like Pitch were coding custom D3/canvas work for each project
- D3.js ecosystem: Pitch used and contributed to the emerging JS visualization ecosystem
