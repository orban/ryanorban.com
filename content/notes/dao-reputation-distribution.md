## Summary

DAOtalk is the community forum for DAO design discussions, and this thread tackles one of the hardest bootstrapping problems in DAO design: how do you distribute initial governance rights fairly? Whatever mechanism you choose encodes value judgments about who deserves governance power at the start — and those choices have lasting effects on the organization's power structure.

The methods discussed typically include: retroactive distribution based on past contributions (reward people who built the thing before formal governance existed), equal distribution to early members (one person, one vote), capital-weighted distribution (buy in), work-based allocation (ongoing tasks earn reputation), and combinations of these. Each has problems. Retroactive allocation rewards founders and insiders. Equal distribution ignores contribution quality. Capital-weighted creates plutocracy immediately. Work-based requires a working organization to already exist.

The thread likely documents community thinking from DAOstack's ecosystem (DAOtalk is associated with the broader DAOstack/Genesis DAO community) circa 2020-2021. The problem is also explored in the broader literature on cooperative governance, sortition, and Ostrom's common pool resource management principles.

The practical reality: most successful DAOs used retroactive founder/contributor allocation, then layered in participation-based reputation accrual. The bootstrapping problem is usually solved by founder fiat, which then legitimizes itself through subsequent fair governance.

## Key points

- Bootstrapping problem: initial governance distribution encodes who has power, and no method is neutral
- Retroactive allocation: rewards past contributions but favors founders/insiders by definition
- Capital-weighted (token purchase) creates immediate plutocracy but is easy to implement
- Work-based reputation accrual (like Colony) requires existing organization to bootstrap it
- Ostrom's common pool resource principles suggest legitimate rules need community consent from the start
