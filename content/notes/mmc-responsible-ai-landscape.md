## Summary

MMC Ventures published a 2024 market map of approximately 125 companies in the Responsible AI space, organized into three categories: AI Governance, Risk & Compliance (GRC), AI Quality, and AI Security & Privacy. The report by Advika Jalan and Mina Samaan covers the commercial landscape that emerged around AI safety and AI governance concerns — the businesses building tools for enterprises to manage AI risk.

The three categories carve up the problem differently. AI GRC focuses on compliance and regulation: bias auditing, explainability reports, model registries, policy enforcement. AI Quality covers what happens in production: monitoring for data drift, accuracy degradation, and behavioral changes in deployed models. AI Security & Privacy covers adversarial attacks, data leakage prevention, input/output filtering, and prompt injection defenses.

The funding asymmetry is striking: AI Security & Privacy attracted roughly half of the sector's $1.3 billion total investment, with the UK leading European AI Quality and GRC activity, and Israel dominating European AI Security. This reflects where enterprise risk perception concentrates: security threats feel more urgent and concrete than governance or quality issues, even if quality failures cause more cumulative harm. The market itself was ~5 years old in 2024 — most companies were still at bootstrapped or early stages.

## Key points

- Three categories: AI GRC (compliance/audit), AI Quality (production monitoring/drift), AI Security & Privacy (adversarial attacks, data leakage).
- ~125 companies mapped; ~75% bootstrapped or early-stage as of April 2024.
- AI Security & Privacy attracted ~50% of $1.3B sector funding — security risk feels most urgent to enterprise buyers.
- UK leads European AI Quality/GRC; Israel leads European AI Security.
- Useful for understanding the commercial AI safety landscape alongside research-focused efforts from Anthropic, METR, and similar.
