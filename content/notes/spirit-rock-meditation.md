## Summary

Spirit Rock is an insight meditation (vipassana) center located on 411 acres of oak woodlands in Woodacre, Marin County, CA. It was founded in 1988 by Jack Kornfield and other teachers in the Theravada Buddhist tradition, and it has become one of the most well-regarded retreat centers in the United States for mindfulness meditation and dharma study.

The center offers a range of programs: day-long events, week-long and month-long residential retreats, teacher training programs, and online courses. The residential retreats are held in silence — participants meditate 8-12 hours per day, with guided instruction and group dharma talks. The longer retreats, particularly the 3-month winter retreat, are considered transformative experiences in the Western Buddhist teacher lineage — many prominent Western dharma teachers did their most intensive practice at Spirit Rock or the related center Insight Meditation Society (IMS) in Barre, Massachusetts.

The curriculum is grounded in Theravada Buddhism but taught in a secular, accessible way suited to Western practitioners. Core practices include mindfulness of breathing, body scan, metta (lovingkindness), and vipassana (insight) practices that examine the three characteristics of experience: impermanence, suffering/unsatisfactoriness, and non-self.

Spirit Rock also hosts the Community Dharma Leaders (CDL) program and has been central to expanding dharma teaching in underrepresented communities through its BIPOC and LGBTQ+ dedicated programming.

## Key points

- 411-acre retreat center in Marin County — one of the premier vipassana retreat centers in the US.
- Founded by Jack Kornfield in the Theravada tradition; sister center to Insight Meditation Society (Barre, MA).
- Offers silent residential retreats from day-long to 3-month duration.
- Western-accessible teaching style — Theravada teachings without requiring Buddhist conversion or belief.
- Community Dharma Leaders program trains teachers, with intentional focus on BIPOC and LGBTQ+ communities.
