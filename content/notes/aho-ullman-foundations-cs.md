## Summary

*Foundations of Computer Science* by Alfred Aho and Jeff Ullman is a classic undergraduate textbook that pairs algorithms and data structures with the mathematical foundations of computing — sets and relations, induction, logic, automata theory, and complexity theory. Ullman made the full text freely available on his Stanford website, which made it a widely used self-study reference.

Aho and Ullman are two of the most influential computer scientists of the 20th century: Ullman co-authored the "Dragon Book" on compilers and the foundational databases textbook; Aho co-authored *The Design and Analysis of Computer Algorithms* (with Hopcroft) and the AWK language is named for Aho-Weinberger-Kernighan. Their collaboration produced a textbook that approaches CS foundations with the rigor of mathematics but the practical orientation of engineering.

The book covers data structures (lists, trees, heaps, graphs), core algorithms (sorting, searching, graph traversal), and theoretical foundations (finite automata, context-free grammars, Turing machines, NP-completeness). What's notable is the integration: most undergraduate curricula separate these into distinct courses, but Aho and Ullman show how the theoretical and practical are connected — regular expressions are finite automata, compiler parsing is context-free grammar recognition.

## Key points

- Alfred Aho and Jeff Ullman: two of the most influential figures in programming languages, compilers, and database theory — deep expertise behind the pedagogy.
- Integrates theory and practice: finite automata, context-free grammars, and Turing machines alongside concrete data structure implementations.
- Freely available from Ullman's Stanford website — a rare case of a classic textbook made fully open access.
- Covers NP-completeness accessibly: the theoretical hardness framework every CS practitioner needs to reason about algorithmic limits.
- Companion to [Mining of Massive Datasets](/notes/mining-of-massive-datasets/): Ullman's other free textbook — together they cover foundations and large-scale applications.
