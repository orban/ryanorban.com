## Summary

pytudes is Peter Norvig's personal collection of Python programs, described as "usually short, of considerable difficulty, to perfect particular skills." Norvig is the co-author of the canonical AI textbook (*Artificial Intelligence: A Modern Approach*), former Director of Research at Google, and one of the most respected programmers of his generation. A collection of programs he wrote to sharpen his skills is worth paying attention to.

The notebooks span a wide range: solving Advent of Code puzzles, implementing classic algorithms from scratch (shortest paths, constraint satisfaction), working through probability and statistics problems, building small interpreters, and exploring mathematical puzzles. The common thread is Norvig's programming style — clear, dense, idiomatic Python that uses the right data structures and expresses solutions at the right level of abstraction.

The value of pytudes isn't just the problems but the solutions. Norvig's solutions are teaching artifacts: he writes code the way an experienced programmer thinks, not the way a beginner writes explanations. Reading how he solves a problem you've already solved yourself is often more instructive than solving a new problem, because you see what you could have done differently. The notebooks are Jupyter Notebooks, making them interactive and readable.

## Key points

- Peter Norvig's personal Python SKILL-building programs — curated, high-quality, instructive solutions.
- Covers algorithms, puzzles, probability, Advent of Code, and mathematical problem-solving.
- The value is Norvig's *solutions* — seeing idiomatic expert-level Python applied to familiar problems.
- Interactive Jupyter Notebook format; runnable and readable.
- By the co-author of *AI: A Modern Approach* and former Google Research Director — pedigree matters here.
