## Summary

[Invisible College](/notes/invisible-college/) is a DAO that uses an NFT membership model to fund and deliver Web3 education. The "DegenU" concept: buy a Decentralian NFT to join the college, which gives you access to courses, a community of learners, and governance rights over the educational program. NFT sale proceeds fund the DAO treasury, which pays for course development, instructors, and operations.

The model tries to align incentives in a specific way: if the education is genuinely valuable, the NFTs appreciate as the community grows, rewarding early members. Members are also stakeholders who vote on curriculum, partnerships, and treasury allocation — not just students paying tuition to a passive institution. This is the DAO-as-educational-institution thesis applied to the most relevant domain: teaching people how DAOs work.

[Invisible College](/notes/invisible-college/) launched with significant attention in late 2021 as one of several learning DAOs or education DAOs that emerged from the insight that the web3 space had a talent shortage problem — not enough developers, designers, and organizers who understood how these systems worked. Other contemporaries: Buildspace (developer education, less DAO-focused), Odyssey DAO (similar education DAO model), Rabbithole (learn-to-earn model).

The NFT membership as tuition model has the same risks as all token-gated communities: if the token price falls sharply, the DAO treasury loses value and the membership signal weakens.

## Key points

- NFT membership funds DAO treasury → treasury funds education → quality education → NFT appreciation
- Aligned stakeholder model: students are also owners with governance rights over the curriculum
- "DegenU" branding: playful self-awareness about the crypto-native audience
- Part of the 2021 learning DAO wave: Buildspace, Odyssey DAO, Rabbithole — talent shortage driving education DAOs
- Risk: token price crash → treasury loss → reduced educational capacity → further price pressure
