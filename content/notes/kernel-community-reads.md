## Summary

Kernel is a Web3 education community and fellowship program built around cohort-based learning. Founded by Andy Tudhope, it runs cohorts (KB0, KB1, KB2...) that work through a structured curriculum covering the technical, economic, and philosophical foundations of Ethereum and decentralized systems. The community reads list is the reading backbone of that curriculum.

The list reflects Kernel's distinctive approach: it doesn't treat Web3 as purely a technical or financial domain but as part of a longer history of how humans coordinate and create shared meaning. The reading selections span cryptography and security (The Cypherpunk Manifesto, A Cypherpunk's Manifesto), economics and game theory, political philosophy (thinking about governance and sovereignty), and works on the nature of the internet and digital culture (New Media Reader, writings from early internet thinkers).

Kernel's curriculum is notable in the Ethereum ecosystem because it explicitly engages with value questions — what do we want these systems to do in the world? — rather than just technical or investment questions. The fellowship has produced hundreds of graduates who form a notable overlap between thoughtful builders and the broader Ethereum community. Gitcoin (quadratic funding) was incubated with Kernel involvement.

## Key points

- Kernel fellowship: cohort-based Web3 education mixing technical, economic, and philosophical readings
- Reading list spans: Cypherpunk Manifesto, political philosophy, game theory, internet history — unusually broad for crypto education
- Curriculum philosophy: technology choices are value choices — what kind of internet do we want to build?
- Andy Tudhope founded Kernel; Gitcoin connection highlights the public goods focus
- Produces web3 thinkers rather than just developers or traders — a distinct niche in the Ethereum education ecosystem
