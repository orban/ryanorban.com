## Summary

NEW MODELS is a podcast and media project that sits at the intersection of technology, culture, and critical theory — covering AI and digital culture from an aesthetic and intellectual angle rather than a purely technical one. The project was active in 2022 at the height of early generative AI cultural interest.

The content draws on art criticism, accelerationism, post-internet culture, and speculative theory to think through what AI tools mean culturally — not just what they can do technically. This puts it in a different register than most AI coverage: less about product features and benchmarks, more about what AI-generated art means for creativity, authorship, and culture.

NEW MODELS exemplifies a strand of AI discourse that operates outside the typical VC/researcher/engineer pipeline — cultural theorists, artists, and critics processing AI's implications for human creative practice. This perspective was underrepresented in 2022 mainstream AI coverage and remains worth tracking.

## Key points

- Podcast and media project at AI × culture intersection.
- Approaches AI through art criticism, theory, and cultural analysis rather than technical coverage.
- Active during the 2022 generative AI art explosion (Stable Diffusion, DALL-E 2).
- Represents the cultural critics processing AI angle missing from mainstream tech coverage.
- Sits alongside e-flux, Rhizome, and other digitally-oriented cultural publications.
- Different register from AI safety or AI capabilities discourse.
