## Summary

Openby.design is a curated collection of open-source design resources — fonts, icon sets, illustrations, UI components, and design tools — all available for free use in projects. The site aggregates resources from across the open-source ecosystem and presents them in a browsable format oriented toward practitioners who need assets without license friction.

The category of curated open design resources has several players: Figma Community, Google Fonts, unDraw, and various GitHub awesome-lists. Sites like Openby.design provide a single browsing surface rather than requiring separate searches across these sources. For product teams that want professional-quality visual assets without budget or licensing overhead, this kind of aggregator reduces friction.

The resource types typically included: open typefaces (beyond Google Fonts, including lesser-known but high-quality releases), open icon libraries (Feather, Lucide, Heroicons, Phosphor), open illustration packs, color palettes, and occasionally UI kits. The curation quality determines whether the site is useful beyond a bookmark — the best of these resources would surface options most practitioners haven't seen.

## Key points

- Curated open-source design assets: fonts, icons, illustrations, UI components.
- Reduces the search overhead of finding license-free design resources across multiple sources.
- Competes in a crowded category: Figma Community, unDraw, Google Fonts, and various awesome-lists cover similar ground.
- Useful specifically when you need something that's not in the most commonly known libraries.
