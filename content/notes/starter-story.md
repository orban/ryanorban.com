## Summary

[Starter Story](/notes/starter-story/) is a database of entrepreneur case studies created by Pat Walls, where founders describe how they built their businesses — including revenue numbers, traffic, costs, and the specific tactics that worked. The emphasis is on small and mid-size bootstrapped businesses rather than VC-backed startups, covering everything from Shopify stores to SaaS products to newsletters.

The format is interview-based: each case study answers standardized questions — what the business does, how much it makes, how they got their first customers, what marketing channels work, what the hardest part was. The standardized format makes comparison across different businesses genuinely useful, revealing patterns that aren't obvious from individual stories.

The insight density is high because the subjects are people who built businesses with their own money and have concrete numbers to share. Unlike VC startup stories which often obscure unit economics behind growth narratives, bootstrapped businesses need to be profitable, so case studies include specific revenue figures and margins. [Starter Story](/notes/starter-story/) is in the same category as Indie Hackers but with more depth per case study.

## Key points

- Database of bootstrapped business case studies with real revenue numbers.
- Standardized interview format — enables comparison across industries and business models.
- Focus on small/mid bootstrapped businesses, not VC-backed startups.
- Covers e-commerce, SaaS, newsletters, service businesses, and more.
- By Pat Walls; complements Indie Hackers community.
- High signal for solopreneur and indie hacker tactics with proven economics.
