## Summary

This bikely.com route covers cycling from San Francisco to San Jose — roughly 50 miles down the Peninsula via El Camino Real or the Caltrain corridor. Ryan saved this while planning a group ride with colleagues (@gstatton and @fulgenteft). The tweet context: Ryan had done a prior ride successfully and was recruiting others for an SF-to-SJ route.

The route covers the tech corridor — passing through Palo Alto, Mountain View, Sunnyvale — and was a reasonable endurance cycling challenge for recreational riders. The Peninsula has reasonable cycling infrastructure along the Bay Trail and multi-use paths, though El Camino Real itself is high-traffic.

Personal artifact: a logistics bookmark from Ryan's time in the Bay Area, reflecting an active outdoor culture among the SF tech community in 2013.

## Key points

- 50-mile route, one-way: SF to SJ via the Peninsula — manageable in a day for experienced recreational cyclists.
- Bay Area cycling infrastructure: Caltrain allows bike carriage for the return trip, making one-way rides practical.
- Personal context: group ride planning with Nutanix colleagues — Ryan working at Nutanix in San Jose while living in SF.
