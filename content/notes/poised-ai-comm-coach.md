## Summary

Poised is an AI-powered communication coaching tool that runs silently during online meetings, providing real-time and post-meeting feedback on speaking performance. It integrates with Zoom, Slack, Microsoft Teams, Google Meet, and other video platforms, analyzing speech for filler words (um, uh, like), pacing, clarity, and confidence signals.

The product sits at the intersection of speech analysis, natural language processing, and professional development. Real-time feedback — rather than review-after-the-fact — is the differentiating angle: Poised can display a heads-up display visible only to the speaker, showing active indicators during the call. Post-call summaries provide a breakdown of what was said, how it was said, and specific improvement areas.

This was a 2022 Product Hunt launch, placing it in the early wave of AI tools aimed at the remote work communication gap. The use case maps onto a real problem: people often have blind spots about their own verbal habits, and the shift to video calls made communication skills more visible and more scrutinized. Earlier tools in this space (like Orai for public speaking practice) focused on offline rehearsal; Poised moved feedback into live calls.

## Key points

- Real-time speech coaching overlay during live video meetings — filler word detection, pace, confidence analysis
- Integrates with major video platforms: Zoom, Slack, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams
- Post-call summaries with trend tracking over time — progress metrics for communication improvement
- Launched on Product Hunt January 2022 during peak remote-work AI tooling wave
- Competes with Otter.ai (transcription focus), Fireflies, Gong (sales call intelligence) in adjacent meeting-AI space
