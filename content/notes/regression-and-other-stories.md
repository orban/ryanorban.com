## Summary

[Regression and Other Stories](/notes/regression-and-other-stories/) is a statistics textbook by Andrew Gelman, Jennifer Hill, and Aki Vehtari that covers applied regression modeling from the ground up, with a strong emphasis on real data analysis and practical workflow. The companion site hosts the code examples in R using the rstanarm package for Bayesian regression and the Arm package.

The book's distinguishing feature is its integration of Bayesian workflow throughout — not just as a separate chapter but woven into how you think about building, checking, and interpreting models. The examples are drawn from social science, medical research, and public policy domains, and the authors are frank about the messiness of real data. This is a book that teaches you to think about what your model is actually claiming, not just how to fit it.

Andrew Gelman and Jennifer Hill also wrote Data Analysis Using Regression and Multilevel/Hierarchical Models (the ARM book), which was the previous standard reference in this space. This book updates and partially replaces that one, with a more accessible presentation and better coverage of causal inference and Stan/rstanarm for modern Bayesian computation.

## Key points

- Covers regression modeling from basics through multilevel models and causal inference — a complete applied statistics curriculum.
- Bayesian workflow is central throughout: prior choice, model checking, posterior predictive checks, not just point estimates.
- Examples from social science, public health, and policy — real messy data, not textbook-clean examples.
- Code examples in R with rstanarm (Stan-based Bayesian regression); accessible to non-statisticians.
- Successor to the classic ARM book (Gelman & Hill 2006) — updated for modern Bayesian computation workflows.
