## Summary

Trait Sniper is a tool for NFT traders that calculates rarity scores for items in collections and helps identify underpriced assets — the practice known as sniping. Most NFT collections (profile picture collections like BAYC, Azuki, Doodles) generate tokens by combining traits randomly, where some trait combinations are far rarer than others. The floor price of a collection represents the cheapest available NFT, but a rare-trait NFT priced at the floor because the seller didn't realize its rarity can be worth multiples more.

Trait Sniper solved a specific information asymmetry: it would calculate rarity rankings for a collection's metadata (often published before images are revealed) and surface the rarity ranks in real time on OpenSea. When a new collection minted, the tool would show rarity scores within minutes, giving users who had it open an edge over traders who had to wait for informal community rankings. The snipe is buying a rare NFT at floor price before the seller or market understands its rarity.

The existence of tools like Trait Sniper reflects the gamification of NFT trading in 2022: a significant portion of activity was speculative trading around trait rarity, reveal events, and floor price movements rather than genuine collecting. The information advantage these tools provided was real but fleeting — as more participants adopted them, the edge compressed. The category of on-chain analytics tools that emerged around this era (including Nansen, DappRadar, and wallet-tracker tools) represented the professionalization of NFT trading.

## Key points

- Calculates rarity scores for NFT collection traits — identifies rare combinations that the floor price doesn't reflect.
- Pre-reveal sniping: metadata is published before images, so rarity can be calculated before visual reveal.
- OpenSea integration: shows rarity rank in-browser while browsing, reducing information friction.
- Information asymmetry edge: advantage compresses as more traders adopt the same tools.
- 2022 peak NFT market context: trait rarity arbitrage was a significant trading strategy.
- Related tools: Rarity Tools, Rarity Sniper, NFTGo — the broader on-chain NFT analytics space.
