## Summary

A Twitpic photo from March 2009 showing UC Berkeley students camping overnight to secure tickets for an upcoming Dalai Lama campus visit. The image was shared via Twitter and captured the enthusiasm around the event.

This was during the early days of Twitpic — the main way to attach photos to tweets before Twitter had native image support. The Dalai Lama visited UC Berkeley around this period as part of several California university engagements; ticket queues for his appearances regularly drew overnight campers.

The URL now leads to an inaccessible or defunct Twitpic page (Twitpic shut down in 2014).

## Key points

- Students camping overnight at UC Berkeley for Dalai Lama tickets, April 2009.
- Captured via Twitpic, a photo-sharing companion to Twitter that shut down in 2014.
- Reflects the campus culture around high-profile speaker events at UC Berkeley.
