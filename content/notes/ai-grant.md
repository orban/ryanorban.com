## Summary

[AI Grant](/notes/ai-grant/) is a no-strings-attached grant program co-founded by Nat Friedman (former GitHub CEO) and Daniel Gross (former Apple AI director, Pioneer.app founder) to fund people building with AI. The first cohort launched in August 2022 with $10,000 grants aimed at researchers and builders working on ambitious AI projects — no equity taken, no reporting requirements. The stated goal: get money to interesting people before the VC system reaches them.

The program launched at a significant moment: Stable Diffusion had just gone public, GPT-3 was widely available, and a new category of AI-first applications was clearly possible but had no established funding path. Traditional VC was still calibrating on AI — the major AI labs were raising large rounds, but the application layer was underserved. [AI Grant](/notes/ai-grant/) was positioned as a seed-before-the-seed: find people with interesting ideas and give them enough money to quit their day jobs and experiment.

Nat Friedman and Daniel Gross were both credible to the technical AI community — not generalist VCs applying AI themes to existing models, but people who had thought deeply about where AI was going. The grant's positioning as for the builders and its no-strings framing made it attractive to researchers who might be skeptical of VC incentives. The program later evolved and expanded, with both founders going on to make significant AI investments through other vehicles.

## Key points

- $10k no-strings grants to AI researchers and builders — no equity, no reporting requirements.
- Founded by Nat Friedman (GitHub CEO) and Daniel Gross (Pioneer.app) in August 2022.
- Timed to the Stable Diffusion / GPT-3 moment — filling funding gap before VC caught up with AI applications.
- Targets: people building AI-first products, not retrofitting AI onto existing ideas.
- Part of a broader ecosystem-building move — finding and backing the next generation of AI builders early.
- Both founders subsequently became major AI investors (Daniel Gross led Claude early rounds; Nat Friedman invested widely).
