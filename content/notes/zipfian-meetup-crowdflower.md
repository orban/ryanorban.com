Zipfian Academy tweet: standing-room-only at the #SFDataScience Meetup, hosted by CrowdFlower (later renamed Figure Eight, acquired by Appen). Captured the energy of the San Francisco data science community in mid-2014 — these meetups were where practitioners, bootcamp students, and hiring companies all met.

CrowdFlower was a data labeling platform that provided training data for machine learning models — a natural venue for data science events.
