## Summary

FutureStack was New Relic's annual developer conference, held in San Francisco. The 2013 edition (FutureStack13) took place while Ryan was attending Zipfian Academy, reflecting the overlapping worlds of data science and DevOps/monitoring that characterized the 2013 SF tech scene.

New Relic was one of the early SaaS monitoring companies — application performance monitoring (APM) as a product rather than something engineering teams built themselves. FutureStack was their attempt to build community around observability and software operations, themes that would later crystallize into SRE (Site Reliability Engineering) and the DevOps movement.

The conference timing coincides with a period when data science and data engineering practitioners were increasingly thinking about production systems, not just analysis — the pipeline from Hadoop to visualization needed monitoring, reliability, and operational tooling.

## Key points

- FutureStack was New Relic's developer conference, focused on application performance monitoring and observability.
- October 2013 — during Zipfian Academy cohort period; the data science and DevOps worlds were beginning to converge.
- New Relic represented the SaaS observability category before Datadog, Grafana Cloud, and others dominated.
