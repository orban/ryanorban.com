## Summary

A personal photo from TacoConf 2012 in San Francisco — a small, informal tech conference organized around tacos and bicycle culture. TacoConf was part of the wave of playful, self-organized tech gatherings in the early 2010s Bay Area: BarCamp, Ignite, and similar unconferences that positioned themselves against the formality and expense of traditional tech conferences.

The format: speakers, tacos, bikes, a casual venue. The attendees were generally from the SF tech community — the same people who showed up to Maker Faire, read Hacker News, and organized informal tech events. This kind of community-organized conference was a genuine alternative to the TechCrunch Disrupt / enterprise conference circuit — no badges, no suits, no PR-managed panels.

Path in 2012 was the social network for sharing these moments privately with a curated 150-person list — appropriate for a small conference where most attendees knew each other.
