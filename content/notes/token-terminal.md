## Summary

[Token Terminal](/notes/token-terminal/) applies traditional finance metrics to crypto protocols — revenue, price-to-sales ratio (P/S), total value locked (TVL), and active users. The premise is that most crypto analytics tools in 2022 were either price trackers or raw on-chain data dashboards, with little tooling for the kind of fundamental analysis (how much revenue does this protocol actually generate?) that equity investors would apply to a tech company.

The key metric [Token Terminal](/notes/token-terminal/) focuses on is protocol revenue — fees that accrue to the protocol's treasury or token holders, not just fees paid to liquidity providers. This distinction matters: Uniswap generates enormous volume-based fees, but those fees go to liquidity providers, not to the protocol itself. The protocol revenue is much smaller, which changes the valuation calculus significantly. The P/S ratio (market cap divided by annualized revenue) allows rough comparisons across protocols.

The dashboard covers major protocols across DeFi, L1 blockchains, and infrastructure: Ethereum, Uniswap, Aave, Compound, MakerDAO, and others. It was particularly useful during the 2021-2022 period when many protocols were trading at valuations that implied extraordinary growth — the revenue data provided a grounding reality check.

This complements [Web3 Index](/notes/web3-index/) (which tracks similar metrics with slightly different framing) and Dune Analytics (which lets you query raw on-chain data yourself). [Token Terminal](/notes/token-terminal/) is the Bloomberg terminal approach: curated, normalized metrics rather than raw data.

## Key points

- Applies P/S ratio, revenue, and user metrics to crypto protocols — traditional finance analysis for DeFi.
- Distinguishes protocol revenue (fees to treasury/token holders) from supply-side revenue (fees to LPs) — a critical distinction for valuation.
- TVL as a metric can be misleading; revenue is harder to game and better reflects genuine product-market fit.
- Covers Ethereum, Uniswap, Aave, MakerDAO, and most major DeFi protocols.
- Complements [Web3 Index](/notes/web3-index/) and Dune Analytics in the on-chain analytics ecosystem.
