## Summary

Ryan Selkis, founder and CEO of Messari, publishes this ~120-page annual crypto market report as a comprehensive scan of every major sector in the cryptocurrency ecosystem. Written in late 2021 and released January 2022 — right at the peak of the bull market before the 2022 crash — it offers both retrospective analysis of what drove the prior year's moves and forward-looking theses for where each sector is headed. The report covers DeFi, NFTs, DAOs, Layer 1 blockchains, Layer 2 scaling, gaming/metaverse, privacy coins, stablecoins, and regulatory landscape.

The 2022 edition is notable for its optimism tempered by sector-specific skepticism. Selkis identifies sustainable DeFi primitives (AMMs, lending protocols, Aave, Compound, Uniswap) as the durable infrastructure while flagging froth in NFT speculation and DAO governance theater. The Layer 1 analysis covers Solana, Avalanche, Terra, and Cosmos as the primary challengers to Ethereum — with prescient flags about Terra's algorithmic stablecoin model months before the LUNA collapse. Bitcoin gets its own section as a macro asset class rather than a speculative token.

On Ethereum, the report covers the merge to proof of stake (expected sometime 2022), Layer 2 rollup scaling (Arbitrum, Optimism, zkSync), and the EIP-1559 fee burn dynamics. The regulatory section flags the tension between SEC enforcement approaches and the industry's lobbying push for clearer rules — a dynamic that would define 2022-2023 crypto policy. The report serves as a useful primary source for the mental models and narratives dominant at crypto's previous peak.

## Key points

- Comprehensive sector-by-sector analysis: DeFi, NFTs, DAOs, L1s, L2s, gaming, privacy — a scan of the entire crypto landscape as of early 2022
- DeFi infrastructure thesis: AMMs, lending, and composable primitives are the durable layer; speculation on top is noise
- Terra/LUNA flagged as risky algorithmic stablecoin model — prescient given the May 2022 collapse
- Ethereum merge timeline discussed; Layer 2 rollups identified as the scaling path
- Regulatory outlook: SEC enforcement vs industry lobbying — the tension that would dominate 2022
