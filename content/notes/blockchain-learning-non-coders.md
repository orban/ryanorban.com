## Summary

This Medium article by Josh Cottrell-Schloemer addresses the question: how does someone without a technical background break into the crypto / web3 job market? The answer is that the space desperately needed non-engineering talent in 2021-2022 — community managers, writers, designers, operations people, and product managers — but most of the learning resources were code-focused.

The guide likely covers: resources for learning blockchain fundamentals without coding (Decrypt, Bankless, various YouTube channels), entry paths via DAO contribution (many DAOs onboard contributors without requiring coding skills), content creation as a portfolio building strategy (writing about web3 builds public proof of knowledge), Discord communities where opportunities surface, and certifications from platforms like Coursera or blockchain certification bodies.

The 6 months framing is aspirational but not absurd — the web3 hiring market in late 2021 was hot enough that community managers and operations leads with genuine understanding of the space were in short supply. Companies and DAOs were explicitly hiring people with web3 native mindset and communication skills over people with traditional industry experience.

The context: late 2021, near peak bull market. Job boards like Cryptocurrency Jobs, Proof of Work job board, and Bored Town were full of non-technical roles. By 2023 the picture had changed significantly following the bear market and industry contraction.

## Key points

- Non-engineering blockchain roles: community management, operations, writing, product, design — high demand in 2021
- DAO contribution as career entry: unpaid bounty work → reputation → paid contributor roles
- Learning path: fundamentals (what is blockchain/DeFi/NFT) → specialized content → Discord community participation → job search
- Content creation as proof-of-work: writing or video explaining web3 concepts builds a portfolio and public presence
- Context-dependent: the 2021 hiring market was unusually hot; this path got harder post-bear market
