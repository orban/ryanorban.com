## Summary

Anthropic was founded in April 2021 by Dario Amodei, Daniela Amodei, and several other former OpenAI researchers including Tom Brown (lead on GPT-3), Jared Kaplan (co-author of the scaling laws paper), Sam McCandlish, and others. When this job postings page was saved in April 2022, the company was exactly one year old and had raised a $124M Series A, but had not yet released any public products — Claude wouldn't launch until March 2023.

The early Anthropic research agenda, articulated in public writing from this period, centered on AI safety and [Constitutional AI](/notes/constitutional-ai/) — methods for making large language models reliably helpful, harmless, and honest through a combination of RLHF and explicit constitutional principles rather than just human preference labeling. The company's founding thesis was that safety research required building capable systems to study, and that commercial viability was necessary to fund that research, creating the safety-focused AI lab model.

The job postings in this period were for a small, highly technical team — ML researchers, safety researchers, and infrastructure engineers. The company was working on internal models (the precursor to Claude) and had released the [Constitutional AI](/notes/constitutional-ai/) paper describing their approach. Saving the Anthropic job postings in April 2022 represents a moment just before the company entered public consciousness — before ChatGPT changed the industry, before Claude launched, before Anthropic became a household name in AI circles.

## Key points

- Anthropic founded April 2021 by Dario Amodei, Daniela Amodei, and ex-OpenAI researchers.
- April 2022 state: ~1 year old, $124M Series A raised, no public product yet (Claude launched March 2023).
- Research focus: AI safety, [Constitutional AI](/notes/constitutional-ai/), RLHF — the helpful, harmless, honest framing.
- Key early papers: scaling laws (Jared Kaplan), [Constitutional AI](/notes/constitutional-ai/) methodology.
- Historical snapshot: pre-ChatGPT (November 2022), pre-Claude, pre-public AI race awareness.
- The safety lab that builds model: commercial viability as means to fund safety research — distinct from purely academic safety work.
