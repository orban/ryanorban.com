## Summary

Formfunction is a Solana-based NFT marketplace focused on 1/1 editions — unique, single digital artworks rather than the mass-generated PFP collections (10,000 algorithmically combined profile pictures) that dominated NFT activity on Ethereum in 2021-2022. The platform positioned itself as an artist-first marketplace: lower fees, a cleaner presentation focused on individual works, and a community of serious digital artists and collectors rather than traders.

The Solana choice was partly environmental and partly economic. Ethereum gas fees in early 2022 (before the Merge and EIP-1559 matured) made minting and buying individual cheap artworks impractical — a $50 artwork with $30 in gas fees doesn't work. Solana's near-zero transaction costs made the 1/1 art model viable at lower price points. The environmental argument was also relevant: Solana's proof-of-stake design consumes orders of magnitude less energy than Ethereum's proof-of-work at the time.

Formfunction occupied a specific niche in the NFT marketplace landscape: not trying to be OpenSea (general marketplace, high volume, speculative trading) but rather competing with platforms like Foundation and SuperRare on Ethereum, which also emphasized curated, artist-focused 1/1 sales. The Solana ecosystem had [Magic Eden](/notes/magic-eden/) for general NFT trading but lacked a quality art-focused platform — Formfunction filled that gap. The broader 1/1 art NFT category attracted artists who were genuinely interested in digital art as a new medium for direct patronage.

## Key points

- Solana 1/1 NFT marketplace — unique single editions from individual artists, not generative PFP collections.
- Low transaction costs: Solana made small-value 1/1 art economically viable — impossible with Ethereum gas fees.
- Environmental positioning: Solana PoS vs. Ethereum PoW (pre-Merge) as a selling point to environmentally conscious artists.
- Artist-first design: curation, presentation, direct artist-to-collector relationship.
- Solana equivalent of Foundation / SuperRare on Ethereum — competes in the curated art NFT tier.
- 2022 context: 1/1 digital art NFTs were a real artistic practice alongside the speculative PFP trading frenzy.
