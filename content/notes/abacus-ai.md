## Summary

Abacus.AI is an enterprise ML platform that enables companies to build and deploy production AI/ML systems without building large internal ML engineering teams. The platform provides real-time feature stores, automated model training, and deployment infrastructure — a full-stack AI platform rather than individual MLOps point tools.

The company was founded by Bindu Reddy and Arvind Sundararajan, both with backgrounds at Google in ML infrastructure. Abacus.AI raised significant funding and targeted enterprise customers who needed AI capabilities but couldn't compete for top ML engineering talent. The platform spans use cases from recommender systems to NLP to time-series forecasting.
