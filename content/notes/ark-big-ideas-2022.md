## Summary

ARK Invest's annual Big Ideas report is the firm's flagship research document, synthesizing their views on disruptive technology investment across five convergent platforms: genomics, robotics, energy storage, artificial intelligence, and blockchain. Led by Cathie Wood and the ARK research team, the 2022 edition extends themes from prior years while updating the cost-curve projections that drive ARK's conviction — the core methodological claim being that Wright's Law (costs fall predictably as production cumulates) applies to all five platforms simultaneously.

The genomics section centers on multi-cancer early detection and the falling cost of whole-genome sequencing, projecting that population-scale sequencing becomes economically viable. The robotics thesis converges on autonomous vehicles and industrial automation, projecting dramatic unit-cost declines from battery improvement and software learning curves. The energy storage case is driven by lithium-ion cost declines enabling both grid storage and EV adoption at scale. The AI section covers the productivity multipliers from deep learning applied across industries, with compound economic impact estimates. The blockchain / Bitcoin thesis treats digital wallets as the on-ramp to global financial inclusion for the unbanked.

As investment research, this report is important context for understanding where capital was flowing into deep tech in 2022. ARK's reports generate substantial market commentary and often serve as a high-level map of what technologists and investors believed were the five-to-ten year structural tailwinds. Comparing these 2022 predictions against 2026 outcomes is instructive — large language models and generative AI were notably underweighted relative to what actually played out.

## Key points

- Five platform thesis: genomics, robotics, energy storage, AI, blockchain — all claimed to be on exponential improvement curves simultaneously.
- Wright's Law is the underlying analytical framework: every doubling of cumulative production reduces costs by a fixed percentage.
- Multi-cancer early detection and low-cost sequencing projected as a near-term genomics breakthrough.
- Bitcoin and digital wallets framed as financial infrastructure for the global unbanked — ARK's persistent crypto thesis.
- In hindsight, generative AI / large language models are conspicuously absent as a standalone platform category in the 2022 edition.
