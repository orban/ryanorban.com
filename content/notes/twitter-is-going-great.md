## Summary

[Twitter is Going Great](/notes/twitter-is-going-great/) is a site cataloging the rolling chaos at Twitter after Elon Musk's acquisition in October 2022. It's explicitly modeled on Molly White's Web3 is Going Just Great — a chronological log of incidents, failures, policy reversals, and dramatic moments, presented with dry timestamps and brief commentary. The format makes the cumulative scale of dysfunction visible in a way that individual news articles can't.

The acquisition was one of the more turbulent corporate events in tech history: Musk fired roughly half the company in the first week, eliminated major trust and safety teams, reversed account bans including Donald Trump's, introduced and then walked back paid verification, and presided over advertiser departures as major brands paused spending. The site launched specifically to track these events as they accumulated, since the pace of developments made any individual story feel isolated.

The meta-commentary the site implicitly makes: Twitter's problems weren't random, they followed predictably from decisions made at the top. Aggregated chronologically, the pattern is legible in a way it isn't when events are reported individually. Web3 is Going Just Great made this format effective for crypto hype and fraud; [Twitter is Going Great](/notes/twitter-is-going-great/) applied it to a major tech company's self-destruction in real time.

## Key points

- Timestamped catalog of Twitter's dysfunction under Elon Musk — layoffs, service outages, policy reversals, advertiser departures.
- Modeled directly on Web3 is Going Just Great by Molly White.
- Started October 2022 at acquisition; the format makes cumulative pattern legible.
- Tracks not just failures but the decision-making context behind them.
- Demonstrates the value of the running timeline format for ongoing institutional dysfunction.
